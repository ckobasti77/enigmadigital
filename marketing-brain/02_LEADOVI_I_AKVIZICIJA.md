## Definicije: lead, engaged lead, oglašavanje

**Lead** = "somebody that you can contact". [`w7g08dVTwaE`]
**Engaged lead** = "someone who you can contact comma who has shown interest in the thing that you sell." [`w7g08dVTwaE`]

Iz *$100M Leads*, preciznija formulacija: lead = **"information that you can leverage to create a transaction."** YouTube subscriber **NIJE** lead; IG follower **jeste** (možeš pokrenuti razgovor). [`WrCt0R3FBFs`]

**Definicija oglašavanja:** prevesti **unengaged lead** (osoba koju možeš kontaktirati) u **engaged lead** (osoba koju možeš kontaktirati i koja je pokazala interesovanje). [`DaRXece2ItE`, `HsQeQM1jUeg`]

## Core Four

Jedina četiri načina da bilo ko sazna za tebe, kao **2×2 matrica**: *jedan-na-jedan vs. jedan-prema-mnogima* × *ljudi koje znaš vs. ljudi koje ne znaš*. [`reisEL_D7xc`, `9unucIBuNio`, `HxEQCHpZzHk`, `DaRXece2ItE`, `HsQeQM1jUeg`]

1. **Warm outreach** — 1-na-1, ljudi koje znaš
2. **Cold outreach** — 1-na-1, ljudi koje ne znaš
3. **Content (posting free content)** — 1-prema-mnogima, ljudi koje znaš
4. **Paid ads** — 1-prema-mnogima, ljudi koje ne znaš

"If you're not spending your day doing one of those core four things, you are not advertising." [`reisEL_D7xc`, `9unucIBuNio`]

U `9unucIBuNio` Core Four je preformulisan kao četiri načina da napraviš **asocijaciju** (branding).

**Pravilo: ne radiš sva četiri odjednom. Izabereš jedan, ideš all in.** [`HxEQCHpZzHk`] Kako izgleda "more" u Core Four: outreach 100 → 200 dnevno; ads $100 → $200 dnevno; content 1× → 2× dnevno.

**Core Four se koristi dvaput:** prvo da regrutuješ affiliatee, pa onda te iste kanale učiš affiliatee da koriste za tebe. *"You tell people about your stuff and then other people tell people about your stuff. That's the game."* [`DaRXece2ItE`, `HsQeQM1jUeg`]

**Core Four važi i za regrutovanje:** outreach · ads · content · recruiter. Za senior role ads i content ne rade — ostaje outreach. [`JE2_7elAcxM`]

**4 sata dnevno na promociju, pre svega ostalog.** [`reisEL_D7xc`, `HxEQCHpZzHk`] Na Gym Conu je pitao publiku vlasnika teretana ($500k–$3M godišnje) ko provodi prva 4 sata dana radeći cold outreach, sadržaj ili oglase — **nijedna ruka**. "This is why you're small." Prva 4 sata svakog dana idu isključivo na problem "niko ne zna da postojiš". Ispod $1M prihoda: *"basically everyone on Earth doesn't know you exist."*

**"Boom" = Business Order Of Magnitude change.** [`HxEQCHpZzHk`] Optimizacije su capped na 100% (close rate +10%, opt-in +10%). Advertising nije: *"with advertising you can, in a very real way, 100x the amount of people who find out about your business."* Zamka: **"optimization mouse trap"** — *"Dude, just get 100 times the leads, and your business will grow."*

## Rule of 100

Hormozi kaže da je sam skovao termin. **Po osobi, svaki dan: ili 100 outreach-a dnevno, ili $100 dnevno na oglase.** [`UR9dO1jZemE`] "You can add zeros, you can remove zeros, whatever, but at a base level — and this is per person, per human being that you have that's working — if you are not doing this then you're not going to grow."

Puna lista varijanti: **100 hladnih poziva / 100 mejlova / 100 DM-ova / $100 ad spenda dnevno**; ako je organski content — **100 nedeljno**. [`WrCt0R3FBFs`] *"If you do 100 primary actions a day to promote your business or make your products and services known you will get more business"* → *"Your problem will not be demand generation, it will be satisfying the demand."*

Alternativna formulacija po kanalu: 100 warm outreach kontakata, ILI 100 cold prospekata, ILI **100 minuta pravljenja sadržaja**, ILI $100/dan na oglase (uz 100 minuta rada na samim oglasima). [`spXH1kJ6q-Q`, `HsQeQM1jUeg`, `Z2tgZC_XkT4`]

**Trajanje:**
- **90 dana bez prekida** [`UR9dO1jZemE`]: "Do that first and keep doing it — not one day, do it every day for 90 days, and I guarantee you will have customers by the end of that process."
- **100 outreach-a / 4 sata dnevno / 100 dana zaredom** [`spXH1kJ6q-Q`]: *"way before day 100, they're like, holy cow, my whole business has changed."*
- Skaliranje volumena: 10 danas → 20 sutra → 30 → 50 → 100. "Set a kitchen timer."

**Konverzija ~1%** i kod oglasa (klik) i kod outbounda (odgovor). [`UR9dO1jZemE`] "Paid ads is fundamentally outbound; you're displaying stuff, it's just that you're paying a platform to have the eyeball rather than doing the manual work to get the eyeball."

**Input:output aritmetika** [`spXH1kJ6q-Q`, `HsQeQM1jUeg`, `Z2tgZC_XkT4`]: *"if you do 10 reach outs a day then you might get your one sale every 10 days... 100 inputs equals one output."* Volatilnost prihoda = premalo inputa dnevno. *"Businesses themselves are machines for consistency. If you have consistent inputs you have consistent outputs."*

**"What presents as volatility is typically a symptom of low volume."** [`WrCt0R3FBFs`]

**Volume → skill → veći volume** [`UR9dO1jZemE`]: "How do you think you become the best paid advertiser of all time? Volume. So the volume begets the skill — but as you get better at something you like doing it more, so this is actually a circle that continues to reinforce itself." Zamka: "everyone wants to have a huge skill and do low volume to get their outcome, but the reality is that to generate the skill you have to have the volume."

**"Volume negates luck."** [`Jmkq5RLjm0U`, `StVqS0jD7Ls`, `dMZ-n2KSlxE`]

**Konkretni volumeni iz drugih primera:**
- **100 poruka dnevno** u warm outreach playbooku [`w7g08dVTwaE`].
- **1.000 outreach akcija dnevno** u reverse-math primeru za $1M/god, pri odnosu **50 outreach = 1 zakazan termin** [`BSwJQsogah0`].
- Regrutovanje senior kadra: "kontaktiraj 100–1.000 ljudi, ne 5–10", "hire one to hire ten" [`JE2_7elAcxM`].

**Primer premalog volumena:** čovek koji je za 8 nedelja uradio **100 reach-outa umesto 56.000** (7 dana × 8 nedelja × 100). [`WrCt0R3FBFs`]

**Flyer priča** [`WrCt0R3FBFs`, `HxEQCHpZzHk`]: on je testirao sa **300 flajera**; mentor: *"I don't even test for less than 5,000 flyers... and then we do 5,000 a day once we have a winner"* → 5.000 × 30 dana = **150.000 mesečno**, ~20× po danu.

**Formula za potreban volumen:** uzmi celu godišnju reklamnu aktivnost i sabij je u jedan dan. *"If we wanted to get 12 customers a day, we would have to do 365 times more advertising than we currently do."* / *"The people who are crushing it literally are doing a thousand times more than you."* Poenta: *"it's not these 2x 3x increases in volume, it's typically multiple orders of magnitude."* [`WrCt0R3FBFs`]

**Skaliranje kroz ljude uz prihvatanje pada kvaliteta** [`UR9dO1jZemE`]: "They're not going to replicate the same result — they'll probably do it half as well or a third as well. But if you can still get 30-to-1 or 10-to-1 returns on the reach out efforts, then you can stand to have a 50% as effective person, but have ten of them doing it." I: "the people you hire aren't going to be better than you at these things, but 10 of them will be."

**"Take a short step back to go forward"** — prelazak sa "ja prodajem" na "neko drugi prodaje" privremeno smanjuje rezultat, ali skida usko grlo. [`UR9dO1jZemE`]

**Prihvatljiv ROI na outreach: 10:1 do 30:1.** Break-even u početku je prihvatljiv ishod: "You're going to suck in the beginning, and you hope you can break even; and then over time the skill will improve." [`UR9dO1jZemE`]

Tipično ponašanje koje ne radi: outbound jednom nedeljno kad ima motivacije; ili jednokratnih $100 na oglase umesto $100 dnevno. [`UR9dO1jZemE`]

## Rule of 300

Razrada Rule of 100 za warm outreach, **2–4 h dnevno**. [`spXH1kJ6q-Q`, `HsQeQM1jUeg`, `Z2tgZC_XkT4`]

1. **Objavi 1 post** na 1 platformi.
2. **Napiši 100 komentara**, po mogućstvu u grupama/zajednicama gde su tvoji idealni kupci. Logika: u grupi se ne takmičiš sa svima nego samo sa članovima koji komentarišu — promišljen komentar te izdvaja jer su ostali "cool stuff"/"awesome".
3. **100 one-on-one poruka novoj "mlakoj" listi** — 4 izvora: ko je lajkovao tvoj post, ko je komentarisao tvoj post, ko je lajkovao tvoj komentar, ko je odgovorio na tvoj komentar. Svi su preduzeli AKCIJU = prešli iz stranca u engaged lead.

## Hijerarhija akvizicije — 4 poluge pre novog tržišta

[`9gVdCR7W8o8`]

1. **METHOD** — plaćene reklame / content / outreach / referrali / affiliates.
2. **PLATFORM** — Facebook, telefon, email, Hulu, YouTube, Google Ads.
3. **MEDIA unutar platforme** — na Instagramu: DM-ovi, stories, postovi, reels. **Različiti mediji su različiti kanali.**
4. **VOLUME** — koliko toga radiš u tom mediju.

"These are the things you can do immediately to get more customers, get more leads today" — i većina nije iscrpela **nijednu**.

**Redosled skaliranja:** prvo **volume** u postojećem mediju → pa **novi media tip** na istoj platformi → pa **nova platforma** → pa **novi metod** → i tek onda novo tržište.

**Dijagnoza plateaua:** firma sa **$3M prihoda / $1M profita** troši **$50k mesečno na Facebook oglase**, ne može više bez pada profitabilnosti, zaključuje da je tržište zasićeno i hoće nove linije proizvoda. "That is a mistake... they need to confront the problem ahead, which is they don't know how to get more customers within their current niche." Teza: **"Your market isn't capped, your mindset is capped."**

**Merenje veličine tržišta preko konkurencije:** "if there are a hundred other agencies that market to chiropractors and they're all marketing on Facebook, then you could add up all of the ad spend of every one of those agencies together, and that gives you an idea of how much there is available." Isti argument u `reisEL_D7xc`: chiropractor agencija radi $2M/god, misli da je zasitila Facebook u **industriji od $10 milijardi**; troši **$40k mesečno** dok sve agencije u niši zajedno troše **~$4M mesečno**.

**Kad su 4 poluge stvarno iscrpljene — Gym Launch dokaz:** u SAD postoji **~50.000 mikro-teretana**; razgovarali su sa **20.000** (40% tržišta), iscrpeli cold call, cold email, cold DM, Google, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram ads i organski content. **Tek tada** su otvorili health clubs.

**"The should-do list":** "I know I should be calling my leads faster, I know I should be following up better, I know I should be making more content, I know I should start a cold call thing, I know I should be running ads on more platforms — but you're not." Rast je u izvršenju te liste, ne u novoj ideji.

## 6 akvizicionih kanala i zašto je referral drugačiji

[`neTSqOAMgao`]

**Kanali:** paid media (ads), owned media (tvoja lista), earned media (platforme koje te distribuiraju organski), + tri "people-based": **manual outbound, affiliates, referrals/word-of-mouth**.

**Pet od šest je linearno** (fiksni odnos ulog:izlaz, opadajući na skali). **Samo referral/word-of-mouth je kvadratno = eksponencijalno.** Najteže se postiže, ali jedino zaslužuje disproporcionalno ulaganje vremena.

**Referral test pitanje (najjača dijagnostika):** "If I were the god of marketing and I said: right now you can get no more customers from any acquisition methods you're using — no affiliates, no paid ads, no reach outs. The only way you can get new customers is if your existing customers bring you more customers. **How different would the experience you have with your customers look if that was the only way you could get new clients?** ...Then change that now, and then they will come."

## Warm outreach: prvih 5 klijenata u 10 koraka

[`w7g08dVTwaE`]

1. **Napravi 3 liste:** svi email kontakti; svi social profili kojima možeš DM; eksportovani kontakti sa telefona. "If you add all three of those together you will have a shitload more leads than you thought you did."
2. **Izaberi jednu platformu** — onu gde imaš najviše ljudi.
3. **Personalizuj** — 30 sekundi gledanja profila. "This is also just called being a human being."
4. **Reach out — 100 ljudi svaki dan.**
5. Ako odgovore: **ACA — Acknowledge, Compliment, Ask.** Ask mora biti vezan za kompliment i voditi ka usluzi.
6. **Traži da ti predstave prijatelje, ne prodaješ njima.** "You're not asking them to buy from you. This should make you not feel weird about messaging people."
7. **Ponudi besplatno** uz 3 uslova (koriste, daju feedback, ostave recenziju).
8. **Vrati se na vrh liste** i prođi ponovo.
9. **Počni da naplaćuješ kad ljudi počnu da te preporučuju** — "that's when you know you were good enough to start charging."
10. **Keep them warm** — javljaj se listi, isporučuj vrednost, postuj testimonijale; na kraju te oni kontaktiraju.

**Brojke:** **3.000 kontakata** → "if you reach out to 3,000 people with a free thing you'll get five people who will take you up on it, I promise you" (≈0,17%). Uzmi **max 5 besplatnih klijenata** — "you have no idea what you're doing so you better not take more than five customers."

**Free klijenti donose posao na 3 načina:** recenzije, preporuke, konverzija u plaćajuće.

**Hidden costs model:** ako neko neće ni besplatno, razlog su skriveni troškovi (vreme, trud, rizik, nezgodnost). Eliminiši ih i naplaćuj značajno više od konkurencije koja ih prebacuje na klijenta. Primer ponude koju je Hormozi prihvatio: "I want to do short form videos for you, you don't have to do anything… you actually just need to say yes."

## Četiri faze do prvih 10+ klijenata (bez budžeta)

[`HsQeQM1jUeg`]

1. **Homies** — pozovi ljude iz svog telefona, radi besplatno u zamenu za rezultate/before-after. Njegov broj: **6 ljudi, 6 meseci.**
2. **Referrals** od tih ljudi, tek nakon rezultata: **6–10 dodatnih.**
3. **Free-with-skin-in-the-game** — "Free Training Project": klijent ne plaća tebe nego donira **$500–1.000** humanitarnoj organizaciji.
4. **Paid** — prevedi iste ljude na plaćanje: **20 klijenata × $200/mes = $4.000/mes**, 12 nedelja → prvih **~$12.000**.

Ukupno preko godinu dana pre prvog content posta. Prvi klijent uopšte: **$100 ček**, razgovor od 1,5 h u pizzeriji.

**Napomena o grešci:** faza besplatnog rada bez uloga je propala — *"because it was free none of them really did anything."*

**Proof over promise:** ako biraš između sjajne ponude bez dokaza i loše ponude sa 100 dokaza — dokazi pobeđuju.

**Ne-monetarna kompenzacija:** testimonijal, feedback ili preporuka su često vredniji od cene koju bi naplatio.

## Cold outreach / outbound mašina

[`okA9Yt2KZuk`]

**Prag ulaznice:** *"if you want to do outbound, your ticket has to be at least 15,000"* ($15k+ po dealu; njihov najjeftiniji je $20k, LTV $40k).

**Pipeline:** scrape liste sa Google-a → VA-ovi na Filipinima "enrich"-uju podatke (verifikuju da su realni vlasnici, da brojevi i mejlovi rade) → BDR zove kroz **PhoneBurner** → SDS value call → live handoff → BC close call.

**Brojke:**
- **BDR kvota: 100 poziva/dan u 4 sata rada.** Zašto 4h a ne 8h: *"if you give them eight hours of pay, they'll do five and a half hours of calls... so you might as well do four hours of pay and get three and a half."* Bonus: omogućava "moonlight" uz drugi posao.
- **Funnel: 100 dials → 20 pickups (20%) → 2 zakazana koja se POJAVE.** BDR se kompenzuje **samo za showed appointment**. Nov BDR: 1/dan; "savage": 5/dan.
- **Close rate 10% od showed SDS appointmenata. CAC: $50 po showu × 10 showova = $500 po klijentu**, pri LTV $40.000.
- **PhoneBurner obavezno sa local area code wrapping** — *"If you don't have that feature there's no point in using it... or your pickup rate's going to be [dead]."*
- **75% outbound rezultata dolazi sa telefona**, ne mejla/SMS-a.
- **BDR ramp: 2 dana** ("Here's the list. Here's the phone. Say these words."). Pozivi 5–10 min.
- **Ramp krivulja outbound-a po mesecima (broj prodaja): 1 → 2 → 4 → 6-8 → 12 → 16 → ... 45/mesec u 14. mesecu.** Rast je **linearan, ne eksponencijalan** (*"you'll notice that this isn't quadratic"*). U 14. mesecu outbound je ~60% ukupnog volumena.
- Drugi slučaj [`HxEQCHpZzHk`]: novi outbound kanal — **4 meseca, 1 prodaja**, exec tim mu radio "intervenciju"; leading indikatori su ipak napredovali.
- **Recruiting BDR-ova: NE screenuj previše.** *"Just let them all in and we'll see how they do because it takes two days to ramp them up and we don't really have any cost per lead."*

**Odvoji outbound i inbound timove** — i settere i closere. [`JE2_7elAcxM`]

**Omiljena voicemail/email skripta** [`StVqS0jD7Ls`]: "Hey [business], I'm calling about [competitor]. Give me a call back."

**Double dial** — prvi poziv blokira screening, drugi prolazi. [`JE2_7elAcxM`]

**Timing prodajnih poziva uz društveni dokaz** [`okA9Yt2KZuk`]: gomilaj prodajne sastanke **tokom** klijentskih događaja/rezultata — *"for the next couple weeks, your closing percentage is going to be sky-high."*

## Outreach kanali — konkretni brojevi i taktike

[`zzleYxkf39k`, `Z2tgZC_XkT4`]

- **IG DM limit: 10–20 dnevno.** Ako te zaprate nazad — DM neograničeno. Dve varijante za test: (a) zaprati sve → DM samo onima koji uzvrate; (b) hladan DM 10–20 dnevno nezavisno od follow-backa.
- **Email → "absolutely zero conversion"** u tom slučaju. **SMS radi. Follow + DM na Instagramu ima najveću konverziju** od svih outbound kanala.
- **SMS:** koristi **pravi SMS sa lokalnim area code-om**; testiraj **iMessage "blue bubble"** (skuplje, ali testiraj response rate).
- **AI-personalizovan cold outreach (workflow):** lista (12.000 agenata) → AI agent pretražuje svakog online → Excel sa personalizovanim poljima → template kroz AI generiše prvu poruku → čovek brzo pregleda batch od 100 → pošalji. A/B protiv generičkog opener-a. Dodatna korist: *"It'll also make it less likely that you get put into spam — because if the carrier sees you send that same thing to 100 people, they spam the number."*
- **Multi-kanalni outreach:** isti čovek ima 1.000 DM-ova na Instagramu i 10 poruka na LinkedInu — idi tamo gde ćeš iskočiti. Uključi i **direct mail**: *"you can't unsubscribe from direct mail."*
- **Profil pre outreach-a ("don't be Rakesh, be Jim"):** plati verifikaciju; bio mora eksplicitno reći šta tražiš; profilna slika = **lice iz blizine, svetlo i vedro**; postavi nekoliko postova koji pokazuju ko si. Verifikacija naloga (~$10/mes) = **5–10× veći response rate** [`Z2tgZC_XkT4`, `oRqnTOT9ZG8`].
- **Deli storije/postove ciljanog čoveka PRE nego što mu se javiš.** Kad uđe u DM i vidi 15 share-ova, oseća dug pre nego što ste ijednu reč razmenili.
- **Pokloni:** ekstravagantan, personalizovan, sa vidljivim trudom + rukom pisana kartica; **stavi NJIHOV logo na poklon, ne svoj.**
- **Mockups + grafikoni "ovo si ti sad / ovo si ti sa mnom"** pre prvog razgovora — over-preparation = profit.

## Lead magneti — definicija i teorija

**Definicija:** "A lead magnet is a **complete solution to a narrowly defined problem**." NIJE parcijalno rešenje. [`7NMH1oAkgLY`, `ovL6Z5z0jxQ`]
Anti-obrazac: "hey I'm gonna give you the first six minutes of this 12 minute video, get the rest — click here" → gubi se poverenje.

**Doslovna definicija iz knjige (str. 31)** [`Mst4hreQYl0`, `OpeN4O5myIg`, `eX3Ch_HuR70`]: *"A complete solution to a narrow problem — typically lower cost or free — just to see who's interested and raises their hand. Once that little mini offer solves it, it reveals another problem that's solved by your core offer."*

**Zašto radi:** kupovina je **predikcija buduće vrednosti**; najlakši način da neko predvidi vrednost posle kupovine je da mu je daš **pre** kupovine. "The lead magnet is the thing before the thing." [`7NMH1oAkgLY`]
Princip: *"When someone pays with time now, they're more likely to pay with money later."* [`Mst4hreQYl0`]

**Core offer vs. lead magnet:** core offer = ono što donosi NAJVIŠE novca. Ako lead magnet počne da donosi najviše novca — on JE postao core offer. [`7NMH1oAkgLY`]

**Ključno pravilo dizajna:** lead magnet **ne sme da rešava problem koji rešava tvoj core proizvod** — mora da rešava problem čije rešenje **stvara deprivaciju** za core proizvod. [`Mst4hreQYl0`]

**Point of greatest deprivation:** *"You want to sell at the point of greatest deprivation... the moment after you give them the water is no indication of whether your water was good — they're just not thirsty anymore."* Restoran-analogija: posle glavnog jela prodaješ dezert, ne još jedno glavno jelo — a **u biznisu dezert mora biti znatno skuplji od glavnog jela.** [`Mst4hreQYl0`]

**Hard cost je uslov** [`ovL6Z5z0jxQ`, `7NMH1oAkgLY`]: "there has to be some sort of hard cost that people can actually realize... otherwise you don't have reciprocity. We want to jam value into someone else's wallet so that they then have to transfer it back to us in the form of money later." I: "Sure you can have a free PDF, sure you can have a digital video series... but normally, unless it's exceptional, people aren't going to value that. Sometimes giving away actual free work that you will incur cost on — a lot more people will take you up on."

**Backend je uslov** [`ovL6Z5z0jxQ`]: "the only way you can do a giveaway long-term is that you have to have some sort of product you know you're going to sell on the back end."

**Dijagnostičko pravilo** [`Mst4hreQYl0`]: *"If a lead magnet doesn't work, it doesn't mean lead magnets don't work — it just means that that lead magnet didn't work. Just like a headline for an ad."*

**Zašto besplatno ipak ne prolazi:** *"The price of free is still not worth it"* — cena je vreme i pažnja. [`WrCt0R3FBFs`]

**[protivrečno] — koliko uopšte ulagati u lead magnet:**
- `okA9Yt2KZuk`: *"I don't think like lead magnets and stuff like that is valuable. It's got to be something that people really want in your space."* Ako je neskalabilno — radi neskalabilno, pa sistematizuj.
- `fSbqaTlWaYI`: *"Really put time into it, because people skimp on this — that's why people opt in, see it, and think 'why would I continue to book with this person?'"*
- Razrešenje koje izvori nude: nije bitno "lead magnet kao forma", nego da li je stvar koju daješ ono što ljudi u tvom prostoru zaista žele i bolja od plaćenih verzija konkurencije.

## Lead magneti — tri tipa

[`7NMH1oAkgLY`, `ovL6Z5z0jxQ`, `Mst4hreQYl0`, `OpeN4O5myIg`, `eX3Ch_HuR70`]

**1. Reveal a problem** (njegov favorit) — besplatna procena/assessment. Otkriješ problem koji nisu znali da imaju ili im ga kvantifikuješ. Bonus: pokaži i kako izgleda rešeno + **deltu**. Najbolje radi za probleme koji se **pogoršavaju dok čekaju**: posture analiza, inspekcija na termite, finansijski audit, brzina sajta.
Primer: knjiga *Gym Launch Secrets* (400 strana: trainer ads, salesman ads, treninzi, skripte, uređenje lobija, upsell proces, cenovni paketi, semi-private, veće grupe) — "it shows all the things you need to do and then people are like, 'Oh my god, this is so overwhelming... it'd be nice if I had help.'"

**2. Free trial / sample** — za problem-aware kupce ("they know their back hurts"); rešiš im, ali se problem vraća ako ne nastave da plaćaju. Costco teriyaki: daš rešenje pa ga **oduzmeš** — deprivacija je mehanizam konverzije. Ograničavaš broj korišćenja, količinu, vreme, ili kombinaciju.
Primer: **Six Week Challenge**.

**3. One step in a multi-step process** — rešiš prvi korak koji nužno otvara sledeće (prvi sloj boje, prva od 6–8 sesija lasera, prva 2 videa kursa, paleta boja pre kompletnog stila).
Primer: naučiš ljude da prodaju suplemente (S3 sistem) → sledeći problem je "koje suplemente" → **Prestige Labs**.

## Lead magneti — konstrukcija i isporuka

**Micro-step dekompozicija** [`7NMH1oAkgLY`]: svaka isporuka ima mnogo mikro-koraka (krečenje kuće: procena → estimate → dizajn → 3D vizualizacija). "Can I pick one of these very painful steps for my prospect and solve it for them at a massive discount?" Što je stvar veća, više koraka → više kandidata. Okvir: **"before / during / after"** — mapiraj sve što se mora desiti kupcu pre, tokom i posle. Logika: "Solve problem 1 and 2 completely, which naturally leads to problem 3 — that's my paid thing"; "when you solve problems you create more problems".

**7-koračni checklist za pravljenje lead magneta** [`7NMH1oAkgLY`]:
1. Odredi problem koji rešavaš i **KOME** — od "ko" zavisi ime, pristup, marketing, pakovanje i isporuka.
2. Odredi **KAKO** ga rešavaš (reveal / trial / sample / kombinacija).
3. Odredi **način isporuke** — informacija, usluga ili fizički proizvod. Pitanje: "which of these is going to be perceived as the highest value from the prospect and increase the likelihood that they ultimately buy?"
4. **Pakovanje i ime** — "if there's ever a most important step, it's this one: how you name it and how you package it."
5. Napravi ga **brzim i lakim za konzumiranje** — audio, pisano, hardback, digital reader, video.
6. **Napravi ga jebeno dobrim.**
7. **Olakšaj sledeći korak** — jasan CTA, u više formata.

**ČETIRI NAČINA ISPORUKE** [`Mst4hreQYl0`]: (1) **software/tools** — kalkulatori, assessment alati, dinamični šabloni; (2) **information** — mini kursevi, vodiči, statični šabloni ("infinitely scalable, tremendous value, no operational drag"); (3) **services** — besplatan audit sa delom implementacije, done-for-you komponenta; (4) **physical** — knjige, majice, kape. Kombinuju se.

**Free stuff mora biti bolje od konkurentskog plaćenog** [`WrCt0R3FBFs`]: *"What if I make my free stuff better than their paid stuff?"* — checklists, cheat sheets, vizuali, in-depth treninzi.

**Kvalifikacija protiv freebie seekera** [`Mst4hreQYl0`]: drop-down pitanje pre isporuke; za free services daj samo onima koji unapred potvrde **budžet, autoritet, potrebu i spremnost da deluju odmah**.

**Good friction vs. bad friction** [`eX3Ch_HuR70`]: acquisition.com roadmap forma ima **8 koraka i to namerno**. *"conversion rate of whom? Of people who really want to buy the thing — they probably will finish it."* Kviz radi na **sunk cost + consistency bias**, pa daj i "cookie" (kod za popust) odmah po prijavi.

**Lokalni lead magnet — cena** [`fSbqaTlWaYI`]: besplatno ili **$19–29** za usluge gde je vreme skupo (akupunktura). Cilj nije profit nego **kartica u sistemu**: "Do you want to use the card you have on file?"

**Case-study lead magnet format** (nekretnine) [`fSbqaTlWaYI`]: 5–10 reprezentativnih primera različitih avatara + **FAQ sa najstrašnijim pitanjima na dnu** + dokaz (koliko objekata, koliko dugo) + personalizovana procena kao CTA.

## Lead magneti — brojke i primeri

**Matematika lead magneta — masaža** [`7NMH1oAkgLY`]: 100 ljudi × ponuda masaže za $100 → možda **1 kupac**. Istih 100 ljudi × masaža za $10 → **20 kupaca**; od tih 20, **~30% (6)** kupi $100 stvar posle. **1 vs. 6 kupaca.** Ako masaža košta $10 da se isporuči → break-even; ako košta $20 → **$200 uloženo → 6 kupaca vrednih $2.000**. Pravilo: *"Even if your lead magnet costs you money to deliver, it should still lower your total cost to get a new customer."*

**Konverzija sa/bez lead magneta** [`Mst4hreQYl0`, `eX3Ch_HuR70`]: od 100 posetilaca "buy my thing" daje ~1 kupca; "here's a free thing, then I'll ask" daje **3–5× više** kupaca — bez ijednog dodatnog dinara na saobraćaj. (`eX3Ch_HuR70` daje istu cifru kao **3×**.)

**Free-service CAC math:** hard cost 1 sat rada $25; tržišna vrednost $250+; konverzija 1 od 4 → **CAC = $100.** [`Mst4hreQYl0`]

**Physical/qualified lead magnet:** majica ili kapa za CEO-a $3–4; konverzija 1 od 20 → **$60–80 za kvalifikovanog CEO leada.** [`Mst4hreQYl0`]

**Split-test knjige "$100M Leads":** **6 različitih naslova** + slika korica + podnaslovi. *"If I'm going to spend two years writing the book, I can spend two days testing the headline."* [`Mst4hreQYl0`]

## Paid ads — funnel, targeting i friction

[`fSbqaTlWaYI`, `FMzKk73iUhw`, `HwPXctiw_rY`, `m5ordaa7NN4`, `sGv2BTUCcCM`, `HxEQCHpZzHk`]

- **Standardni lead funnel:** oglas → besplatan lead magnet (opt-in) → thank-you page sa schedulerom → poziv.
- **Landing page, ne homepage** [`sGv2BTUCcCM`]: "Get their information, then give them the thing they want, and then they can go peck around the site." Razlog: "people have very large motivation for very short periods of time when they see marketing."
- **Friction dial:** *"What's the perfect funnel? If you get too many bad leads, add friction. If you don't get enough leads, remove friction. And if you have almost no friction and the leads are still bad, the targeting is off."* Dobar friction = VSL, aplikacija, plaćanje. Loš friction = spora stranica. *"The more filters you add, the more expensive the leads will be — if they're GOOD filters, they'll also be better."*
- **Targeting je prvo, sve ostalo drugo** [`HxEQCHpZzHk`]: *"So many smaller businesses think that marketing doesn't work, when in reality they were showing it to the wrong people."* Osnove: lista kupaca → lookalike (**1.000+ mejlova** dovoljno). Ako u copy-ju piše "dentist", Meta AI to prepozna.
- **Shadow funnel:** globalni retargeting preko SVIH platformi (GDN, IG, FB, YouTube) na svakoga ko je posetio sajt. *"It almost always is positive ROAS."*
- **PPC na sopstvene brend-termine:** *"There's no PPC campaign for your own terms that doesn't make money. I haven't seen it ever happen in my entire life."*
- **Interruption vs. intent:** PPC/Google = intent (viši kvalitet leada). Meta = interruption (jeftinije, šire); vredi kad je prosečan tiket visok.
- **Local vs. national:** lokalni biznis je najlakši za oglašavanje jer je poverenje već visoko; oglašavanje je **offer-driven**, brend gotovo nebitan. Nacionalni/online = brend i value-first su presudni.

## Lead quality — 5 poluga i kada ih koristiti

[`Kk6Vdfdwmxw`]

**Trade-off:** "Each of these will increase quality but typically decrease volume." — "it's not to say that you should do all five of these things, because sometimes you're going to be leaving a lot of money on the table."

1. **Targeting** — zip kod, nivo prihoda, profesija. Upozorenje: "if you're doing those [rich] zip codes, understand that other people are also marketing to those zip codes, they will probably be more expensive, and your volume will be lower."
2. **Broj koraka do tebe** — "if someone has to do 10 steps to get on the phone with you versus one step, they're going to be more qualified because they **took more action**."
3. **Priroda ponude** — free vs. paid; giveaway vs. "click here to schedule a sales call". **"Not all leads are created equal."** Giveaway = ogroman volumen ali zahteva "a good mousetrap behind it".
4. **Aplikacija** — "you can have four pages in a row that only require one checkbox, or one page with a lot of questions." Ključno je **tip pitanja**: "lots of paragraph question answers is going to be more work and therefore a higher quality lead compared to lots of checkboxes and A-B-C type questions."
   - **Aplikacija je pre-frame za prodaju, ne samo filter:** navedi ih da izgovore svoj cilj, da **priznaju da imaju problem**, da otkriju "what their biggest unknowns are and the things they're struggling with", i postavi presedan da će biti troška.
   - **Minimum budžeta:** "if you want to and have big balls, which I would encourage you to do, put some sort of minimum amount. If your thing's a thousand bucks, say: **'Are you willing to spend at least $500 on this?'**"
5. **Provide value first** — kvalitet samog oglasa/videa. "A video that's incredibly compelling but doesn't explain anything or add value might get you lots of leads, but the quality won't be as high, versus a video that might be **five minutes long** and provides a tremendous amount of value, tactical importance they can immediately implement." → autoritet pre poziva.

**Njegov default: najširi mogući levak, pa rešavaj na back-endu.** "I try and start with the biggest widest net as possible and then work backwards. **I just want to get flow.** Once I get flow, then I'm going to create a series of offers and upsells on that flow." — "**the worst thing in the world is not having any flow to begin with.**"

**Kada JESTE vreme za kvalifikatore** (dva uslova): (1) toliki volumen sa toliko lošim kvalitetom da si "willing to sacrifice half or a third of the throughput"; (2) **operativni kapacitet** — "if my team can't handle as many and I don't mind doubling my lead cost to put a fence up... then it would be worth it to double my lead cost."

**"Put the onus on the model, not on the marketing."** "Most times you just need to look **behind the lead** at the monetization system that they're coming into, to make sure that **you can afford the traffic**, rather than trying to tweak the front end. **It's usually the model itself.**"

**[protivrečno]** `oTQPxPFROck` traži suprotan default: kvalifikacija i odbijanje kao osnov legitimnosti — "**If the only requirement to become a customer of yours is a credit card and a pulse — red flag number one**", prag odbijanja "you would probably not accept 90% of people". `Kk6Vdfdwmxw` traži najširi levak i kvalifikatore tek kad postoji zagušenje.

## Referrals — zašto i koliko vrede

**Zašto referrals (4 razloga)** [`qpQvdBFW_yI`, `cy2k1GdA-9o`, `B-ogfFiQpXg`]: veći close rate, plaćaju više, kupuju češće/duže, i **najverovatnije će sami dalje preporučiti**. *"You get three spins on the money-making wheel from having referrals versus a cold customer."*

**Brojke:**
- Samo **1 od 3–4 klijenta** da referal, ali **referali zatvaraju 80–90%**. [`JE2_7elAcxM`, `cy2k1GdA-9o`]
- Rep koji je samo pitao "who else would benefit" digao je prodaju **+50%** (bio ~50% iznad drugoplasiranog). Pitanje mora biti **"who"**, ne "do you" — mora da odgovori imenom, i pakuje se u sendvič od komplimenta. [`JE2_7elAcxM`, `cy2k1GdA-9o`]
- Sa close rate-a **1/4 ideš na 2/5**. Ako **1 od 3 klijenta referiše, CAC padne na trećinu**; **jedan referral po klijentu → CAC prepolovljen.** [`cy2k1GdA-9o`]
- **Referrals vrede 2–3× više od hladnih kupaca** [`sPkMHh8zTMI`]: *"if you should pay for any lead the most, it should be referrals."*
- **Cilj: 30% biznisa iz referrala.** [`qpQvdBFW_yI`]

**Referral = trust transfer** [`JfbtySq6_B4`]: preporučen lead dolazi sa "sky high trust" — *"you just need to get out of their way so they can make the purchase."*

**Golden Ratio of business (passive growth rate)** [`IFpHasZ7jN4`] = % kupaca koji mesečno referišu novog ÷ % kupaca koji mesečno odu. 10%/10% → biznis stoji. **20%/10% → biznis se duplira svaki interval, zauvek.** Radi isto za fizičke proizvode, software i servise.
Ista mehanika [`qpQvdBFW_yI`]: 1.000 klijenata, 10% mesečni churn → treba ti 100 novih za nulu. Ako 20% preporuči na 10% churn → **rasteš 2:1 zauvek**, bez obzira na veličinu. Referrals skaliraju bez degradacije: *"30% on 100 million or 30% on 100 grand is still going to be 30%."*

**"Pour gas on the flame" pravilo** [`IFpHasZ7jN4`]: ne skaliraj marketing dok referral % ne pređe churn %. Većina firmi ima **~2% referrals / ~10% churn** i zato mora večno da puni levak plaćenim saobraćajem — *"they don't understand how to build a good product."*

**Word of mouth kao kanal** [`4twK8Yl4iUI`]: "The highest percentage of sales across any market come from word of mouth." Što je veći ulog kupovine, to su preporuke važnije. Word of mouth je **jedini compounding vehicle koji prati rastući CAC** [`qsXxckCbci0`] i meri se kao (a) % prodaja od povratnih kupaca, (b) % kupaca koji dovedu nove.

**Anti-referrals** [`afbP6sB_Atc`]: *"Everyone wants to believe that they're good enough to get referrals, but no one believes that they're bad enough to get anti-referrals."*

## Referrals — 7+1 taktika sa okidačima

[`qpQvdBFW_yI`, `cy2k1GdA-9o`, `B-ogfFiQpXg`]

1. **"Who else do you know…"** — okidač: odmah posle zatvaranja svake prodaje. Ugradi u SOP i u ugovor (prazna polja za imena preporuka ispod potpisa). Prodavac koji je to radio: isti close rate, ali **30–50% više prodaja**; ~1 od 3 klijenta pošalje **1–2 imena**.
2. **Gift card play** — okidač: praznik (ili izmišljen: rođendan, godišnjica firme). Vrednost = **jedna osnovna jedinica usluge** ($200 = mesec teretane; $2.000 za SEO agenciju), **popust 90%+** (klijent plaća $20 za bon od $200), **limit 2 po klijentu** (namerno — da svako dovede dva), izdaju se **na IME** (tako hvataš lead), **rok 30 dana**. Klijent PLAĆA da ti dovede leadove. Računica: **200 klijenata → 30 kupi po 2 = 60 kartica → ~50 uđe.**
3. **Popust u zamenu za 3 predstavljanja** — okidač: klijent traži popust. Predstavljanja se rade ODMAH, uživo.
4. **Popust → veći bonus (marža arbitraža)** — $200 popusta te košta $200 keša; $400 usluge te košta $80 (pri 20% cost of delivery). Veća percipirana vrednost za manji stvarni trošak.
5. **Cash po preporuci** — na proizvodu od $500 davao je **$100 keša u čeku**. Jedna medicinska sestra dovela **10 ljudi**. **30% svih prodaja u svakoj promociji dolazilo je iz ove jedne taktike.** Plaća se tek pošto preporučeni kupi.
6. **Referral u trenutku uspeha** + zajednička fotografija + **three-way tekst sa telefona klijenta**.
7. **Rukom pisana pozivnica za kvartalni event** sa PS-om o plus-one. **30–50% prodaje na eventu** bili su "plus one"-ovi. Na eventu **ne prodaješ — samo zakazuješ follow-up sastanke**, i to blizu ("Utorak? Tu si sad — hajde u ponedeljak").
**Bonus 8: "bring a friend" u SVAKOJ poruci kampanje.** Prijatelj koji vodi challenge-e sa milion+ klijenata ponavljao je to u svakom postu → **25–30% klijenata iz word-of-mouth/neatribuiranih izvora**; kad to nije radio — skoro nula.

**Dodatni mehanizmi:**
- Rukom pisana kartica + gift kartica za dovođenje prijatelja [`y3T4GqoH0Us`].
- Handwritten card sa **PS: "take a picture of this card and text it to a friend in a three-way, and that way your friend is invited too"** [`sPkMHh8zTMI`].
- Three-way intro kroz gift card strategiju [`mHrAjWni65E`].

**BAM FAM — Book A Meeting From A Meeting** [`B-ogfFiQpXg`, `cy2k1GdA-9o`]: svaki susret završava zakazivanjem sledećeg + pitanjem koga dovodiš. *"Now we're not asking for referrals. We're just saying 'who do you want to bring?' It's just much easier."* Nikad "let's circle back offline". **90 dana** je "wow moment" tačka gde se traži preporuka.

**Referral offer > referral discount** [`B-ogfFiQpXg`]: novčani kredit ($500) bogatom klijentu ne znači ništa. Daj **stvarnu uslugu u vrednosti $1.000–2.000 koju klijent POKLANJA prijatelju** — to je *"social capital positive rather than neutral"*. Marginalni trošak je nizak; nudi se **samo za referrale, nikad kao ad**.

## Affiliates — definicija i 4 koraka

**Definicija** [`DaRXece2ItE`, `oRqnTOT9ZG8`, `eX3Ch_HuR70`]: *"An affiliate is a lead getter... an independent business that tells their audience to buy your stuff."*
**Razlika od referrala:** (1) imaju sopstveni biznis i sopstveno oglašavanje; (2) nude tvoje stvari svojim engaged leadovima u zamenu za novac, besplatne stvari, ili oboje.

**Ista stvar, različita imena po industrijama:** promoter / influencer advertising / UGC / affiliates / referrals / centers of influence (COI, finansije) / joint ventures. *"I want to make sure that for every type or genre, I can speak their language."*

**Dva kupca** [`60_7PU9JDIw`]: affiliate avatar i customer avatar. "You have to deliver to two types of people rather than one." **Dva nivoa ponude:** ponuda afilijatu i ponuda koju afilijat pravi svojoj publici u tvoje ime.

**4 koraka mašine** [`DaRXece2ItE`]: (1) **offer za affiliatea**, (2) **mesto gde se saznaje** (affiliate sales page), (3) **promocija tog mesta**, (4) **follow-up/support**. *"You got to make it worth it for them. You got to let them know about it. And you got to make it easy."* Ako ijedan korak fali — ne radi.

**Affiliate playbook 5 koraka** [`eX3Ch_HuR70`]: nađi idealne affiliate → napravi im ponudu → kvalifikuj ih → plati ih → **reci im tačno šta da objave i kojim tempom**. *"affiliates basically become another tier of customer — you have to make an offer so good they feel stupid saying no."*

**WIN-WIN-WIN pravilo (najvažnija ideja)** [`60_7PU9JDIw`, `DaRXece2ItE`]: "you have to win, the affiliate has to win, and most importantly, **their audience has to win**." Plaćanje afilijata usklađuje samo tvoj i njegov interes — ne i interes publike. *"they're like 'I got to save my goodwill for my own stuff'... How can we make the thing that we're going to give them something that's a give to their audience?"*
Konstrukcija nagrade tako da dobija **publika afilijata**: "click my link so that our community can have the prize" umesto "help me win my contest, top 10 get supercars."

**Missionaries, not mercenaries** [`60_7PU9JDIw`]: "mercenaries will just go to the next highest bidder" → "race to the top in terms of how much you're willing to pay."

**Volume vs. Quality dial** [`60_7PU9JDIw`]: "If you want volume, you make it easier. If you want quality, you make it harder." Paid afilijati nose papirologiju (1099) — trenje koje je istovremeno pozitivan filter.

**Zašto free stuff umesto para** [`DaRXece2ItE`]: *"Paperwork-wise, it's an absolute pain in the ass to pay a gazillion people with 1099s... sometimes the non-monetary value can be significantly higher."*

**Anti-affiliate realnost** [`FMzKk73iUhw`]: *"I spent all this effort, all this time to build trust, advertise, sell, create a relationship — and I'm just going to hand them to you for 20% of your ticket? What do I care?"* Pravilo: *"If you're going to have affiliates, do it like you mean it. It has to be incentivizing enough to change their behavior, or it's useless."*

**"Peel the corner" model** [`FMzKk73iUhw`]: ne nudiš 20% provizije — **odsečeš jedan modul svog proizvoda i daš partneru 100% prihoda** od njega, uz obavezan **MINIMUM cene**. Ako konvertuješ 1 od 3 u proizvod od $2.000: **CAC = $270 za kupca od $2.000.** *"There's something mental about 'you don't split it with me, just take all the money.' It's a very different vibe."*

**Affiliate umesto sopstvenog softvera** [`pw1PcHfYP2w`]: ako šalješ ozbiljan saobraćaj, možeš ispregovarati **20–30%, ponekad 40–50%** provizije, bez troška razvoja i održavanja.
