## 1. Temelji: tri kategorije sadržaja i anti-funnel teza

### Tri kategorije definisane po ISHODU, ne po formi
`Jmkq5RLjm0U, HVOubeXUcx0, JOfsujnXxCg`

- **Entertainment** — "the only point is to get people to watch. If it accomplishes that, it has succeeded."
- **Education** — "the point of education is to get someone to change what they do." Uspeh = promena ponašanja. Definicija koju koristi kroz ceo materijal: **"Learning means same condition, new behavior."** I: *"I define education as change of behavior."*
- **Edutainment** — pokušaj oba istovremeno.

Kategoriju ne bira autor nego publika: **"You don't control the audience — you make something and then education or entertainment occurs."** Kategoriju definiše NAJČEŠĆI ishod kod publike.

Svrha edukacije, argument koji koristi: **"You don't put your kid in school so that they act the same."**

Ko je edukator: **"If you sell services to anyone, you're likely going to be an educator, not an entertainer."** `dMZ-n2KSlxE`

Granica medija-biznisa i edukacije (`ZTSI3DDP_4A`): "if you're doing memes or entertainment there's nothing wrong with that, it's just that **you're a media business and eventually you're going to sell eyeballs to an advertiser.** But if you want to sell your own stuff... **education is the door.** That is how you gain influence, how you gain authority."

### ANTI-FUNNEL TEZA — najkontraintuitivniji nalaz
`Jmkq5RLjm0U, HVOubeXUcx0, lEIqyLE4iOY`

Preovlađujući model kaže: entertainment → education → kupci. Njegovi podaci to obaraju:

> **"Entertainment people want more entertainment content and education people want more education content. These people do not become these people — not at any appreciable rate that we would build a business around."**

Isto važi na tri nivoa:
- **po tipu sadržaja** — zabavljena publika ne prelazi u edukovanu;
- **po temi** — "people who consume relationship stuff just want more relationship stuff";
- **po formatu** — "People have consumption preferences by platform... if you only watch YouTube shorts you're not going to become a Longs viewer." Shorts i longs se tretiraju kao **odvojeni koloseci**.

**Jedini priznati izuzetak: cross-platform.** "Someone finds you on TikTok but they are a Longs viewer on YouTube." Shorts na TikToku → longs na YouTubeu.

Zašto entertainment nalozi uvek izgledaju veći: **"Everyone gets value from entertainment. But there is no one thing from an education perspective that can provide value to everyone, because everyone already knows different stuff."** Praktična posledica: **uči principe od zabavljača, ali NE kopiraj njihove taktike.**

### Content JE targeting — "social media is now interest media"
`dMZ-n2KSlxE`

**"The content is the targeting."** Algoritam čita o čemu pričaš, šta nosiš, kakva ti je pozadina, i servira te ljudima sa istorijom gledanja te teme. **"If you want to attract the right avatar, make content for that avatar."**

- **Grandma primer protiv views-a:** "If a grandma in public comes and just slaps me across the face, that video will probably get views. But does it get me any more people who now believe more in my stuff? No." Varijanta (`HsQeQM1jUeg`): "if you go up to strangers on the street and slap them and run away you're going to get a ton of views... but you're not going to get the right types of views from the right WHO — unless you sell how to slap strangers in public."
- **Klavir primer:** "If I have a room of a thousand people and all of them are only interested in fixing pianos — that's a hell of an opportunity."
- **Ko je u kadru određuje kome algoritam servira** (`HxEQCHpZzHk`): "One of the easiest ways to make sure that you're displaying your ads to the right avatar is make sure that the person in the ad looks like the avatar." Case: thank-you page sa njegovom slikom → slab response; ista stranica sa **Leilinom** slikom i "Leila's going to be the one contacting you" → *"our response rates went through the roof."*

### Media kao input, ne output
`hHkdbr6_JJs, VPre_XMgKjs, 4pZwlE86A5Y`

*"This is where we put our media... This is what we create every day. We put money, we put effort, and we put team into this."* Sadržaj je kapitalna investicija sa timom, ne marketinški dodatak.

- **"Your product IS your marketing"** — jedinstvena prednost medijskih firmi: *"the more you spend on your product, which is your media, the more marketing ends up happening."*
- **Media je jedini biznis gde je marketing ujedno i isporuka** (`4pZwlE86A5Y`): *"How you market the thing that you have is also how you fulfill that thing. That's only a media thing — no other business is like that."*
- **Email lista kao proizvod, ne kao lista** (Mozi Minute — "in some ways a product in and of itself").
- **Knjiga kao konverzioni mehanizam** — $30 proizvod čija je funkcija da uhvati kupca i kvalifikuje ga, ne izvor prihoda.
- **Content-to-case-study petlja:** ljudi iz advisory practice-a prave case studies koji idu u content; content prodaje advisory practice u kome se sreću ljudi iz tih case studies ("meeting Mickey"). Zatvoren krug.
- **Content kao ventil za višak ideja** (`rc7cxL7ql7Y`): ima ~1 novu ideju dnevno, firma izvršava ~1 po kvartalu; višak ide u sadržaj. *"you guys are the product of a creative outlet for me."*
- **Procena vrednosti njegovog društvenog prisustva: ~$1 milijarda.** `lEIqyLE4iOY`

### Trust economy / show-me times
`ZTSI3DDP_4A`

- "This is the year of the trust economy... **we're in the show-me times, not the tell-me times.**"
- **"The proof in this trust economy world is not in the pudding — it IS the fucking pudding."**
- **Precizna specifikacija case-study sadržaja:** "you need to **demonstrate expertise in as many varied conditions as possible**, with the avatars that most broadly represent the types of customers you serve, **solving the specific problems that they have**, in the way that you want to solve them, that gets them the outcome they're looking for. **That is content that drives commerce. That is views that turn into cash.**"
- **Neoborivi prigovor koji publika ima na tebe:** "Tell me how you're going to grow my e-commerce store, except you've never run an e-commerce store. Tell me how you're going to teach me how to sell, except you've never sold anything. Tell me how you're going to help me make content, but you don't have a personal brand."
- **Imposter syndrome = stid zbog laganja.** "Imposter syndrome is you feeling ashamed for lying — because you are misrepresenting the truth." Lek nije mindset nego istina.
- **Dva uslova da neko sledi tvoje instrukcije:** (1) **comprehension** — "the instructions have to be easy to follow, which means you've got to talk in words that other people understand"; (2) **conviction** — "they have to believe that you are worth following... increase conviction that they're going to get the payoff, which makes the risk of taking those actions worth it."
- **Granice autoriteta:** pitan da li da priča o sreći — "**No. I don't have authority there.** 'You're trying to figure out how to scale your business?' That's me. 'How to sell shit?' That's me. **I've got a lot of proof there.**"
- **Kako izgraditi lični brend:** "**Just do shit.** You will get a bigger personal brand by doing shit in reality for 5 years and then starting your personal brand."

### Creator Risk Continuum — koga AI prvo pojede
`XsWSvz-aewA`

Osa po tome koliki rizik se traži od potrošača sadržaja; AI disruptuje niski kraj prvi:
1. **Entertainers** (najniži rizik) — "Entertainment is one thing: the objective of the content is to be consumed."
2. **B2C educator** (hair/makeup, dijeta, trening) — nizak ulog, kredibilitet se demonstrira uživo (before/after).
3. **B2 "prosumer"** (novac, štednja, investiranje) — veći ulog, više dokaza: Erika Kullberg (advokat), Vivian Tu (Wall Street), Dave Ramsey (uspeh + dugovečnost).
4. **B2B creator** (najviši rizik) — tu svrstava sebe; AI će najteže replicirati dokaz.

**Dve vrste dokaza:** *Proof* (third-party/postignuće: exits, prihod, headcount) — kažeš jednom; *Demonstration* (u realnom vremenu) — "incredibly difficult to fake." Pitanje koje postavlja: **"How can I do demonstration at scale? How can I provide proof at scale in real time?"**

Test kredibiliteta: "One person that just GPT'd 'here's six things you need to do to scale your sales team,' never sold anything in their life… versus somebody who's built 10 sales teams… Assuming the information was literally identical, the person who has the most credibility wins — and not just by a little bit, by a lot." / "Why is Elon the number one business influencer in the world? Because he's the richest man in the world."

Zašto dokaz uopšte radi: **"We take it as a signal to decrease our own personal risk."**

### SPCL — uticaj umesto pregleda
`dMZ-n2KSlxE, Jmkq5RLjm0U`

**Influence = "high likelihood of compliance with requests."**

- **S — Status:** "someone who controls reinforcers in a given environment." Barmen u punom baru ima status; isti čovek na ulici nema.
- **P — Power:** iz **say-do correspondence** — "if I say something and then you do it and then a good thing occurs, you are more likely to comply with a following request." Ako bi birao samo jedno od četiri, bira POWER.
- **C — Credibility:** treća strana, **nešto što se vidi očima**. Platio je Guinness sudije da budu na licu mesta pri lansiranju; snima ispred svoje zgrade od $10M — "that's hard to fake."
- **L — Likeness:** psihološka (vrednosti, vibe) ili doslovno fizička sličnost. Podatak: njegova publika **89% muška**, Leilina **~54% ženska** uz gotovo identičnu temu — dokaz da je likeness moćnija od teme.

Sve četiri rade nezavisno ali se **stack-uju**; kontinuumi, ne binarno ("think not in binaries, yes or no, but think in continuums"). Uticaj je **domenski specifičan**. Roditelji su maksimalni SPCL: kontrolišu sve oskudne resurse (status), hiljade reinforcement ciklusa (power), delimičan credibility, i doslovna likeness.

**Struktura intro-a treba da čekira sva četiri:** "32.7 million views" i "$100M in 72 hours" = STATUS; "I'm going to break down four things, and if you follow these things…" = POWER; Guinness sudije = CREDIBILITY; ostajanje svoj = LIKENESS.

### Hijerarhija formata i "internet ide ka istini"
`dMZ-n2KSlxE`

Mr. Beast anegdota (aplauz-metar pri izlasku na teren, UK vs US soccer game): A-listeri (klasične zvezde) — skoro bez aplauza → **shorts kreatori** — malo → **long-form (podkasteri, YouTuberi)** — jako → **live streameri** — stadion eksplodira.

**"The internet will always move towards truth"**: A-listeri su kurirani, ispolirani, skriptovani; podkast nije skriptovan; live stream se ne može kontrolisati. Cilj formata: **"approximate the rawest reality of us hanging out."**

**Reinforcement volume argument za long-form:** "How many reinforcing cycles do you think you can have in 30 seconds compared to two hours?" Ako short traje 15s → 4 shorta po minutu → **480 shortova = 2 sata longa.**

Njegova deklarisana buduća strategija u dve reči: **"Live, interactive."**

### Cancel teza
`UGEc9-7X3OQ`

"I actually don't think you can be canceled. You can only be canceled if all means of communication with an audience are removed from you **and you stop making content.** ... **You become un-cancelable as long as you don't accept that you can be canceled.**"

## 2. Šest promena u content strategiji + izmereni rezultati
`Jmkq5RLjm0U, HVOubeXUcx0, JOfsujnXxCg`

30-dnevni eksperiment (rezultati mereni posle 3–4 nedelje).

**1. Edutainment → Education.** Posledice: drugačije pakovanje, drugačiji naslovi, manje specijalnih efekata.

**2. "For us" → "For you."** Pravio je sadržaj za svoj tim umesto za avatar — pitao je tim šta da snima. *"Your team might not be the type of people you're trying to make content for."* Okidač promene: prijatelj sa $10M/god koji je rekao "it just doesn't resonate as much." Pet taktičkih promena: **packaging, introductions, meat, visuals, pre-work**.

- **Packaging: vague → clear.** Thumbnail i naslov moraju tačno reći o čemu je video. "when people click on something they don't know what it's about, they have a smaller percentage of getting something they actually want." Direktan napad na curiosity-gap doktrinu: *"There was a pervasive narrative in the marketing world: you got to have curiosity, you don't want to tell them everything. **No.** If they want that thing, they will click on that thing, and then you just give them that thing. That has been a very hard lesson for me to learn."*
- **Introductions: "Proof, Promise, Plan"** — izvedeno rewatchom **top 35 sopstvenih edukativnih videa** (detaljno u §17).
- **Meat: be-real/vlog → lists, steps, stories.** I mogu se mešati: *"you can have a list of things, have steps within the list, and tell stories about each step. That's fundamentally what content is."*
- **Visuals: overproduction → effective production**; "from distracting to enhancing, from visual effects to visualizing data" — *"instead of having flames behind me, it's: what does this look like on a chart, so we can give relative scale, so we have scope, so we can show changes over time."*
- **Pre-work: post-production → pre-search.** **"An ounce of pre-work is worth a pound of post-work."**

**3. Wide → Narrow.** Odbacio veze, koledž, hranu, lifestyle; ostavio **business models, business leverage, selling in a business** — "notice *business* being in all of these." **Tema je filter publike, ne dekoracija.**

**4. Views → Revenue.** Ad revenue = **views × RPM**; RPM je *quality* metrika uparena sa *quantity* metrikom. Ako platforma plaća više po hiljadu očiju, te oči su vrednije oglašivaču — dakle i tebi. **RPM je direktan način da meriš KVALITET publike bez pristupa CRM-u.** Paired KPIs kao opšti princip (customer service: broj rešenih tiketa × ocena kvaliteta rešavanja; firma za čišćenje: broj čišćenja dnevno × recenzije). *"You want both edges so you can control for both sides."* / **"You can't pay payroll with views."**

**5. Shorts → Longs.** Teza "shorts prave long-form gledaoce" — **nema dokaza da važi.** *"People who watch short form on YouTube watch short form; people who watch long form watch long form."* **Shorts ne ukida** — zadržava isti volumen shortsa, menja im sadržaj da bude biznis-fokusiran; težište prebacuje na long-form jer *"Longs drive a lot more conversions than shorts — conversions being book sales, opt-ins, applications."*

**6. Assume more → Assume nothing.** *"Way more people don't know me than know me."* Prestao sa "Alex Hormozi diet"/"vlog" formatima. Detaljna ček-lista u §17.

### Izmereni rezultati posle 3–4 nedelje
- **RPM +68%**
- **+25% komentara po pregledu**; share-ovi neznatno gore, lajkovi neznatno dole (rangira komentare i share-ove iznad lajkova)
- manje pregleda po pojedinačnom videu ali **ukupni long-form pregledi +29,56%** (~+30%) — jer manje post-produkcije znači više videa
- **konverzija gledalac→pretplatnik +24,6%** (apsolutni broj novih pretplatnika porastao uprkos manjem broju pregleda po videu)
- **opt-in +26% nedeljno**
- **prodaja knjiga 2x** — "twice, not like 20%"
- više aplikacija za portfolio kompanije

**Trade-off koji svesno prihvata:** *"I'm okay with a video that gets 10,000 views instead of 400,000 views if those 10,000 people are $10 million plus business owners."*

### Deep vs. Wide matrica
Neke teme su i **duboke i široke** (leverage, brend) — najviše pregleda I najviši RPM; njegov najgledaniji video je ujedno i najveći po RPM-u. Neke su **duboke a uske** (skaliranje sales tima od 40 ljudi, mega-influenceri, prodaja firme) i to je OK — vredne su malom podskupu.

**Views nisu mera kvaliteta nego mera koliko je tema relevantna za koliko ljudi.**

- **Teme sa visokim RPM-om:** "kako smo smanjili churn", "kako naterati nekoga da kupi više puta", "kako smo skalirali sales tim od 40 ljudi", "kako se brend dekonstruiše i rekonstruiše".
- **Široke teme sa drastično nižim RPM-om:** fitness, odnos sa suprugom, produktivnost.

**Uloga shorts-a je isključivo top-of-funnel brand/face recognition + punjenje retargeting publike.** ROI ne ide shorts→prodaja nego shorts→prepoznavanje lica→kasniji retargeting oglas: *"The ROI is actually not from shorts turning into book sales — it's the shorts getting retargeted later with an ad."* Zato **shorts smeju biti ŠIRI po temi nego long-form.**

## 3. Niša vs. širok sadržaj

### Verifikovani test, izveden više puta
- **90 dana namerno širokog sadržaja** (veze, fitnes, hrana, "advice to my younger self") → **više ukupnih pregleda, ali drastično manje kvalitetnih aplikacija i prodaje knjiga.** `lEIqyLE4iOY`
- **3–6 meseci "razblaženog" sadržaja** (ishrana, brak) → prosečan broj pregleda po videu **PORASTAO**, ali su **site conversion, opt-in, book sales i portfolio applications PALI**. Povratak na 100% biznis → sve te metrike gore iako je average view po videu pao. `kloJJeiysxg, reisEL_D7xc, 9unucIBuNio`
- **90 dana entertainment sadržaja:** views **2–3x gore, ad revenue prepolovljen.** *"We had this thing with 10 million views — okay, but it drove nothing — versus a video with 100,000 views that made more ad revenue than the one with 10 million."* Meseci sa najvišim RPM = meseci sa najviše prodatih knjiga, opt-in-ova i aplikacija — "and this had nothing to do with how many views we got." `HVOubeXUcx0`
- **Ista greška dvaput** (`0S5xsICW8qg`): 18 meseci pre prodaje Gym Launch-a prešao sa "gym" na "general business" content → **organska prodaja pala praktično na nulu**, i trebalo im je **6 meseci** da shvate zašto.

**Izjave koje iz toga sledi:** *"I'm here for the money, not for the fame."* / *"I'm not in this to get famous, I'm in this to make money."* / *"I have no marriage to anything."*

**Interno pravilo: "If it's not business, we don't make it."** Business-adjacent je dozvoljen samo ako su SVI primeri biznis primeri.

### Adjacency lestvica
`lEIqyLE4iOY`
business → personal development / produktivnost / time management = **jedan korak niže, još uvek adjacent**. Veze = predaleko: *"It's too many steps to get the right people, because people who consume relationship stuff just want more relationship stuff."*

**Publika je rasla pogrešnim ljudima:** prijatelj koji radi $10M/god (tačan avatar) rekao mu je *"I don't listen to too much of your stuff anymore... I guess I'm not really your avatar."*

### Ratio širokih vs. niche tema
`UGEc9-7X3OQ`
**3:1 do 4:1 na long-formu** — "one goes a little bit wider and the rest stay on topic; you'll probably get the best of both worlds."

Merenje: "lessons to my younger self" tip sadržaja dobija **~3× više pregleda** od case-study sadržaja, ali kad je pitao punu salu vlasnika biznisa šta konzumiraju — *"the whole room was this side"* (case studies). **Široke teme = top of funnel, ne cilj.**

### Ladder tema (od uske ka širokoj, ne obrnuto)
`MD5-HByRxoA`
kako se popravlja WC → kako se širi vodoinstalaterski biznis → kako se širi lokalni biznis → marketing metod → enterprise → "poslovni ekspert".

**Nišni positioning test:** **"How narrowly can I define the problem that I solve, so that in that tiny world, in that pond, I am king?"** / **"I'm the fastest toilet fixer this side of Tuscaloosa"** — hiper-uska niša gde se takmičiš protiv 3 čoveka, ne protiv Gary Vee-ja.

**Niša prvo, širenje kasnije i tek organski** (`7ITff1fIbSc`): **prvih 150 podcast epizoda isključivo o tome kako bolje voditi teretanu**; tek oko epizode 150 prelazi na marketing, pa prodaju, pa biznis i M&A.

### Credibility test za izbor niše
`0S5xsICW8qg`
Biraj temu gde postoji **objektivna mera uspeha**. Zašto odbacuje relationship content: *"There's no relationship billionaire. The only objective measure of if your marriage is good is that you are not divorced — everybody's been in a relationship so everybody has an opinion, and there's no objective proof that one thing is better than another."*

### TAM matematika — kalibracija očekivanja
`dMZ-n2KSlxE, Jmkq5RLjm0U, JOfsujnXxCg`
Podaci iz US Census-a:
- samo **9%** ljudi ima biznis (~32–33 miliona)
- **95%** od njih je ispod $1M prihoda
- **5%** (≈1,5M) preko $1M
- preko $10M — **4% od tih, tj. 1 na 250**; drugde formulisano kao **~0,1% svih firmi radi $10M+ godišnje**
- preko $100M — **~1 na 3.000**

Zaključci: *"if I get 100,000 views on a video that's really made for that level of business owner, then I'm crushing it."* / *"It's not fair to compare your views against Mr. Beast. We have different games."*

### Reframe malih brojeva
- *"I would probably go across town to speak to a 100-person audience for lunch… 50,000 views? I speak at conferences with 1,000 people. It's 50 times that."* → **50.000 prikaza = 50x konferencijska sala od 1.000 ljudi.**
- *"When you have 10 views on a YouTube video, that's 10 people. If I had 25 people — that's a workshop, that's a lunch and learn."* `0S5xsICW8qg`
- *"The platforms distribute your content for free — the leverage on free distribution is infinite."* `JOfsujnXxCg`
- *"A specific problem being solved for a specific person is how you make big money."*
- **1.000 True Fans** (Kevin Kelly): *"Even at $10 a month — that's $100,000 a year."*
- Bolja metrika od pregleda: **IRL odziv** — *"If I make a video and then I get texts from business owners that I like and respect being like 'Yo, that was fire' — then I'm on the right track."*

### Mali nalozi, veliki novac — dokumentovani case-ovi

### Jedini stabilni princip
`UGEc9-7X3OQ`
> "the long-term strategy of every platform is to **keep people on the platform**. They want content that **lots of people click on**, that **lots of people watch all the way through**, and that ideally makes them **watch something else from you**. ### The Algorithm — 5 koraka (Elonov proces primenjen na sadržaj)
`ZTSI3DDP_4A`
Hormozi ga je učinio obaveznim za sve koji mu direktno odgovaraju. Sadržaj JESTE proizvodnja — *"it's **digital manufacturing**: raw input is recording video, output is posts, views, likes, shares, leads."*

1. **Question the requirements** — "Why must these things happen in this order? Why are we doing all these requirements? Why must it take this long?"
2. **Delete** — *"we should delete so much that we have to add stuff back."* Zašto je bezbedno: *"everyone's afraid of deletion because they're afraid of messing something up. But you can always add stuff back — it's very unlikely you're going through an irreversible door."*
3. **Simplify / optimize** — "what do we get the most for the least?" (uz opportunity cost).
4. **Accelerate** — "how do you pull up timelines?"
5. **Automate** — I TEK ONDA. **"The only thing that is more efficient than automation is elimination.** Choosing not to do something is more efficient than automating it, because it wasn't worth doing to begin with." Zamerka AI eri: *"because access to AI has become so prevalent, everyone thinks the last step first — they try and automate shit that should not be done to begin with."*

**Tri pitanja za rušenje "zahteva": "What does that mean? How do you know that? So what?"** Primer na color gradingu: "We have to color grade." → What does that mean? → "We have to do all this stuff to the video." → How do you know that? / So what? → "Do we have any evidence this does anything? No. Then why should I care?" → **color grading izbačen iz procesa.**

**AI litmus test, tri pitanja:** *"Though I am using AI, are we getting more views? Are we getting more leads? Am I making more money?"* Signal problema: *"if you notice your token costs going up month over month, but your money's not going up month over month, maybe you're just blowing money on shit that doesn't matter."*

## 17. Hooks, naslovi i packaging

### 80/20 pravilo prednjeg dela
`reisEL_D7xc, JDR-R--4HhM, HxEQCHpZzHk`
- **David Ogilvy:** *"After you've written your headline, you spent **80 cents** of your advertising dollar."* Prvih 5 sekundi / hook / thumbnail / naslov je **frame kroz koji se konzumira sve ostalo**.
- **Alokacija truda:** *"If I have an hour, I would spend 55 minutes on the first 5 seconds and 5 minutes on everything after."*
- **"The more advanced the advertiser, the more obsessive they are on the first frame."**
- **Hook radi na sva četiri Core Four kanala**; podizanje open/click/watch stope sa 1% na 5% ili 2% na 10% je **2–5x ceo biznis**.
- **Law of small numbers:** 1% → 3% CTR = **utrostručio si** broj ljudi koji vide sve ostalo. *"There's very little things in the rest of the entire funnel that have the ability to triple... **a banger headline can absolutely outperform the control by 2x, 3x, sometimes 5x.**"*
- **Front vs. back matematika** (`reisEL_D7xc, jzKpAtzKQ54`): show rate sa 50% na 75% = +50%. **CTR sa 1% na 4% = 4x** protok kroz ceo levak. *"There is nothing that's going to take my show rate from 60% to 180%. There's nothing that's going to take my close rate from 35% to 105%."*

### Dokazani case-ovi o snazi hooka
- **Dean / Larry King** (`reisEL_D7xc`): kampanja od **$250.000** propala; vratio Larryja Kinga, ponovo platio, ponovo postavio set **samo da presnimi prvih 30 sekundi** istog sat vremena dugog intervjua → **$100.000.000 u prodaji.**
- **Kreator sa 15M pretplatnika** napravio video sa 20+ strana brifa; **kopija tog videa dobila 30M pregleda vs. njegovih 10M.** Najveći kreator u sobi zaustavio video na **5. sekundi**: *"You didn't confirm the thumb"*, i izašao iz sobe.
- **Headline test na landing stranici** (`reisEL_D7xc`): promena SAMO naslova (isti oglasi), firma sa osmocifrenim prihodom → **+62% booking rate**, dok je **opt-in rate ostao isti**.
- **Brisanje prva 3 sekunde** (`HxEQCHpZzHk`): isti video, obrisan uvod pre pravog hooka → **sa 40.000 na 780.000 pregleda = 19x.** Praktični edit: **odseci sve pre pravog hooka.**
- **Case study preimenovanja knjige** (`7NMH1oAkgLY`): knjiga o dejtingu propala; promenili SAMO korice i naslov (ništa unutra) → prodala **preko 10 miliona primeraka.**

### Hook je odvojiv modul
`fSbqaTlWaYI, HxEQCHpZzHk`
- **Prva 3 sekunde su jedina jedinica koja se testira i preseljava.** Hook nije deo videa — hook je modul.
- **Creative multiplication:** uzmi **top 5–10% oglasa iz poslednje godine**, izoluj prva 3 sekunde i presadi taj hook na **100+ ostalih**. *"That'll get you 10 to 50 times more ad creative per good winner."*
- **Razdvoj vizuelni hook** (šta se vidi) **od verbalnog hooka** (koje reči padaju). Radi oba.
- **Ad = 3 dela: Hook (call-out) → Meat (value) → CTA/payoff.** `HwPXctiw_rY` *"People noticing your ad is the most important part of the ad by a lot."* Cilj hooka: **"oh, this is for me".**

### Proof – Promise – Plan (Promise – Proof – Path)
`Jmkq5RLjm0U, HVOubeXUcx0, UGEc9-7X3OQ, StVqS0jD7Ls, fSbqaTlWaYI, 4GQLJjH9-oA`

Izvedeno **rewatchom top 35 sopstvenih videa.** Format za **prvih 15–20 sekundi / prvih 60 sekundi** svakog sadržaja, webinara ili VSL-a. Eksplicitno kaže da je isto za prodajni poziv i za YouTube: *"humans are still humans, their attention still works the same way."*

- **Proof** = "prove that you know what you're talking about, give people a reason to believe you" / "pomogao sam nekome baš kao ti" — zašto da te slušaju.
- **Promise** = "tell them what they're going to get or learn from the video" — šta tačno dobijaju.
- **Plan / Path** = "sets the expectations of what will happen next" — šta se dešava u narednih 60/90 min.

U `UGEc9-7X3OQ` isti okvir naziva **Promise, Proof, Path**: *"The first two are about **why should I stay**; the path is **what's going to happen**"* (path je za watch time). *(Isti framework, dva imena — njegova sopstvena varijacija, ne protivrečnost.)*

**Redosled je promenljiv — vodi onim što je najjače.** Za biznis/edukativni sadržaj vodi **Proof**, jer publika ima *"this big glaring sign above their head before they can even turn on the volume of their brain, which is: **why should I listen to you?**"*

**Razlika intro-a entertainment vs. education** (princip je isti: "increase perceived likelihood that they will get what they clicked"):
- **Entertainment:** *"say the title again and literally show them what was on the thumbnail"* — "if you smash a Lamborghini, you better have the Lamborghini next to you."
- **Education:** *"show them that you can do what you promised them in the thumbnail"* — *"if you're educating, **the Lamborghini in our situation is proof** — what is the big Vegas lights of why should I listen to you?"*

**Doslovan skeleton dobrog edukativnog intro-a** (`UGEc9-7X3OQ`):
> *"Hey, I sold my company for $46.2 million. A big part of that was that we had a guarantee that beat all of our competitors. And so we did four things that made our guarantee stronger."*
→ proof + promise + path u tri rečenice.

**"Obsess about your first 30 seconds"**; hook se meri na **"past the first 3 seconds"**.

**Dva pitanja koja svaki komad contenta mora da odgovori** (`MD5-HByRxoA`): **"Why should this person listen to me?"** i **"Why is this worth their time?"** Plus: *"If people aren't willing to pay for it with their attention, then it has other costs — it's not worth their attention."*

### 12+1 copy hakova — interni checklist za SVAKI komad oglašavanja
`JDR-R--4HhM`
*"Whether that be in emails for outreach, or writing phone scripts, or video sales letters, or web pages, or making content."*

**1. Headline / hook je prvi jer je najvažniji.**
Pravila: **"curiosity is king, different is ideal, and sexy works, and never run an ad without a headline"** — *"all ads have headlines; there's just bad ones and good ones. If you don't write a headline, you do have a headline — it's just not very good."* Headline je **"different wrapping paper for the same offer"** i mora da **"grab the reader by the throat."**
- **Univerzalna headline formula:** **"How to [yay / good thing] without [boo / bad thing], even if [your biggest fear/insecurity]."** Primer: *"How to speak confidently in front of strangers without ever practicing, even if you're deathly afraid of speaking in public."*

**2. Say what only you can say.** **"Proof will always outdo promise."** *"Competitors can copy your offer, but they cannot copy your proof. That has to be earned."*
- AI ugao: **"It doesn't matter how many AI avatars come out — the AI avatar can never have done something in the real world. They can lie, but they can never have done something. No one can take your story or your experiences away from you."**

**3. Uvek zovi po imenu koga tražiš — i koga NE tražiš.** *"Bonus points: **who you're not looking for**, which is actually even more powerful than the original callout."* Efekat: **polarizacija.** *"When you say no to customers, you polarize the customers who are the right customer to move even more towards you. **What you don't want to be is vanilla.**"* Cilj: self-selekcija — *"Wow, I feel like this person's talking to me."*
- **Nikad ne oglašavaš masi — oglašavaš JEDNOJ osobi.** *"You want them to think: **how does he know my secrets?** You can only be that specific if you're talking about one person."*
- Primer polarizacije: **"Only for people who are trying to lose over 30 lbs."** — *"There's enough people trying to lose over 30 lbs that you can say no to the people who have less than 30 lbs to lose. You might only need a couple hundred customers."*

**4. Uvek imaj "reason why".** Cialdini eksperiment sa preskakanjem reda: čak i besmislen razlog ("my cat is back home") diže pristanak. **"The word 'because' is one of the most influential words in the English language. Use it because it works."**
- Fraternity logika: *"The bar for having a reason to party is quite low. Timmy got his wisdom teeth out — kegger. It's Tuesday, let's drink."* → rođendan, rođendan psa, godišnjica firme — *"there's four dates that you can run for a whole month."*
- **Svaka linija copy-a prodaje čitanje sledeće linije.** *"Those lines of copy sell the click. The click sells the landing page, the headline of that page sells the opt-in, the opt-in sells showing up to the next thing."* Prodaješ **jedan korak**, ne devet. **"A confused mind does not buy, and a confused mind certainly does not click."**

**5. Damaging admissions — njegov favorit.** (Puna razrada u §21.) *"There is nothing more powerful than damaging admissions."* / *"Own your flaws. Of course your product isn't perfect. Say so. **What you'll sacrifice in promise, you'll gain in trust. And trust trumps promise.**"*

**6. Show, don't tell = opiši MOMENAT, ne rezultat.** *"Instead of saying 'get more sales', describe what the experience of getting more sales means."* Primeri: *"you're running out of order forms because customers are reading their credit cards to you over the phone too fast"*; *"the overwhelm of having too many phones ring and not enough salespeople."*
- **Opisuj buduće probleme koje bi RADIJE imali** nego sadašnje — *"arguably some of the most compelling things you can include in copy."*
- Bonus: to je izvor originalnosti — *"the way you describe that moment will always be unique to you."*

**7. Veži benefite za STATUS.** Primer: *"a cookbook so fast and easy that **all your friends will wonder how you found the time** to be fit and cook for your family."*
- **What / Who / When framework** ($100M Leads, str. 140): *šta* dobija, *ko* mu daje status (porodica, prijatelji, kolege, rivali, konkurenti — "who are they vanquishing?"), *kada* (past / present / future). *"So it gives you **endless variations and angles**."*
- Status je subkulturno specifičan: mami = "kids that excel and other parents wonder how they're parenting"; 20-godišnjem hustle bro = Lamborghini. *"We have to meet them where they're at."*

**8. Urgency ≠ scarcity** — mora biti legitimno, inače gubiš moć. **Scarcity koja je istinita, ne izmišljena** (`qsXxckCbci0`): *"space is limited based on our actual headquarters."*

**9. Implied authority.** Longevity ("we've been in business for 40 years"), **agregacija** ("in aggregate we've done over 20 years of advertising" — čak i ako su četvorica po 5 godina), klijenti-poznata imena, brojevi ("we've helped 5,000 or 6,000 gyms over the last seven years").
- **Sitne nagrade i liste vrede zbog razgovora koji otvaraju:** "As seen on Oprah", "best boot camp in Vegas" → otvaraš prodajni razgovor sa **"Did you find us on the Vegas best boot camps list?"** — *"you might know that no one even knows that list exists, and that's okay, because that list gives you implied authority when you have the conversation."*
- **Yelp logika social proofa:** **5.0 sa 3 recenzije vs. 4.6 sa 4.000 recenzija** → svi biraju 4.6. *"The proof demonstrates the implied authority."* → **dokumentuj i supstanciraj sve što si uradio.**

**10. Uvek imaj P.S. ("power sentence").** **"The two most read parts of advertising: number one, the headline; number two, the P.S. statement."** **P.P.S. je treći najčitaniji red.** Tri strategije:
- U emailovima: P.S. kao **nagrada za čitanje** (šala, nešto lako) — da bi, kad povremeno staviš CTA u P.S., ljudi već bili istrenirani da klikću tu.
- U oglasu: **disklejmer koga ovo NIJE za.**
- **Rekapitulacija svega u jednoj rečenici.**

**11. Kristalno jasan CTA + "say-do correspondence".** *"If you do not tell someone exactly what to do next, you decrease the likelihood that they do it."* Struktura: *"If you want X, click the button. On the next page, fill in your information, then select a convenient time for you to come in."*
- **Mehanizam sticanja uticaja:** kažeš šta će se desiti → oni preduzmu akciju → to se stvarno desi → dobijaš uticaj. **"Make promises, keep promises, make promises, keep promises"** — ključ je da **oni moraju preduzeti akciju**. Martha Stewart model: 18 koraka → torta → pohvale porodice (status) → sledeći put te slušaju.
- Ekstremna primena: **"The reason I called my whole company acquisition.com was so that the name of my company was the call to action."**

**12. Treći razred osnovne škole — nivo čitanja.** (Puna razrada u §22.) Pretpostavka o čitaocu: **"the prospect is in a rush, doesn't care about what you have, has below a third grade reading level, and is distracted."**

**13. BONUS: humor.** *"If you can entertain and educate at the same time, you win. **Entertainment makes something significantly broader. Education is what's going to change someone's behavior. Entertainment to go broad, education to go deep.**"* Old Spice = najkonvertujući oglas ikada po njegovom saznanju. Ali: **"If I had to pick one thing to cut out, it would be humor"** — ostalih 12 su dovoljni; humor je "extra sizzle".

### PODACI KAO HOOK — "Three Wheels"
`0BZ7-PZR8jE`
Svaki hook se pravi kombinovanjem tri promenljive iz sopstvenih podataka o klijentima:

1. **Outcome** — 4 načina prikaza: procentualni porast; apsolutni porast ("dodatnih $15.500"); ukupan broj; **relativni porast** (šta taj iznos *kupuje* — "otplati 9 meseci kirije").
2. **Time duration** — prva nedelja, prvih 14 dana, 30 dana, 8 nedelja, "one year later", "two years later"; ILI **inverzija**: "svakih X dana/sati postignemo Y".
3. **Percentage of clients** — procenat, razlomak ("one in 5.5"), "top X%", "bottom X%", medijana, prosek, i **inverzija** iste statistike ("one in five" ↔ "four out of five").

**Konkretni primeri i zašto:**
- **"One in 5.5 gyms is a million dollar a year run rate"** — svesno izabrano umesto "18%": *"18% ne zvuči tako ubedljivo kao one in 5.5. Like really, one in five, that's not that bad."*
- **"One in every 7.7 days we create another seven-figure gym"** — izvedeno iz **~50-i-nešto seven-figure teretana godišnje / 52 nedelje** *(u izvoru: „like 50 something… I honestly can't remember the number" — ne otvrdnjavati u tačnu cifru)*. Isti podatak, drastično jači format.
- **"Our average gym in the first two weeks: $15,500 additional revenue"** → odmah se dodaje *"that means half of people get better than that."*
- Relativni prikaz istog: uz kiriju od **$2.000/mesec** → *"in the first 30 days the average gym in our program pays off nine months of rent."*
- **Pomeranje praga:** "70% success rate of people hitting over 12 pounds lost in the first six weeks" — na **30 lbs** procenat pada na **~10%**, pa se koristi format **"the top 10% of our clients hit X"**.
- Kontrola: *"30% increase doesn't sound as good as $15,500"* — **biraj format broja koji zvuči najveće, ali ostani tačan.**
- **"One Year Later" kampanja je njegova najuspešnija ikad:** uzmi sve klijente starije od godinu dana, izmeri metrike pre i sada, pokaži razliku. Duge periode koriste dvostruko: (a) ishodi su najbolji jer su preživeli klijenti, (b) dokaz da klijenti **ostaju** → "social proof of continued value creation" i dokaz starosti firme koji noviji konkurenti ne mogu da pokažu.
- Podatak je **hook, ne dodatak dokazu**: *"I will hunt for the piece of data that tells the story that the copy can't tell."*
- Igraj i na **survivorship bias** ("one in five — možda sam ja taj") i na **inverziju** ("4.5 out of 5.5 hit this") — ista statistika, dve poluge; **testiraj obe.**
- **Compliance:** *"You have to be absolutely compliant when you use numbers"*; konkurentska prednost je baš to što ti trackuješ — *"most people don't even capture their own data anyway... it's usually because it's not good."*

**[protivrečno / korekcija]** `hnfh2jqNVEQ`: prestali su da objavljuju outliere ("100k za 6 nedelja") i prešli na **komunikaciju proseka** (~$16.000 u prvih šest nedelja), plus prosečne cene lidova i prosečan lead flow. Manje atraktivno, ali **podiže retention**. **Idealna prodajna baza za očekivanja: donjih 25% rezultata** — tako svi imaju osećaj da nadmašuju.

### Substantiated claims — formula sa 4 varijable
`reisEL_D7xc`
**% ljudi (ili prosek/medijana) → koji postižu X ishod → u Y vremena / posle X pokušaja → pod Z uslovima.**
Manje uslova = ubedljivije. *"I would rather have a fewer-condition statement with lower results than a higher result with more conditions."*
Prateće: **"State the facts and tell the truth"**, **"If you don't track, you don't care"**, i **measurement as intervention** — samo merenje poboljšava rezultat.

### Ad creation proces — korak po korak
`reisEL_D7xc`
1. **Stalno prikuplja podatke** (swipe file, screenshotovi, snimci).
2. Jednom nedeljno, **ujutru istog dana kad snima** ("cramming for the test").
3. Otvara **swipe file** + **istorijski najbolje oglase od pre 1, 2, 3 godine**, gleda im prvih 10–30 sekundi da vidi hook i ugao.
4. Piše **50 hookova** — **40 (80%) dokazanih/recikliranih, 10 (20%) novih eksperimenata.** *"most people flip this."*
5. **3–5 uglova** za sredinu oglasa: edukacija, rušenje uverenja, lista/koraci/priča, dokaz.
6. **1–3 CTA.**

**Ritam objave taktičkog sadržaja** (`kGfzLjPNsBU`): oglasi se prave nedeljom (*"this is when a lot of people are making ads, on Sundays"*) — video je namerno tempiran za taj trenutak. Lekcija: **objavi taktički sadržaj u trenutku kad ga publika stvarno izvršava.**

## 18. Nivoi svesnosti i call-out

### Call-out princip
`reisEL_D7xc` (iz $100M Leads, str. 136)
> *"A call out is whatever you do to get the attention of your audience. Call outs go from hyper specific to get one person's attention, to not at all specific to get everyone's attention."*

Ilustracija: tacna se razbije na žurci — svi pogledaju; dete vikne "mama" — mame pogledaju; neko kaže tvoje ime — samo ti.

**Cilj: najspecifičniji mogući call-out koji i dalje hvata dovoljno ljudi**; publika treba da pomisli **"this is for me"**. Primeri: **"moms in Nevada"**, **"homeowners in Clark County"**.

### Eugene Schwartz — 5 nivoa svesnosti
`reisEL_D7xc, HxEQCHpZzHk, HwPXctiw_rY, JDR-R--4HhM` (iz *Breakthrough Advertising*)

**unaware → problem aware → solution aware → product aware → most aware**

Svaki nivo naniže zahteva **red veličine bolje oglase**. *"The problem isn't that your market isn't big enough, it's that your ads aren't good enough and they're not specific enough to hit this next massive tranche."*

*"When you make your hooks for ads, you make your hooks depending on what part of this funnel you're finding people. **The colder the traffic, the more different the ad will be.**"*

**Primeri hookova po nivou:**
- **Unaware** = čista radoznalost, hook ne pominje rešenje: *"Texas homeowners are getting reimbursement checks for up to $X"*, *"5 weird foods that help you lose stomach fat"*.
- **Problem aware:** *"Do you get up at night to pee more than two times?"* / *"Have you ever had someone cancel a contract before it comes due?"*
- **Solution aware:** *"Have you ever tried to collect using some of these softwares and it hasn't worked the way it was supposed to?"*
- **Product aware:** *"Have you ever tried an ad agency before that's done meta ads?"*
- **Most aware** = samo ponuda, ništa drugo. *"Hey, it's Ryan Reynolds, owner of Mint Mobile"* radi samo tu.

**Ponude ("offers") rade samo na most aware — najmanjoj publici.** *"You will make money quickly doing that, but you will also cap yourself quickly."*

### Runway metafora
`reisEL_D7xc, Ec41cSp_tWc`
**"The size of the plane is directly correlated with the length of the runway."** Najduža prodaja = potpuno nesvestan čovek + veoma skupa stvar; najkraća = veoma svestan + jeftina stvar.

Problemi sa skaliranjem su obično **mismatch**: *"you're sending a warm message to a cold audience."*

- **"The longer you delay the ask, the bigger the ask can be."** / **"The longer the runway, the bigger the plane that can take off."**
- **Cold-to-warm pravilo** (`8VqSFBMMS4M`): *"You got to sell like it's cold to warm and it converts everything. If you sell like it's warm to everyone, you'll only convert warm."*

### Lubenica / golf analogije
`reisEL_D7xc`
Kod tople publike su semenke guste; kako ideš u hladnije, **gustina kupaca po hiljadu pari očiju pada, ali je tranša 10–100x veća.**
Golf: **svaki nivo svesnosti je novi štap** — ako umeš samo da putuješ, hvataš samo 15 cm.

### Continuum kupaca umesto "emotional vs. logical"
`HxEQCHpZzHk`
Osa je **high-information vs. low-information buyers**. Low-info kupaca ima malo i svi se bore za njih; high-info kupaca ima **red veličine ili više puta više**. *"Only crazy people buy immediately."*

### "Need to believes"
`FMzKk73iUhw` (Perry Belcher)
Koliko stvari kupac MORA da poveruje pre kupovine. **Cilj = nula ili jedna.** Svaki dodatni "need to believe" **eksponencijalno usporava** prodaju.
**Branding = masovno rušenje "need to believes".**
Ako imaš 2, ne guraj ih u jednu kampanju — **multi-layered kampanja**: prva rešava veliku odluku, druga (middle-of-funnel, topla publika) prodaje konkretan proizvod.

### Declarative vs. procedural — šta se daje, a šta prodaje
`spXH1kJ6q-Q, f_ceDZqhKbw`
- **Declarative** = kako nešto FUNKCIONIŠE, šta je moguće, brojke → to je ono **ČIME** prodaješ.
- **Procedural** = KAKO se radi → to je ono **ŠTO** prodaješ.
**"Give away all the information and then sell the implementation."**

Zašto: *"what's really nice about declarative knowledge is that everyone gets value from it because you just have to be there to get the value. When you teach tactics, you have to DO the tactics to get the value... which means they don't want to buy right now."*

- **Declarative hook forma:** *"Did you know that if you go from not personalized to personalized, this is the increase?"* → "I'm going to show you that's how it works, and then I will sell you how to do it."
- **Belief-breaking ram:** *"This is the old model. This is the new model."*
- Savršen primer sopstvenog pravila: u `f_ceDZqhKbw` objašnjava jednačinu hipotetičkog maksimuma (declarative), pominje "8 C's" za churn ali ih NE objašnjava — samo uputi na drugi video (**open loop**).

## 19. Give vs. Ask

### Goodwill-kapital model
`Ec41cSp_tWc`
Svaki ask **troši** goodwill i vraća te na nulu. *"You're constantly depositing and extracting capital in the relationship, and the hope is that it costs you less to deposit than you make on the extraction."* Posledica: **uvek si kapiran količinom vrednosti koju imaš u odnosu.**

**"Goodwill compounds faster than revenue does."** Alternativa give-give-give-ask ciklusu je **give, give, give, give…** — grafikon ne pada na nulu nego eksponencijalno raste. Prošireno (`neTSqOAMgao`): *"Goodwill compounds and it compounds faster than revenue. You can get outsized returns on providing value into a pool of a marketplace, and that goodwill will multiply far faster than your asking will."*

**Mehanika:** *"Most people are waiting for the other shoe to drop, they're waiting for you to make the ask, so they have their guard up. But if you give, give, and then right when they're expecting you to ask — you give again, and then again, and give even more, their guard begins to drop."*

**Word-of-mouth okidač:** ljudi te dele *"because they don't believe that they're sacrificing their own brand equity by sharing your message."*

Hormozi ide dalje od Gary Vee jab-jab-jab-right-hook obrasca: ask treba odlagati što duže, **idealno nikad** — *"and ideally you never do — you simply allow the goodwill to continue to compound."*
Isto (`neTSqOAMgao`): **"If you give enough, you don't have to ask."** — *"You've heard of the jab jab jab right hook thing? Let me tell you a real secret: **you can just jab jab jab and never right hook, and people will just lay down and say you win.**"*

**"The more you give, the more you grow. The more you take, the more you shrink."** `MD5-HByRxoA`
**"A lot of people oversell the small amount of demand they have, so they can never grow the audience."** `MD5-HByRxoA`

### Merljivi ratio
- **Give-to-ask ratio ≈ 3.5 : 1** (`HxEQCHpZzHk`), potvrđen na tri nezavisna mesta: **TV je izračunao 1 minut reklame na 3,5 minuta sadržaja**; **Facebook feed 1 reklama na 3 posta**; **TikTok je godinama imao nula reklama** dok je rastao.
- Njegova primena: **70%+ svih reklamnih impresija** mora biti "giving rather than asking" — meri **impresije**, ne samo dolare.
- **Give/Ask ratio je direktno proporcionalan prodaji** (`YUXLJauT4eY`): *"how much give has happened prior to this ask is almost directly proportional to the amount of sales that they're generating."* Gym Shark daje celu godinu (Reps for Mental Health i sl.), pa **"asks like they mean it"** na Black Friday. Ali i: **"there is a time for branding and there is a time for making an ask, and when the going is good, go hard."**

### Content = give, ads = ask
`lEIqyLE4iOY, UGEc9-7X3OQ`

## 20. Formati — repertoar struktura koje rade

### Odakle dolazi sadržaj — okidači i izvori
`eVY4Zo-hpqU, UGEc9-7X3OQ, reisEL_D7xc, 0S5xsICW8qg, kloJJeiysxg, 9unucIBuNio`

**Dva i samo dva razloga za pravljenje videa** (`eVY4Zo-hpqU`):
1. *"I want to document a thought process"* — za sebe
2. *"it's fresh off of some sort of conversation I had with a client, employee, or one of the business owners that we're invested in"* — pravi ga za tu jednu osobu pa anonimizuje

**Content Trigger Rule: "Do it when it's fresh, do it when you feel emotions."** *"You just got to catch it when you get it. You got to go make like six videos."*

**Četiri izvora tema (topic buckets)** (`UGEc9-7X3OQ`):
1. **Real time** — tvituj ideje kako se javljaju
2. **Recent past** — *"look at your calendar, look at every single meeting you had that week and what you spent your time on"*
3. **Far past** — *"the big lessons that changed or shifted my beliefs that are valuable to somebody else"*
4. **Manufactured** — "I lived on $100 for a month", "I started a school community from scratch" (bliže entertainmentu)

**Kalendar kao generator tema:** *"one of my clients had this issue with her order bump and it wasn't working, and this is what we did — great content."*

**Ostali izvori:**
- **Endless content wheel** (`reisEL_D7xc`): komentari na sadržaj → novi sadržaj → novi komentari. *"Problems never end and people never stop complaining."* Drugi izvor: **friction tačke u sopstvenoj isporuci.**
- **Content engine = FAQ engine** (`0S5xsICW8qg`): *"They all have questions, so I just make my posts answering their questions. That's it."* Isto (`kloJJeiysxg`): slušaj gde se **"problem du jour"** gomila.
- **Content plan se izvodi iz liste "šta oni vole / šta ih boli", ne iz tvoje ekspertize** (`9unucIBuNio`). Radni primer: knjigovodstvena firma → med spa klinike; njihove želje: više pacijenata, veći profit, prodajiv biznis, regrutovanje i zadržavanje sestara. Praviš content tačno po tim stavkama, uključujući ono što nije knjigovodstvo: *"here's how people set up their lobbies, this actually makes it more efficient so they can see more patients in less time."*
- **Interni proces kao izvor sadržaja** (`CojS0DwflXc, MwhsM7qijy8`): svaka sesija coachinga sales tima je gotova epizoda. Svaka glupa/pogrešna rečenica iz stvarnog razgovora je gotov hook za N-tačkasto razlaganje. *"The founder made a very ridiculous statement... I thought I would make a video about it."* Materijal dolazi iz svakodnevnih poziva, ne iz brainstorminga.
- **Bez content bucketa** (`lEIqyLE4iOY`): *"We have never had content buckets ever. If content buckets happen, it's because they occurred, not because we planned them."* Priča o onome što mu je trenutno top of mind — to samo po sebi pravi varijetet.
- **Ne pravi content po kalendaru** (`kloJJeiysxg`): *"That's a Henry Ford production line for community... I don't want to read a bloody article. I want a solution or I want to laugh."*
- **Deljenje neuspelih testova kao content** (`okA9Yt2KZuk`): *"if some of you guys do experiments and they didn't work, share it. It's valuable."*
- **Source-of-content test** (`eVY4Zo-hpqU`): **"If the source of your content is other people's content, don't make it."** Ono što content čini zanimljivim su *"the unique anecdotes that you have experienced and gone through."* / **"Step one, do stuff. Step two, talk about the stuff you did."** Greška: *"they read someone else's article on passive income and then they do a worse, diluted version with less authority."*
- **Domain-authority scoping:** ograničen ali stvaran uzorak je legitiman — *"I only have seven clients, but this has worked really well."*
- **Non-declarative framing:** ne iznosi tvrdnje kao činjenice nego kao ličnu perspektivu — *"one, the intention is pure. And two, no one can mess with you on that, because you're not saying this is fact."*
- **Named-client relevance device:** umesto adresiranja uverenja, uvedi stvarnu osobu — *"Hey, if you're struggling with this, my client Karen…"* → *"all of a sudden you have domain authority because you're now introducing a real story."*
- **Message vs. Messenger:** *"It's not because of the message, it's because of the messenger. And the messenger is a big part of the message."* Publika pita "Why should I listen to you?" — *"if you don't have a genuine answer for that, then they won't have one either."*
- **Prvo pitanje pre svakog snimanja: "Who am I talking to?"**
- Uči dekonstrukcijom: *"I'll literally take notes on his videos and deconstruct what he's doing."*

### Capture, don't create
`XsWSvz-aewA`
> **"The reality is that I'm usually not making content. We're just capturing it. That's what allows me to do so much more volume."**

Ako moraš aktivno da snimiš svaki komad a hoćeš **20–50 komada dnevno**, *"you're going to do nothing but stand in front of the camera."*

- **Meeting-mining workflow:** pogledaj kalendar → svi sastanci su virtuelni → ubaci transkripciju na svaki → *"Hey AI, tell me the interesting moments from these meetings"* → pričaj o tim momentima.
- **Bolje: ugradi dokaz u isporuku.** *"I'm trying to engineer more and more components of this into my existing business so that I can document while I deliver, so that it's no extra added time."*
  - **B2B primer doslovno:** *"Bring someone in, walk through the campaign, bring the customer in, and show it. Even if you're a small agency, do it with all your customers."*
  - **B2C primer:** sweepstakes tiket u pakovanju ekstenzija za kosu → pozoveš dobitnice u salon → ugrađuješ im ih → snimaš.
- **Ako nemaš klijente:** radi besplatne audite javno i dokumentuj ih — *"Hey, let me do a whole bunch of work for free, I'll document it, and I'll just give it away. Who says no to that?"* Čak i **5–10 klijenata** je dovoljno za dokaznu petlju.
- **Self-licking ice cream cone:** prodaješ → kupci kupuju → isporuka proizvodi dokaz → dokaz dovodi nove kupce. *"You want to install it at every business you have, especially in light of AI where proof is going to matter more than anything else."*
- **"Delivery becomes content, pitch stays pitch."** (`8VqSFBMMS4M`) *"Just sell when you need to sell, deliver when you need to deliver. And all that delivery stuff, that becomes content."*

### Q&A → content pipeline
`8VqSFBMMS4M, eVY4Zo-hpqU, LVM89ik-7Kw`
- **30–50 Q&A pitanja nedeljno = 30–50 objava nedeljno.** Uz green-screen odgovore = **3–5 objava dnevno po platformi**, *"you're going to like 20, 30 posts a day per platform."* Sečenje radi AI ili offshore editor ("a very repeatable cutting process").
- **Green screen format:** pitanje iz zajednice izvučeš iza sebe na ekran i odgovaraš; **biraju se pitanja sa najviše engagementa.**
- **FAQ = rudnik iz DM-ova:** kategorizuj sve DM razgovore u buckets pa ih odgovaraj **redosledom po učestalosti** (najčešće ≈**40%** ljudi, drugo ≈**25%**, pa long tail). **"There's no new questions."**
- **Rezultat implementacije: vreme u DM-ovima palo sa ~30 sati nedeljno na 3–5 ("reduced by 90%").**
- Ponavljanje istih odgovora (Dave Ramsey primer): *"Even though it's the same question for you, the audience will not perceive it as the same question."*

### Strukture videa — obrasci koje sam koristi
- **Obećanje na početku → isporuka na kraju** (`7NMH1oAkgLY`): *"at the end I'm going to give you a seven point checklist"* → drži gledaoca do kraja; isporuči checklist + jedan jasan URL.
- **Open loop na kraj** (`jzKpAtzKQ54`): *"at the very end I'm going to tell you the two levers you can crank to move this number into the stratosphere."* Varijanta (`Q2VUuUGfpNY`): *"I'm going to show you my favorite one at the end that I think is the most powerful of the six."* Zatim: definiši termin pre upotrebe, rekapituliraj listu na sredini i na kraju, pa otvori najavljeni finale.
- **Story-to-framework šablon** (`QtE6kk0158o`): (1) hook sa emocijom + konkretnom cifrom; (2) kratki self-intro sa proof brojkom i razlogom zašto pravi sadržaj; (3) anegdota ispričana kao **dijalog sa tačnim brojevima**; (4) izvučen **imenovani framework** koji gledalac može da nacrta; (5) CTA "recognize which of these four you are NOT using". Namerno **crta framework uživo** (*"you can draw this — you know what, I'll do it for you right now"*).
- **"Story → framework → application"** (`xy1h4qqF778`): (1) lična priča sa poniženjem i brojkama, (2) tačka preokreta u jednoj rečenici, (3) imenovanje koncepta, (4) zašto radi, (5) brojke, (6) kako gledalac da nađe svoju verziju, (7) CTA. Otvaranje kroz sopstveni neuspeh (*"it felt horrible"*, *"I just kept getting beat up"*) gradi konvikciju pre taktike.
- **Fiksna struktura po segmentu** (`qsXxckCbci0`): oblik krive → zašto taj oblik → moji kredibiliteti → pros → cons ("let's talk about the dirty") → "what's the big hairy problem you have to solve" → "how do you win" → gde reinvestiraš keš. **Ponovljivo za celu seriju.**
- **Prezentacijski šablon** (`VQM3DrnVTcs`): (1) šokantna brojka + eksplicitno imenovanje kome je govor namenjen; (2) *"raise your hand if you'd like…"* — tri konkretne želje; (3) najava tri tačke; (4) rekapitulacija posle svake; (5) na kraju: *"let's test if the pairing occurred"* + pokloni.
- **Struktura case-study contenta** (`yPDQCfrwh8E`): hook s brojkom u prvoj rečenici → eksplicitan sequel na prethodni video → **before-tabela sa ružnim brojevima** → engagement poziv → rešenja korak po korak → **after-tabela** → CTA na sledeći video.
- **Prezentacija sa pre/posle tabelom po koraku funnela** (`F3NyhOtRpOE`): impresije → response rate → zakazano → show → close → cena → cash — *"najubedljiviji format"*. Završi **živom demonstracijom** umesto slajda: *"This exercise will do more for you than anything I'd say with slides."* (`4GjwtnA76ig`)
- **Dva suprotstavljena case-a u istom videu** (`BZQtuK-ucDM`): jedan uspeh, jedan neuspeh, isti mehanizam. Hook sa brojkom i gubitkom u prvoj sekundi, cliffhanger, druga priča, razrešenje obe, framework, CTA.
- **Numerisana lista poznate firme** (`TIH1w-KuATk`): "N lessons from [famous company]" — svaka tačka je sopstveni klip za Shorts/Reels; brojke i poređenja (6 vs 7 vs 3 sastojka) su gotov materijal za carousel. Ne prepričava istoriju kompanije: svaka činjenica odmah dobija *"and here's what I do with it in my portfolio companies"*.
- **Nastava kroz case-ove sa brojevima, ne kroz definicije** (`jzKpAtzKQ54`): FB agencija (kako se greši), Starbucks + cookie (kako izgleda kad je odlično), hair extensions (kako se stackuje), glass/pool (productization). **Svaki case je gotov standalone klip.**

If I wanted someone to have an emotional experience going through this, then I have to subvert the audience."* / *"There were some dark days in the middle of the stack there — you gotta touch the bottom of the pool, that's what makes it memorable."* / *"I went from villain to hero in 10 seconds."*
- **Death by PowerPoint pravilo** (`qel9bf653Es`): **jedna ideja po slajdu, uparena sa vizuelom.**
- **Numerisane liste = retention** (`LVM89ik-7Kw`): definiši prostor mogućnosti (1–7) → dodaj filter za izbor → dodaj framework za skaliranje; svaki korak je zaseban chapter i klipabilan short.
- **Struktura short "one idea" formata** (`JfcIUxa8Nn0`): jedna izreka, jedna priča kao poreklo, tri žive anegdote kao dokaz, konkretne brojke iz sopstvenog funnela, ponavljanje poente.
- **Anti-hype okvir** (`Kk6Vdfdwmxw`): otvori problemom publike, odmah daj broj ("there are five ways"), pa **upozori na trade-off pre nabrajanja**; zatvori iskrenim preokretom: *"maybe I was giving you an answer without giving you an answer... it's usually the model itself."*
- **Struktura webinara / strukturisanog pitcha** (`8VqSFBMMS4M`):
  1. **Big promise** — prvo, pre svega ("why the hell are you here? why is it worth staying on?")
  2. **Intro / ko sam ja**
  3. **Core transformation / origin story** u formatu **OLD WAY → NEW WAY**: *"I used to do this, this didn't work, and then a friend of mine shares the secret knowledge… I implemented the stuff, and then I got this great outcome."*
  4. **"It didn't just work for me, it worked for X, Y and Z"** — različiti avatari
  5. **Tri najveće brige publike**, svaka po šablonu: **"What they think" → "Why that's wrong" → "What's right" → "PROOF"** ("ideally as many forms of proof as you can")
  6. **OFFER**
  7. **FAQ uživo**, sa pitanjem izvučenim na ekran

## 21. Proof, damaging admission i testimonijali

### Proof > Promise
`fSbqaTlWaYI, HxEQCHpZzHk, JDR-R--4HhM, JfbtySq6_B4`
- **"Your promise is not unique. Your proof is."** Sve u tržištu obećava isto (više novca, manje vremena, manje glavobolje). Diferencijacija dolazi iz (a) **mehanizma** i (b) **dokaza da mehanizam radi.** *"If you want a more unique place in the market, have more proof, not a different promise."*
- **"Proof will always outdo promise."** / *"Competitors can copy your offer, but they cannot copy your proof. That has to be earned."*
- **80% Hormozijevih oglasa je o dokazu**, ne o njemu. *"I'm not in the ads anymore at all, and I wasn't for the last two years."*

- **Testimoniali skaliraju po količini** (`_qspvJAq34M`): *"If I had a thousand testimonials versus one, you believe the person is better at the thing, and if they're better at the thing you're more certain — which actually **DECREASES RISK**, which becomes more valuable."*

### Proof Continuum — rangiranje dokaza
`fSbqaTlWaYI`
Rangiraj dokaz po tome **koliko je teško falsifikovati ga**.
- **Najslabije:** anonimni, bezlični, 10 godina star tekstualni testimonial.
- **Najjače:** čovek koji izgleda kao ti, iz tvoje industrije, uživo na bini, sa konkretnim brojem.
- **Ose:** **uživo > snimljeno; lično > virtuelno; liči na mene > ne liči; rezultat koji ja želim > rezultat blizu toga.**

### Hijerarhija proofa u sadržaju
`lEIqyLE4iOY`
Od najjačeg naniže:
1. *"I helped all these people just like you do this thing"*
2. *"I did this thing that you also want to do"*
3. **"I did this work that you're not willing to do, and I will show you the result of that work."** — poslednje radi i **bez rezultata**; **sam obim rada je proof.**

- **Education = ROI on time:** *"I spent this time learning math and I'm going to teach it to you in less time than it took me to learn it."* → što više vremena i novca možeš pokazati da si potrošio, **lekcija je vrednija**.
- **Alternativni naslov bez proofa:** umesto "I got 8 million people across these platforms, here's what I learned" → **"I made 35,000 posts — this is what I learned."**
- **Za one bez rezultata: piši o tuđim rezultatima.** *"Make an observation of how someone else did the hard thing and back into their process... ideally about a big person who's really successful where the metrics are easy to prove, and you're just connecting the dots."* On je počeo pričajući o stoicizmu i Marku Aureliju — *"no one cares what I say, but people care what he said."*
- **"How I, not how to"** (`WrCt0R3FBFs, reisEL_D7xc`): *"You start your message with 'how I built a $13 million a month company', not 'how you can build a $100 million company'."*
- **De-branded content shift** (`lEIqyLE4iOY`): ukinuli su branded content — *"If you don't know who I am, who gives a f***. I have to not say who I am, I have to say what I've done, and then that becomes the reference point for the content."*

### Damaging admission
`JfbtySq6_B4, FMzKk73iUhw, HxEQCHpZzHk, JDR-R--4HhM`
**Formula: "bad, bad, bad, BUT good."**
> *"Everything that happens AFTER the word 'but' is amplified, and everything that is BEFORE the word 'but' is diminished."*
Negativ mora biti **relevantan** za pozitiv. Lingvistička mehanika: **[istinita mana] BUT [tvrdnja koju hoćeš da veruju].** **Kontrola redosleda = kontrola naglaska.**

- **Pravilo skaliranja:** *"the more negatives I can say in the beginning, the more believable the thing that I say right afterwards is."*
- *"Nothing sells like the truth."* / *"The truth isn't just a good strategy, it's the only long-term strategy."* / *"If you don't say it, they're going to think it — so you might as well account for it up front."* / *"If you claim them, then people can't hold it against you."*
- **Referentni model: Eminem u 8 Mile** — *"he owned all of his flaws... The other person has nothing left to say. He pulls all the wind out of their sails."*
- **Ubaci damaging admissions u copy, sve bliže CTA** — *"as you get closer to your call to action, people will believe you."*
- **Zadatak:** *"Write down all of the flaws, all the things that people complain about, the things that you know you could do better, and claim them."*
- **"Own your flaws. Of course your product isn't perfect. Say so. What you'll sacrifice in promise, you'll gain in trust. And trust trumps promise."**
- **"If you want to stand out in a crowded marketplace, tell the truth."**
- **Male mane kao prodajni argumenti:** mali kapacitet = scarcity + personalizovan pristup; mala firma = "buy local". *"A lot of what people think are negatives are actually things that make their offer more compelling — especially to the right audience."*
- **Reverse-risk warning** (negativna posledica pozitivnog rezultata): Viagra — *"if an erection lasts longer than four hours, contact a medical professional"* — rizik je **amplifikovao moć proizvoda.**
- **Negative reviews kao proof.** Gym Launch: *"tons of one-star reviews... but we also have thousands of five stars."*
- **Damaging admission na nivou brenda** (`HxEQCHpZzHk`): unapred prisvoji sve zamerke ("of course I'm trying to make money, I'm in business") — onda kad uradiš nešto lepo, ljudi kažu "that was nice of him".
- **State the facts and tell the truth** (`ZTSI3DDP_4A`): **"The truth sells the most. The truth converts.** Above everything else. **But it has to be the whole truth** — you have to say all the shit where you look bad too, and then people will believe the shit where you look good. **That's the whole game."**

## 24. Repurposing i content multiplication

### CONTENT MULTIPLICATION SISTEM — 5-koračni model
`lEIqyLE4iOY, WrCt0R3FBFs`
Model kojim je rastao sa **180k na 1,2M pratilaca za 6 meseci**:
1. **Test** — Twitter/X (i TikTok) kao javni "brain dump". Ranije je ideje slao sam sebi mejlom u beskonačan thread; sad ih objavljuje javno.
2. **Record** — najbolje performirajući test-content pretvara u video: *"tweets turn into videos, the threads turn into long videos, the short tweets turn into shorts."*
3. **Add call to actions** — CTA na 3 mesta: **početak, sredina, kraj** (+ mid-rollovi u dužem sadržaju).
4. **Contextualize** — svaki klip prepakovan za platformu; thumbnail i tekst pišu se posebno za TikTok / YT Shorts / IG. Mentalni model: **"Dolly Parton meme"** (me on Facebook / me on Tinder / me on LinkedIn).
5. **Distribute (volume)** — masovna distribucija preko svih kanala.

## 25. CTA mehanika

### Pozicija i broj
- **CTA na 30% dužine YouTube videa** — konkretan pozicioni benchmark. `3yAiVjcImQ4`
- **Pet površina, jedan CTA** (`3yAiVjcImQ4`): (1) CTA na 30% videa, (2) u opisu videa, (3) u pinovanom komentaru, (4) u YouTube profilu / about sekciji, (5) proaktivan DM svakom novom followeru. **Princip: jedan CTA ponovljen na svakoj površini; ne pet različitih CTA-ova.**
- **CTA usred videa, ne na kraju** (`ovL6Z5z0jxQ`), formulisan kao share za druge: *"send this video to whoever is going to be implementing it, or your partner, or just to yourself... It would mean the world to me. Otherwise, back to the video."* **Kraj videa = redirect na sledeći video, ne prodaja.**
- **Dva mid-roll CTA-a, oba uslovna i za različite avatare** (`RVbvhPGFi6E`): vlasnik biznisa → scaling workshop; iskusan prodavac → zapošljavanje. Plasirani **posle vrednosti, ne pre.**
- **Broj CTA dugmadi na stranici** (`HwPXctiw_rY`): *"If you only have one CTA on a site, if you add another you will increase conversions; if you add a third you'll also probably increase conversions. At a certain point you have too many referees on the field."* **Optimum: 2–3.**
- **Dužina CTA: 5–10 sekundi**, nikad 3 minuta. `WrCt0R3FBFs`

### Kvalitet i personalizacija CTA
- **CTA pro-tip: personalizacija, ne "više"** (`reisEL_D7xc`): umesto "if you want more stuff like this, go here" → **"If you want me to help apply this stuff to your business, let me know."**
- **"Custom" umesto "more" kao organski CTA** (`lEIqyLE4iOY`): na organskom postu ne nudiš "još sadržaja" (toga ima beskonačno) nego **personalizovano** — "I can make custom recipes based on your macros". **Custom se ne dobija skrolovanjem.**
- **Kontekstualan CTA, nikad copy-paste** (`pLhQOYMGa88`): *"a lot of people do the same copy-paste CTA, or I see YouTubers take their mini-ads and just slice them in — they're wearing a different shirt or it's a different energy. That's a huge mistake. **Take the extra 30 minutes to make it contextual.**"* **Dva CTA su OK ako služe različitim publikama** ("ako skaliraš — ovde", "ako tek počinješ — ovde"), ne pet.
- **Slojeviti CTA po fazi kupca** (`VQM3DrnVTcs`): postojećim vlasnicima → besplatne video verzije knjiga (acquisition.com/training); onima koji tek počinju → Skool zajednica, besplatan start, "friendly competition and some prizes".
- **Kvalifikacioni CTA** (`jzKpAtzKQ54`): *"we just started a workshop division at acquisition.com for companies that we don't own — if you want to see if you qualify you can fly out to Vegas... hit the scale button and if you qualify our team will reach out."* **Filtriranje povećava percipiranu vrednost.**
- **Lead magnet na sredini videa, ne na kraju** (`sGv2BTUCcCM, orMbq2LtzKE`): acquisition.com/roadmap, sa **kvalifikacijom kao statusnim signalom**: *"to be fair, this is only for business owners. If you're not there, by all means, go enjoy the scaling roadmap."*
- **CTA uvek sa razlogom** (`NA61omfYgvI, AGCtZmgJ1JA`): *"smash the subscribe button and hit the notification bell — **the reason for that is because I don't actually have a cadence** when I make these videos."*
- **Gift framing** (`i7bLRKwKSms, qsXxckCbci0`): *"This is a gift either way. **No pressure.** It's absolutely free."* / *"This is not a promise, I'm just saying I'd love to help."*
- **Low-friction mystery CTA** (`3yAiVjcImQ4`): besplatna registracija za launch, *"I can't tell you what it is, but it's less than a Bitcoin and more than an NFT — every single person who shows up live gets one."*
- **Preemptivni ad-break** (`7NMH1oAkgLY`): usred edukativnog videa kratka, samosvesna reklama (*"you might be wondering why I just caught a football"*) pa povratak na sadržaj. Meta-transparentnost: *"The only reason I'm making an ad is because I talk about ads in the book and I want to prove that I know what I'm doing."*

### Engagement CTA
- **CTA koji traži commitment, ne mišljenje** (`qpQvdBFW_yI`): *"Tell me which of the seven you're actually going to do — not what you think would be cool — **which one can you implement in the next 30 days**."*
- **Engagement CTA sa zadatkom** (`jzKpAtzKQ54`): *"comment below — or have one of your teammates comment below — **which one of the eight ways** that I just talked about you can implement in your business tomorrow."* Traži specifičan komentar (broj iz liste), ne "šta mislite". Uz **"show this video to your team"** — video se deli interno.

- **Share CTA** (`reisEL_D7xc`): *"It would mean the world to me if you just share this video with another business owner."*

### Split-testiranje

- **CPM vs. CPL dijagnostika** (`HxEQCHpZzHk`): ako **CPM stoji isti a CPL 2x/3x/4x** — nije skuplje doći do ljudi, nego **manje njih reaguje** ⇒ negativan word of mouth. *"Two out of the three people who would have bought your thing heard it was bad."*

- **Upozorenje za vendore** (`MD5-HByRxoA`): većina agencija je "data driven" i opsednuta klikovima/pregledima/pratiocima. **"Just because it's easy to quantify doesn't mean it's important."** Ako hita samo ono što nije tvoj core — odbij, inače gradiš publiku koju ne želiš.

## 27. Diferencijacija, konkurencija i hejt

### Diferencijacija sadržaja
`zNJ5JzEJgyo`
- **"Starter kit" identitet:** svaka niša ima uniformisani template (real estate agent = buzzed sides, hard part, gel, odelo, srednja BMW serija, "just bought this beauty" post). **Kopiranje starter kita = nevidljivost.** *"You're not building anyone's brand. You're just reinforcing an identity that everyone already knows."*
- **Belief mismatch:** namerno iznesi **2–4 uverenja koja NE idu uz uniformu** koju nosiš (model: Joe Rogan — UFC + vanzemaljci + standup + carnivore). Zašto: *"as soon as someone feels like they have figured you out, they'll move on… they know everything about you already."* **Namerno objavi ono čega se plašiš da kažeš.**

- **Ne konzumiraj konkurentski sadržaj:** isključio je sve konkurentske reklame iz feeda. *"The act of consuming everyone else's stuff… those thoughts become top of mind for you and you become another Mickey Mouse."* / **"It's really easy to be original if you're just not looking at everyone else's stuff."**

### Us vs. Them
`HwPXctiw_rY, cGup9yYrOoc, HxEQCHpZzHk`
- **Us vs. Them** je najjača tema u Mint Mobile arsenalu, u skoro svakom adu. *"This strategy is best employed when you are **NOT the market leader**. If you're number one you don't want to do us versus them — you'd use **us versus the alternative**"* (npr. "us vs. your couch").
- **Side-by-side comparison** je dozvoljen ako si underdog: *"If you're the top dog you don't show anybody else's; if budget is your primary sales point, side by side makes sense because they can see the delta."*
- **[protivrečno / razrešeno]** `cGup9yYrOoc`: **"Coke never acknowledges Pepsi. The classic never acknowledges the inferior."** Nikad ne pominji konkurenta imenom — svako pominjanje ga podiže na tvoj nivo.
  **Razrešenje:** Mint napada **kategoriju** ("big wireless"), ne imenovanog manjeg konkurenta. **Pravilo: napadaj kategoriju/alternativu odozdo kao underdog; nikad ne imenuj nekoga manjeg od sebe.**
- **Shrink your competition, don't beat it** (`HxEQCHpZzHk`): (a) narasti toliko da ih niko ne čuje, (b) ubij ih ljubaznošću. *"There's two ways that you can become the tallest building: you can knock everyone else down, or **you grow so that they become smaller in comparison**."*

## 28. Landing page, CRO i reklame

### Landing page template koji je "već rešen"
`IFpHasZ7jN4`
Amazon, Zillow, Airbnb konvergiraju na isti layout — (1) video, (2) carousel slika, (3) recenzije, (4) buy button, (5) šaka bulleta. *"Those guys have more traffic and conversion data than anyone on Earth — they built it that way for a reason."* **Nemoj izmišljati.** **Uči strukturu sales page-a od Amazona.**

### Above the fold
`9Dc2UQbQNTo, JfcIUxa8Nn0, HwPXctiw_rY`
- Gotovo sav dobitak u konverziji dolazi **iznad preloma**, na desktopu i telefonu. *"That is where you're selling them on whether they're going to stay on the page."*
- **"You could probably spend 90% of your attention above the fold and get way higher returns on your time than everything below the fold."** Ako si daleko ispod cilja — **"don't even worry about anything below the fold."**
- **Uklanjanje često pobeđuje dodavanje:** *"I can't say the amount of times where literally just **deleting everything below the fold** got us better throughput than even putting anything there to begin with."*
- **Struktura above the fold ("layout three", $100M Leads str. 147):** **headline → subhead → 3 bullets → dugme.** Plus **attention avatar** na vrhu ("Attention new customers", "Time is literally ticking").
- **Redosled testiranja — 90% svih njihovih testova:** (1) **headline**, (2) **slika ispod headline-a.** Ako promena headline-a čini sliku besmislenom, testira se **par headline+slika** protiv starog para.

### Rezultati CRO rada
`JfcIUxa8Nn0`
- **100+ testiranih varijanti funnela za 3 nedelje**
- **3× throughput na landing page-u samo promenom headline-a**
- **5× throughput na scheduling stranici UKLANJANJEM headline-a** (izbačeno "You finished this process", pa su ljudi pomislili "Oh, I'm not done, I need to take this next step")
- rutinski **2× do 4×** samo od rečenice na vrhu i jedne slike
- Komentar traffic guy-a: *"If people knew how much we work on optimizing our conversion process, they wouldn't want to change anything, because they'd know that we've probably already tried it."*

### Ostala CRO pravila
- **Budućnost marketinga je CRO** (Neil Patel, prihvaćeno): cene oglašavanja rastu, platforme rade na aukciji — **pobeđuje ko bolje konvertuje iste oči.**
- **Page speed** (`HwPXctiw_rY`, Neil Patel studija): *"For every 1 second saved you get something in the neighborhood of a **2.5% increase in conversion**."* Sa **9–13 sekundi na 3 sekunde = 2x+** na konverziji.
- **Landing page ≠ sales page:** kratka landing page prvo, pa sales page. *"When you have too much traffic and not enough conversion, you add a step, you add friction, and it sorts the right traffic."*
- **FAQ na dnu sales page-a skoro uvek podiže konverziju:** *"Think about every one of these questions as a couple percentage points of customers that haven't gotten all their questions resolved."*
- **"Buying questions" kao micro-commitment:** Mint-ov ZIP/coverage checker = ekvivalent pitanja "talk to me about payment plans" na prodajnom pozivu. Napravi micro-korak PRE kupovine (**"onboarding a customer before they're a customer"**).
- **Kongruencija:** *"One of the easiest ways to drop conversion is have someone click somewhere and then not get what they think they're getting."* **Svi hookovi moraju voditi na stranicu koja ih ponavlja.**
- **Konverzija knjige** (`IFpHasZ7jN4`): na sopstvenom Shopify page-u **2–3%**; ista knjiga na **Amazonu 30%** — **10x**, zbog pozajmljenog poverenja platforme.

### Reklame — 6-faktorski rubrik
`npi7UeOE_0o`
**Checklista za svaki ad/video/landing:**
1. Did it grab attention?
2. Did it make you care?
3. Did it sell the dream?
4. Did it create urgency?
5. Did it show social proof?
6. **Most importantly: did it tell you what to do next?**

**Tri objektiva reklame — ne mešaj ih:** čist **branding**, **reinforce existing position** (Doritos), **direct response.** Ocenjuješ ad prema **njegovom** objektivu, ne prema svom ukusu. **"You might not be the demo."**

- **Pravilo br. 1 marketinga:** **"No one cares about you, they just care about themselves."** GoDaddy i On/Federer su napravili reklamu **o SEBI** (ime firme, glumci) umesto o kupcu.
- *"You can't beat data… at the end of the day, **the best ad is the one that drove the most sales.**"* / **"Let the data talk."**
- **"The point of advertising is to let people know about your stuff, but the point of persuasion is to change behavior."**
- **Tipografija CTA:** *"A lot of people are watching TV from afar, they don't have subtitles on, so I tend to be **over-indexed on text**."* (Ramp je CTA rekao samo audio; trebalo ga je **napisati na ekranu.**)
- Rocket: CTA je bio **jedna sekunda na kraju** — *"I have to be paying attention to know rocket.com and to know it's a mortgage company."* Zato nije S tier.
- O pažnji: *"It has to keep your attention… This was the only ad I had to **rewatch** to try and understand — and if that's what a lot of people had to do, they're just never going to rewatch it, and the moment's gone."*
- **Zašto je Google Gemini jedini S tier:** ista emotivna narativna kriva kao Rocket, ALI **product demonstration je utkana kroz ceo spot** — u svakom trenutku znaš čiji je proizvod i kako se koristi. **Emocija + demo = S. Emocija bez jasnog proizvoda = A+ maksimum.** Ad je "presekao mnogo publika" prikazujući univerzalne trenutke — *"you've been the kid, you've been the adult, you've been the one applying for the job."*
- **Pain-hook → demonstration** (ramp.com, 15s): počni bolom "kako izgleda BEZ proizvoda", pa ga odmah reši demonstracijom proizvoda.
- **Struktura koju eksplicitno preporučuje za modelovanje** (iz breast-cancer ada): **ogromna pažnja → činjenica koja izaziva nelagodu → testimonial nekoga ko je to izbegao koristeći uslugu → CTA.**
- **Kako bi prepravio GoDaddy ad (šablon za before/after B2B softver):** pokaži pravog vlasnika biznisa kako ne ume **5 emotivno bolnih stvari** ("kako da isplatim platu", "kako da javim na telefon", "kako da odgovorim na mejlove") → ubaci proizvod → **vrati se na tih 5 stvari i pokaži da sada sve rade** → recap → CTA. *"**The more emotive those five things were, the more compelling the ad would have been.**"*
- **Instacart:** najjačih je poslednjih 5 sekundi jer prikazuju **problem → trenutno rešenje proizvodom** ("zaboravio si mleko → hitna porudžbina"). Preporuka: **napravi ceo ad od te mehanike.**
- **Direct-response: prvih 0,5 s mora dati kontekst**; booking.com je pao jer počinje logom pre nego što gledalac zna o čemu se radi.
- **Nekonzistentnost poruke** (Hims & Hers): reći "sve te medicine su za profit" pa "uzmi našu medicinu" — *"that felt very like **pandering**."*
- **Ne kažnjavaj brend ad zbog nedostatka urgentnosti** ako urgentnost nije on-brand — ali onda mora postojati **jaka asocijacija.**
- **Cene:** Super Bowl **~$8M za 30 sekundi**; 15s ≈ **$4–5M**; Jeepov ~2–2,5 min ≈ **$8M × 5.**
- **Tier lista:** **S:** Google Gemini (jedini). **A:** ramp.com, Doritos, Jeep, Nike, **A+** Rocket Mortgage. **B:** Instacart, breast-cancer ad (B+). **C:** booking.com, On/Federer, GoDaddy. **D:** Hims & Hers.

## 29. Monetizacija publike

### Četiri načina, poredana po riziku i exit vrednosti
`Jmkq5RLjm0U`

**1. Affiliate** — plaćen posle prodaje; najbrže, najniži rizik, bez kapa na zaradu, **najniža exit vrednost.**

**2. Sponsorship / endorsement** — plaćen unapred, cena po **CPM**. Dva oblika:
- objave svojoj publici
- **whitelisting** (brend pušta tvoj post kao svoju reklamu van tvoje publike) — *"the business pays money to advertise your brand — they make you bigger… and they get a higher conversion rate because you're better on camera and you already have a warm audience."*
Exit vrednost samo ako izgradiš **medijsku kuću / talent agenciju.**

**3. Partnership (equity)** — struktura: **deo unapred + deo po performansu + deo koji vestuje kroz vreme** — **25% odmah, 25% po performansu, 50% vesting kroz 4 godine.**
- Performance primer: **"1% per thousand customers I bring"**, sa kapom (**do 10%**).
- Upozorenje: **"the association that you make with their brand is permanent"** — zato **uvek traži nešto unapred.**
- **Definiši sve W-ove unapred:** *"links in my bio, one week of the month, one long-form mention, five short-form posts or stories, plus ads once a month you can use for whitelisting."*
- Pravilo palca: *"The more money you have right now, the more you want to lean [toward equity], because the sponsorship cash that you choose to take, you do it at the expense of equity."*

**4. Own brand** — **white label** (brže, jeftinije) vs. **custom** (niko ne može prodavati identičnu stvar). Njegov izbor: **custom** — *"the moment that people find out that you just have a generic product that you slapped a label on, it's not a good look."*

**Četiri promenljive po kojima procenjuje deal: exit value, cash in/out, koliko rada, koliko rizika.**

**Brojke:** Prestige Labs **5.000+ affiliatea, $20M+/god**; Allen (software) **1.000+ affiliatea**; lansiranje $100M Leads **30.000 affiliatea**; exit Gym Launch **$46,2M** (decembar 2021, American Pacific Group).

## 30. Agencije i tim

### Dvofazni pristup
`Jmkq5RLjm0U, fd-hi3NqMYo, UGEc9-7X3OQ`
Tri opcije za kupovinu ekspertize: **slavne ličnosti** (previše personalizovano), **gurui/coachevi** (zastarelo — *"Guys, you've got to do hashtags on YouTube videos"*), **agencije** (izabrao ovo).

- **Faza 1 — bazična agencija $3.000–5.000/mesec** (jedan brend, jedna platforma). Svrha nije kvalitet nego **prisiljavanje na ritam**: *"They just got me to commit to doing three videos a week. They just trimmed it... The main objective of that first phase was just to get the basics down, forcing me to create content on a regular cadence."* Traje **3–6 meseci.** (Plaćao ~$5.000/mesec: *"in no way was he special, basically did nothing, but it kept me accountable to making three videos a week."*)
- **Faza 2 — premium agencija $15.000–30.000/mesec** koja radi sa top kreatorima, da naučiš nijanse.

**Ugovor za učenje 6 meseci = $6.000 × 6 = $36.000 LTV** koji možeš da iskomuniciraš agenciji. **Rok kao alat sa dve strane:** agencija vidi jasan LTV; tvoj tim ima tajmer.

**Prva agencija bila dobra, sledećih 10 agencija loše.**

### Insourcing sekvenca
uči → dokumentuj u interne procese (SOP) → co-creation → tvoj tim radi paralelno → naš tim postane bolji → prelaz na consulting retainer → prekid, amicably.
*"our stuff for us starts to be as good and eventually better than the stuff that they make for us — **because I'm only one client of many to them.**"*
Zašto interni tim pobeđuje: **"They have more time… and they will always have more context on your brand. By having more time and more context, all we have to fill is the gap of expertise."**
**Period preklapanja (plaćaš i agenciju i svoj tim) je namerno prihvaćen:** *"you're paying twice — the thing is, when you do that, you're actually building the enterprise value of your business and you're building the asset that you own."*
Isto (`UGEc9-7X3OQ`): *"There's going to be a period where they work and your team works, so you pay twice, until your team beats their team. And I tell them: **I'm going to beat you** — that's all I'm trying to do here."*

### "Agency trap" — 11 koraka ciklusa
1. uzbude te novim leadovima
2. onboarding koji deluje vredno
3. dodele **svog najboljeg senior repa**
4. vidiš rezultate
5. **prebace senior repa na najnovijeg klijenta**
6. junior preuzima, rezultati padaju
7. žališ se
8. senior se povremeno vrati da te smiri
9. rezultati se nikad ne vrate, otkazuješ
10. tražiš drugu agenciju i ponavljaš ciklus
11. pitaš se zašto nikad nije kao prvi put

### Kriterijum i selekcija
- **"Do you have more money or more time?"** Ako nemaš para — učiš sam, *"you pay down your ignorance assets faster"* / *"you pay down your ignorance debt faster."*
- **Rizik zavisnosti:** *"what more realistically happens is they gouge you and they continue to raise their price because they can see how much money you're making."*
- **Selekcija** (`UGEc9-7X3OQ`): **"talk to 10**, listen to all of them, you'll quickly see who has their shit together."
- **Signal kvaliteta:** **"the best agencies don't need to run ads — they have more customers than they can handle."**
- **Zapošljavanje:** **"find one person to hire 10 people. Who's really smart. Overpay them."**
- **Litmus test za izbor agencije/eksperta** (`rc7cxL7ql7Y`): *"It takes me about **15 calls** with experts to really wrap my head around a space."* Kad prestaneš da učiš nešto novo na pozivu i **počneš da govoriš njihov žargon** — tada si informisan kupac i tek tada biraš. **Red flag** ako agencija ne priča jezikom: *"this is the funnel the leads come in on, this is the offer they're seeing, the people who see the most success call leads within 5 minutes, this is the script they use, this is how they maximize show-ups."*
- **Redosled leverage-a** (`MD5-HByRxoA`): prvo **vendori/specijalisti po platformi** (5× brže uče), pa interni zaposleni, pa skaliranje volumena in-house.
- **Rezultat metoda** (`fd-hi3NqMYo`): sa **<10.000 na 5.000.000+ pratilaca za manje od 24 meseca**, bez prethodnog znanja o YouTube/TikTok/IG/X. Isti playbook primenio na **YouTube, short form, LinkedIn, podcaste i paid ads.**

### Definicija
**"A good book is a complete solution to a narrowly defined problem."** Loši sadržaji imenuju stotine problema i ne reše nijedan.

### Redosled problema kao serija proizvoda
"ne znam šta da prodam" → knjiga o ponudi; "nemam kome da prodam" → knjiga o leadovima.

### Struktura
Jedan usko definisan problem → **3–6 velikih "kanti"** → za svaku kantu **priča koja je oblikovala to uverenje** → **framework po poglavlju.**
Test: *"If this thing I'm writing does not apply to that [one problem], you take it out."*

### Struktura priče (6–7 elemenata)
**Setting → Character → Desire → Struggle → Eureka → Victory → Resolution.**
Uz **dva putovanja heroja: spoljašnje i unutrašnje.**
**Stakes & struggle = jedina stvar koja priču čini zanimljivom.**
**Gde staviti priču:** kad tekst počne da "teži" od gustine taktika, **zumiraj napolje i ubaci priču** koja daje kontekst, pa se vrati u materiju.

### Sedam faktora "newsworthiness"
1. **Recency** (otud "hopping on trends")
2. **Impact** — autor mora eksplicitno premostiti "zašto je ovo važno za tebe"
3. **Prominence** — poznata imena
4. **Proximity** — u lokalnom oglasu bolje **predgrađe (Towson, Maryland) nego grad (Baltimore)**
5. **Conflict**
6. **Unusualness** — navedeš mozak na obrazac pa ga prekineš
7. **Updatability** — koliko puta možeš dati novi update

### Proces
ideacija (najduže) → prvi draft → teško editovanje jezika naniže → **4–8 beta čitalaca** (ljudi čije mišljenje ceniš ali koji te NE poštuju toliko da bi ti prećutali istinu) → finalni draft → lektor.

- **20–25 sati nedeljno na pisanje, ~1.000 sati po knjizi.** Piše **4–5 sati PRE radnog dana.**
- **Kontraintuitivan deo:** piše dok ne naiđe na **logičku nekonzistentnost** → **vraća se na početak, menja pretpostavke i piše IZNOVA.** Ponavlja dok model ne izdrži.

### Rezultat
**$100M Offers — 200.000+ prodatih primeraka u prvoj godini, bez izdavača, bez reklamne kampanje, lansirano pre nego što je imao publiku.** Čist word of mouth.
Kontekst: **1,6 miliona knjiga se objavi godišnje.**
