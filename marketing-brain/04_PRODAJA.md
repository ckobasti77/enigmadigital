## Osnovni okvir: šta je prodaja

- **Cilj prodaje nije "yes, I'll buy" nego "yes, I'll decide"** [NcD2t9qt-fM, WrCt0R3FBFs]. Prodaja = davanje moći (power = *"the ability to influence or direct events or people"*). Uspeh sesije se meri time da li je čovek doneo odluku. *"The point of the sale is not to get them to buy, the point of the sale is to get them to decide."* Etimologija: decide ← lat. *caedere*, "to cut off" → *"Which future are we cutting off today?"* [WrCt0R3FBFs]
- **Prodaja = transfer ubeđenosti preko mosta poverenja.** *"Selling is a transference of belief over a bridge of trust."* [NcD2t9qt-fM] / *"Sales is a transference of conviction... it's a transference of belief. And the bridge of that is trust."* [okA9Yt2KZuk, JE2_7elAcxM] / *"Sales is a transfer of conviction from you to them through a bridge of trust."* [q32-l3Yoqg4] / *"Fundamentally a sale is simply a transfer of belief. And if you do not believe, then you cannot transfer that belief."* [OmDv6-651SM]
- Dve varijable: **conviction** i **trust**. Trust dolazi iz **ekspertize + nekog nivoa rapporta** [AGCtZmgJ1JA]. Bez baseline ubeđenosti skripta je bezvredna [bHnVItVGJPA].
- **Trust kao uzrok kupovine** [JfbtySq6_B4]: tri sub-verovanja moraju biti poravnata — **veruju u tebe/sistem, u sebe, i u svet.**
- **Emocija vs. logika** [NcD2t9qt-fM]: emocija spušta prag akcije kratkoročno, logika drži odluku kad emocija splasne. Bez logičke konstrukcije dolazi refund poziv "za dva dana". Što je klijent viši u poslu (B2B, veći ugovori), to su odluke logičnije.
- **3 preduslova da bi iko kupio** [NcD2t9qt-fM] — mora želeti cilj i verovati: (1) da će ga proizvod dovesti do cilja **na način na koji on želi da stigne** (*"the way they want to get there"* — ljudi odbiju članarinu od $100 a kupe $400 suplemenata); (2) da će ga ti i ljudi oko njega podržati; (3) da će raditi **za njega lično** ("I'm a snowflake").
- **The Five Ps** [OmDv6-651SM] — dijagnostički checklist kad deal ne zatvori: *"the **promotion** for the right **prospect** at the right **price** for the right **product** following the right **process**."*
- **Prospect knowledge > product knowledge** [2lA_A8BGRRs]: *"We should be focusing on who are we selling to more than what are we selling... If you can explain to somebody exactly the problem that they're going through, they will buy whatever you have."*
- **Belief & Trust kao kontinuumi + "plane / runway" model** [JE2_7elAcxM]: veća kupovina = veći avion = duža pista (više edukacije pre poziva).
- **BF Skinner okvir** [JE2_7elAcxM]: *"If I dehydrated the horse, bled it out and salted its mouth and put water right in front of it on a hot day, I can veritably guarantee that it will drink."* → *"If you control every single variable, you could in theory close every single prospect."* + **"100 golden BBs"** (nema jednog metka, ima sto zlatnih sačmi).
- **Asimetrija iskustva** [NcD2t9qt-fM, AGCtZmgJ1JA]: *"we've had this conversation a hundred times, they have only had it once. We come in with a massive advantage."*
- **Unlosable position** [q32-l3Yoqg4]: *"if you're on the phone with a prospect **you always have the upper hand**, because they have admitted at the beginning that they have a problem and have not solved it — so inherently they're missing one of the keys. They cannot have them all, or they would not be on the phone. **You are in an unlosable situation unless you choose to lose the frame.**"*
- **Zašto će kupiti** [q32-l3Yoqg4]: *"The reason they're going to buy is because of how understood they feel — how accurately you were able to restate back to them exactly how they felt. **Which is why you need to shut the fuck up when they're talking.**"* Šire: *"The goal is not for them to understand what you are writing. The goal is for them to feel understood. That is the goal of great persuasion."* [F3NyhOtRpOE]
- **"The pain is the pitch."** [JE2_7elAcxM, F3NyhOtRpOE]
- **"How someone is sold will dictate how legit they think you are."** [3yAiVjcImQ4]
- **Sales = personalized marketing** [JE2_7elAcxM]: ako kupcu treba 6 informacija, marketing pokrije 1-2-3, prodavac 4-5-6; kod sledećeg marketing pokrije 1-3-5, prodavac 2-4-6. **Sales & marketing = jedan kontinuum** pod jednim krovom (Chief Revenue Officer).

## CLOSER framework
[JE2_7elAcxM, StVqS0jD7Ls, 3yAiVjcImQ4, okA9Yt2KZuk, bgmh7p1s7zs, oi7bnS8uyJM, afbP6sB_Atc, 2lA_A8BGRRs, q32-l3Yoqg4, AGCtZmgJ1JA]

**Univerzalnost:** *"Works for B2C and B2B... and it'll work for $500 tickets as much as $100,000 tickets — the process is the same."* [q32-l3Yoqg4, okA9Yt2KZuk]

**Pravilo forme** [okA9Yt2KZuk]: **svaka stavka u skripti mora biti PITANJE, nikad izjava ili pasus.** *"Do not put paragraphs, do not put statements, because they will start reading them. They can't think that way. Only put questions, because they can pause, they can ask another question, and they need no intelligence to do that, which means it's scalable."* Skripta = **okvir pitanja, ne monolog** [2lA_A8BGRRs].

### C — Clarify why they are there
- *"Why did you reach out today? What's the goal? Why is that important?"* [JE2_7elAcxM]
- *"What made you come in today? What's your goal right now? Why is that important to you? Why that number specifically? What would it look like 12 months from now?"* [okA9Yt2KZuk]
- *"Why are you here, why'd you take the call, what's the problem, what are we trying to solve?"* [q32-l3Yoqg4]
- Premisa: *"People don't get on the phone to get more information. No one wants to learn stuff. People get on the phone because they want to solve a problem."* [JE2_7elAcxM]

### L — Label them with a problem
- *"I can't cure cancer until you admit you have it."* [JE2_7elAcxM, q32-l3Yoqg4] / *"We can't cure cancer unless they admit that they have it."* Kad kažu da — *"you now have cancer, I can cure it."* [okA9Yt2KZuk]
- Prospekt mora **sam** da prihvati etiketu [q32-l3Yoqg4].
- Postavljanje gapa: *"Just to make sure I'm clear, you are here, you want to get here, and this has been the issue that's been holding you back."* [JE2_7elAcxM]

### O — Overview past pain (Pain Cycle)
- **Petlja od 4–5 pitanja, ponavljaj u krug dok se opcije ne isprazne:** *"What have you tried so far to accomplish that?" → "How long did you do that for?" → "How long ago?" → "How did that work out for you?" / "Had that worked for you?" → "What else have you tried?"* [JE2_7elAcxM, okA9Yt2KZuk] — dok ne kažu "probao sam sve".
- Reakcije: na dobro se slažeš (*"Totally"*), na loše se slažeš (*"Oh, that's terrible."*) [okA9Yt2KZuk].
- **"Deprivation creates motivation."** Kvantifikuj potrošeno vreme/novac, uticaj na status u zajednici i **cost of inaction po danu** [JE2_7elAcxM].
- Cilj: *"the goal here is to make them feel like they're six inches away and they need one link to be successful."* [okA9Yt2KZuk]
- Rezultat: *"at that point we have **tons of ammo** we can use later in the close."* [q32-l3Yoqg4]
- **Obavezan potez unutar O — skini krivicu:** *"if they had this missing piece or two of the equation, they were three quarters of the way there, they were just missing this one piece — which we're going to provide."* [q32-l3Yoqg4]

### S — Sell them the vacation, not the plane flight
(Detaljno u sledećoj sekciji.) Uključuje **traženje dozvole**: *"I think you'd be a good fit, can I tell you about it?"* [2lA_A8BGRRs]. Zatim objasni kratkim analogijama kako rešavaš **2–3 problema koje su sami naveli**.

### E — Explain away their concerns
Obstacle overcomes; drilovanje na flash-karticama [okA9Yt2KZuk].

### R — Reinforce the decision
- Dolazi **posle naplate**; dodato naknadno jer ljudi otkazuju. Daje **veći LTV, manji churn, manje refundova i chargebackova**; handshake ka customer success [JE2_7elAcxM, okA9Yt2KZuk].
- *"Make their feet nice and hot."* [okA9Yt2KZuk]
- *"The sale continues throughout the entire customer relationship."* Onboarding ih zatvara ponovo, prvi check-in ih zatvara ponovo. *"We have to consistently sell them on why they should take action — why should they not just watch Netflix?"* [q32-l3Yoqg4]

### Varijanta strukture razgovora [AGCtZmgJ1JA]
1. Zašto su tu / u čemu je problem.
2. **Šta su do sada probali** — i gde je podbacilo.
3. Pokazati zašto je tvoj proizvod drugačiji **od onoga što im nije uspelo**.
4. **Tražiti dozvolu** da objasniš proizvod.
5. Objasniti proizvod — **nikad kroz features, samo kroz iskustva** koja će imati, uz analogije.
6. Zatvaranje — **no-based close**.

### Dodatni okviri unutar poziva
- **BANT** (Budget, Authority, Need, Timing), iz IBM-a 1960-ih [3yAiVjcImQ4, JE2_7elAcxM] — sva četiri se rešavaju PRE traženja novca. Timing pitanje sa drop-down opcijama today / tomorrow / a few weeks — *"You want to sell to people who say today or tomorrow."*
- **Plus-minus-minus-plus, 4 ugla persuazije** [3yAiVjcImQ4], koristi se na "S" u CLOSER-u: *"If you work with me, you get more good stuff. If you work with me, you get less bad stuff. If you don't work with me, you get more bad stuff. If you don't work with me, you get less good stuff."*
- **Proof – Promise – Plan** kao otvaranje poziva, **15–20 sekundi** [JE2_7elAcxM].
- **Boil the decision down to the most basic unit possible** [2lA_A8BGRRs]. Za B2B servis: *"The decision we have to make is whether you're going to make more money on your own than you will with us, in excess of our fee."*
- **Skriptovanje vs. pitanja** [okA9Yt2KZuk]: **super-skriptovano radi samo za transakcione prodaje ~$200 i manje**; za sve iznad — question-based diagnostic framework (lakše se uči, brže ramp-uje). Isto [CojS0DwflXc]: niski transakcioni B2C = strogo skriptovano, ne treba ekspert; kompleksan deal = prodavac mora da razume klijentov biznis ("business consultants").

## Sell the vacation (Maui) i pravilo tri stuba
[JE2_7elAcxM, StVqS0jD7Ls, okA9Yt2KZuk, oi7bnS8uyJM, q32-l3Yoqg4]

- **Ne prodaješ let, prodaješ Maui.** Svi ostali prodaju let: *"your modules, your meal plan, your macros, your workouts, your support team, your URLs, your domains, your funnels, your targeting, your ads — and the other shit that doesn't matter."* [q32-l3Yoqg4] Ne prodaješ TSA, skidanje cipela, loš kikiriki — prodaješ drveće, vetar, osećaj i **status increase među vršnjacima** [okA9Yt2KZuk]. *"We don't want to talk about TSA."* [JE2_7elAcxM]
- **Šta se NE pominje** [okA9Yt2KZuk]: *"We don't talk about Zoom calls. We don't talk about support. We don't talk about hours. They don't want to talk about any of that, because that doesn't matter."*
- **Zašto ne pričaš o proizvodu** [q32-l3Yoqg4]: *"we don't get into jargon, we don't get into technobabble about the program and the modules and the things they're going to do, because **all of that sounds like work — and that's not what they're there for. They're there for a result.**"*
- **Tiering kroz istu destinaciju** [oi7bnS8uyJM, q32-l3Yoqg4, JE2_7elAcxM]: *"Everyone gets to Maui. Do you want to swim to Maui? Do you want to take a boat? A normal flight? Or a private jet?"* — plivanje (ajkule, možeš se udaviti) → brod (duže, bez wi-fi-ja) → normalan let (brže, loša hrana, security) → **privatni džet (direktno, šampanjac, kofer spakovan kad stigneš)**. Svi paketi vode u isti ishod; razlika je samo **brzina, glatkoća i kvalitet putovanja**. **"The likelihood of arrival is assumed."**

### Pravilo tri / Three-Pillar Pitch
- Svaka ponuda se pakuje u **tačno 3 stuba**. *"Humans tend to think in threes… chunk up until it's three."* [JE2_7elAcxM] / *"The human brain can't take more than three."* [q32-l3Yoqg4] / *"human attention span isn't that good"* [okA9Yt2KZuk]
- Primeri, nezavisno od industrije: fitness = **fitness, nutrition, accountability** (odn. nutrition + training + accountability); prodaja leadova (mortgage lead firma) = **exclusive, timely, qualified**. *"There's always three you can find when you're selling a solution."* [q32-l3Yoqg4]
- Struktura svakog stuba: **izjava + metafora** [JE2_7elAcxM]. Svaki bullet se objasni za **5 sekundi**, pa se traži commitment, pa dalje; svaki stub nosi **jednu anegdotu od 30 sekundi ili manje** [okA9Yt2KZuk, JE2_7elAcxM].
- **Dužina pitcha: ispod 3 minuta (180 sekundi).** *"The number one thing that people mess up."* [okA9Yt2KZuk, JE2_7elAcxM] / *"It shouldn't be longer than three minutes, tops, because no one really gives a shit."* [q32-l3Yoqg4] — **[protivrečno]** u [StVqS0jD7Ls] stoji **max 2 minuta**. (Nema izričitog razrešenja u izvorima; obe verzije nose istu poentu — što kraće.)
- **Napredno pravilo o složenosti pitcha** [4GQLJjH9-oA]: što je proizvod više **DIY / done-with-you**, to pitch mora biti **JEDNOSTAVNIJI** (1-2-3, A-B-C); što je više **done-for-you**, to **više koraka prikaži** (11, 27, 38 koraka) — da prospekt pomisli "verujem da radi, ali ne želim to sam da radim".

### Metafore i analogije
- **Toothbrush (accountability)** [JE2_7elAcxM]: *"When you were a kid, did you hate brushing your teeth? …Now you're an adult, do you brush your teeth? In the beginning you had external accountability — your parent was your coach — and over time the positive reinforcement became the internal motivation. We're going to artificially become that external motivation you need in the beginning to create the habit."*
- **Amazing Race (accountability)** [JE2_7elAcxM]: *"I've been to where you're going and I've been in the backseat with a hundred other people just like you, and I can be your backseat driver, because it'll give you an unfair advantage."*
- **SEO vs. ads** [JE2_7elAcxM]: *"Think about your ads as your paycheck… you have to put work in, but you get money out immediately. Think about SEO like an investment account: you're going to put money in, but it's not going to be much for a while, but eventually it starts compounding, and within one or two years it's going to be bigger than anything you could do actively."*
- **Pre-packaged analogies** [CojS0DwflXc]: pripremi VIŠE mini-priča za isto uverenje, da ih rotiraš.

## Pravila razgovora: šta sme da izađe iz usta prodavca
[CojS0DwflXc, q32-l3Yoqg4, 2lA_A8BGRRs, cy2k1GdA-9o]

- **Samo tri vrste izjava:** (1) **pitanja** (najviše ih ima), (2) **restatements** (ponavljanje njihovih reči nazad), (3) **kratke priče / analogije** — i to tek na kraju, kad zatraže. Ništa deklarativno, nikakvo podučavanje. *"Only three things should come out of your mouth: questions, restatements, anecdotal stories. That's it."* Najčešća greška svih prodavaca, uključujući njegove [CojS0DwflXc, q32-l3Yoqg4].
- **Teaching vs. coaching** [CojS0DwflXc]: *"They're not going to listen to you until they buy, so there's no actual point in teaching — but you do want to coach."*
- **Belief-breaking > tactics** [CojS0DwflXc]: *"everyone gets benefits from breaking beliefs; only 10% of people get benefits from tactics."* Razbijanje uverenja daje vrednost **100%** ljudi na pozivu, taktike samo 1 od 10 — i to obično onom ko neće kupiti.
- **Nikad ne pitchuj pre nego što veruješ da će reći DA** [CojS0DwflXc]: *"Lawyers do not ask questions they do not already know the answers to."*
- **Permission-based prelaz na pitch**; ako klijent prerano traži pitch — **ODBIJ ga da bi zadržao frame** [CojS0DwflXc]. *"If someone's commanding you around, you're not going to close the deal."*
- **State shift kroz kontrarnu izjavu** [CojS0DwflXc]: ako u prvih 5–10 min osećaš da nećeš zatvoriti, složi se sa njihovim pesimizmom — pa oni krenu da TEBE ubeđuju da nije tako i time zauzmu stav koji im prodaje tvoje rešenje.
- **Anti-pattern: "technobabble"** (Russell Brunson termin) [CojS0DwflXc] — periodizacija, softver, metodologija. *"They don't care."* Vidljivo u snimcima: engagement pada tačno kad prodavac uđe u taj deo i priča 5 minuta u komadu.
- **Zabranjeno pitanje: "Do you have any questions for me?"** [2lA_A8BGRRs, RVbvhPGFi6E] — *"you're asking the prospect to come up with more reasons to not buy."* / *"It's literally asking them to have objections and asking them to take the wheel."* Crveni signal loše prodaje je prodavac koji to pita više puta.
- **Deflekcija pitanja (hot potato)** [cy2k1GdA-9o]: P: *"What makes you better than your competition?"* → *"Well, what things are you looking for?"* → *"Why are those things important to you?"* — *"I want to be like smoke; they can't catch anything that I say."*
- **"They believe nothing that you say, they believe everything that they say."** / **"People don't feel sold, they feel like they bought."** / **"The person who's answering the questions is the one getting interrogated."** [cy2k1GdA-9o]
- **Word concision** [cy2k1GdA-9o]: loši prodavci nesvesno dodaju rituale jer su bili nagrađeni prodajom → skripta se vremenom razvlači. **Kraći poziv = više poziva = više prodaja.**
- **Sowing seeds of doubt** [zzleYxkf39k]: ne napadaš konkurenciju direktno; postavljaš pitanja o varijablama koje kupac nije uzeo u obzir, pa on sam zaključi da nisu ista stvar. **Shift from local to global:** *"All of a sudden you give them way more variables than they thought about... That's what education is for."*
- **Treća strana kao "loš momak"** [1UhvBSQFy6A]: vaga kaže da si debeo, ne ti; grafikon lica izgovara dijagnozu. Nikad ti.
- **Snižavanje očekivanja** [afbP6sB_Atc]: *"Sometimes when you promise the moon it actually makes people doubt you more... sometimes when you actually LOWER expectations you INCREASE their perceived likelihood of achievement, because they actually believe you."* Reskriptovali su prodajni proces da obećava manje — **close rate se nije promenio uopšte**, a dva od tri problema su nestala.

## Discovery i kvalifikaciono uokviravanje

- **Discovery mora ostati "chunked up"** [4GQLJjH9-oA] — na najvišem mogućem nivou. Čim prospekt postavlja detaljna pitanja, **on kontroliše razgovor**. *"During discovery YOU ask the questions, THEY give the answers."*
- **Details are death traps** [JE2_7elAcxM] — izlazak iz detalja kroz mechanic/secretary close.
- **Discovery = pitanja o preferencijama, ne o specifikacijama** [cy2k1GdA-9o]. Ne dodaješ pitanja o poslu — dodaješ pitanja o preferencijama koje otkrivaju elemente offer-a ("Da li vam je bitna kvalifikacija ljudi? Imate li već materijal? Imate li nešto vrednije od $500?"). **Svako "da" automatski gradi VIP paket.**
- **Kvalifikaciono uokviravanje poziva** [4GQLJjH9-oA]: umesto standardnog intro-a, poziv se otvara kao provera **da li se prospekt kvalifikuje za garanciju / bonus / start u ovom terminu**. *"I'm not here to sell, I'm here to first make sure that I can even give you something"* → *"they're the ones who are on the other side hoping to earn your favor."*
- **Kvalifikacija u DM-u binarnim pitanjem** [3yAiVjcImQ4]: *"Hey — you here for free stuff, or do you want help with your legal stuff?"*
- **Multiplikaciono pitanje** [mRlSb0O5QNU]: pitanje "koliko eventa godišnje radite" pretvorilo je prosečan tiket sa **$80k na $240k** — *"we've just converted a one year client into a three year client."*
- **Ne fact-finding na pozivu** [m5ordaa7NN4]: *"What are we going to do, spend the whole time fact finding before I can actually make a recommendation? That's such a waste of time... I can just click around and find out what I need."*

## Diagnostic Sale — 8 koraka
[JE2_7elAcxM, 1UhvBSQFy6A]

1. **Pre-sale questionnaire** koji agitira problem i **podiže svest o problemu** kod kupca, a prodavca naoružava. Isto pitanje na 5 načina: *"koliko te ovo košta finansijski? koliko gore za 5 godina?"*
2. **Uzmi info + kreditnu karticu PRE nego što si išta prodao** — *"let me complete your customer profile / I want to set up your account profile… standard procedure."*
3. **Current state** — gde si danas.
4. **Desired state** — gde želiš da budeš. **Klijent SAM bira cilj, na skali/grafikonu — time klijent postavlja cenu.**
5. **Obstacle** — *"The thing the person thinks is in the way is often not the real thing in the way"* — ali uzmi NJIHOVE reči i vrati im ih.
6. **Present price in relation to the goal** (ne mesečna članarina).
7. **Guarantee / risk reversal** vezan za taj ishod.
8. **Incentive to prepay** → step-downs → **transition to recurring.**

**5 koraka implementacije** [1UhvBSQFy6A]: (1) secret shop sopstveni biznis, (2) prikupi info i identifikuj constraint, (3) definiši gde je constraint, (4) **"kill zombies"** — anticipiraj sve prigovore i obori ih PRE cene, (5) implementiraj.

**"Kill zombies"** [1UhvBSQFy6A, JE2_7elAcxM]: *"it's way harder to get someone to buy after you presented the price, because now they're like 'he's trying to sell me'. So we have to counter that BEFORE we become salespeople in their mind."* Interni nazivi: **"killing zombies" / "diffusing the bomb"** — *"It's a lot easier to kill a zombie when it's far away than when it's on top of you."*

**Tri tipa prodaje na spektru** [1UhvBSQFy6A]: transactional (20–30 min, nizak ticket) ↔ custom/diagnostic ↔ enterprise/relational. **Magija: isporučuj transakciono, a PRODAJ kao da je custom.**

**Force script adherence kroz softver** [1UhvBSQFy6A]: automatizuj point of sale tako da prodavac ne može da nastavi bez čekiranja polja — a da bi čekirao, mora da postavi pitanje.

## Teorija prigovora: obstacle vs. objection, onion, koreni

### Obstacle vs. Objection
[JE2_7elAcxM, NcD2t9qt-fM, cy2k1GdA-9o]
- **Obstacle** = neslaganje **PRE** nego što si tražio prodaju / pomenuo cenu — lako se ruši.
- **Objection** = neslaganje **POSLE** cene ("crvena zona") — mnogo teže. *"It's easier to handle obstacles than objections."* / *"It's much easier to address things before you ask for money than after."*
- **Taktika: front-load sve prigovore u skriptu, pre cene** — dok nema uloga na stolu.

### Tri izvora distorzije (Albert Ellis / CBT)
[JE2_7elAcxM, NcD2t9qt-fM] — Ellis sveo 11 → 9 → 7 → 3 iracionalna uverenja. Spolja ka unutra:
1. **Circumstances** — *"I must get what I want when I want it… if I don't get what I want I can't stand it."*
2. **Others** — *"Other people must treat me fairly and kindly, and if they don't they are no good and deserve to be condemned and punished."*
3. **Self** — *"I must do well or else I am no good."*

Svaki prigovor je jedna od te tri distorzije — mesto gde kupac **prebacuje svoju moć** (casting power away).

### The Onion of Blame
[NcD2t9qt-fM, cy2k1GdA-9o, WrCt0R3FBFs]
Ljudi krive spolja ka unutra: prvo **okolnosti** (vreme, novac, fit) → pa **druge ljude** (partner, supružnik, zaposleni, deca, decision maker) → pa tek onda **sebe** (sumnja u sebe, avoidance). Ljuštiš luk spolja ka unutra; **po tipu prigovora znaš na kom si sloju i koliko si blizu zaključenja.**
- *"I don't have time"* = najspoljniji sloj.
- *"I need to think about it"* = **dobar znak** — dva sloja si dublje i pričaš s donosiocem odluke; ostalo je samo da kaže da. *"Ako neko kaže 'moram da razmislim' — odlično, znači pričam sa osobom koja ima moć."* [JE2_7elAcxM]

### Pet manifestacija mapiranih na tri korena
[JE2_7elAcxM, NcD2t9qt-fM]: **Time** (circumstances) → **Value/Price / Money** (circumstances) → **Fit / snowflake** (self) → **Authority / decision maker** (others) → **Avoidance / stall** (self).

### Četiri kategorije prigovora i teorija iza njih
[StVqS0jD7Ls]: **Time** → prioritization; **Decision maker** → **support, not permission**; **Details** → preference (*"you want my results your way"*); **Money** → value + resourcefulness; **Stalls** → decision avoidance.

### Tri prigovora u praksi drilovanja
[okA9Yt2KZuk]: postoje samo tri — **cena, stall (odugovlačenje), donosilac odluke.** Prigovori se **DRILUJU na flash-karticama**.
**Strategija po tipu** [oi7bnS8uyJM, bgmh7p1s7zs]: cena → **gradi vrednost, ne spuštaj cenu**; donosilac odluke → **past agreements**; stall → **nauči ih da donesu odluku**.

### Time objection — 3 pod-ugla
[NcD2t9qt-fM]: **macro** (busy season) / **micro** (nemam sat u danu) / **when-then** — svaki sa svojim overcome.
**"When-Then fallacy"** (od Jasona Fladliena): *"When I have X, then I will do Y"* obrće redosled uzroka. *"I will start saving money once I'm rich."* / *"I'll pay for the program that'll make me more money when I have more money — that's the point of the program."*

### Dijagnostika prigovora unazad
[q32-l3Yoqg4]: *"they don't see the value later — **you didn't articulate it**. They have an objection on decision maker — usually because **you fucked something up earlier in the sale**."* Isto kod odbijanja plaćanja: *"you probably messed something else up in the sale earlier."* [Ul87yrDKZ78]

### Cena kao interni okvir za trening tima
[oi7bnS8uyJM]: *"If we were giving away Ferraris right now for five grand, do you think they'd find a way to get the money?"* → **"It is always their fault if someone cannot afford the program"** (tj. prodavčeva greška u prenošenju vrednosti). Procenat ljudi koji zaista ne mogu da priušte: *"a very, very, very, very, very small percentage of people"* [iwskrByMIqo].

### Dijagnostika prodavca
[bgmh7p1s7zs]: *"Crazy people buy without obstacles. Normal people have concerns — you need to overcome them."* i *"You can never make a sale if you never ask for it."*

### Childlike curiosity kao standardni odgovor
[NcD2t9qt-fM, AGCtZmgJ1JA, 1UhvBSQFy6A, RVbvhPGFi6E]: nagni glavu, pitaj neprijateljski-nenapadno — *"Huh, that's so weird, I wouldn't have thought that. Tell me more about that."*
Obrazloženje: *"I believe that you can sell without ever having confrontation... in arguments no one wins."* / *"If you win the argument in a sale, you lose the sale. The only way that you win the sale is being willing to lose being right."* / **"Seek to understand, not to win."**

### Boksersko disanje
[AGCtZmgJ1JA]: *"in the beginning you breathe in too much, you hyperventilate; the guys who've done it enough slow down their breathing when things get intense. Sales is a lot the same."*

### Enterprise upozorenje
[StVqS0jD7Ls]: za velike firme (6–12 meseci ciklusa, odlučivanje po komitetu) transakcione tehnike NE važe — cilj je **umnožiti broj veza** između timova: *"if it's you and one person, that bond is easily broken; if it's three people on their team and two on yours, it's much harder to break."*

## Reframing i 3A framework
[RVbvhPGFi6E, JE2_7elAcxM, StVqS0jD7Ls]

**Definicija reframe-a:** *"the **one to three sentences** that you say after a prospect says anything but 'yes' that increases the likelihood that whatever your next thing you say gets them to buy."* Ide PRE svakog close-a / objection handlinga.

**3A / AAA triad na svaku primedbu:**
- **ACKNOWLEDGE** — ponovi im njihovu rečenicu nazad; deluje kao aktivno slušanje i **kupuje ti 2–3 sekunde**, gradi rapport.
- **ASSOCIATE** — poveži njihovo pitanje sa ponašanjem klijenata koji imaju NAJBOLJE rezultate: *"that's a question a lot of our best customers ask"* — sumnja postaje dokaz da su dobar kupac. Koristi **"foil" / straw man** (neko drugi, autoritet u firmi) da se kaže gruba istina bez napada na ego.
- **ASK / ATTACK the frame** — postavi pitanje o njihovom pitanju umesto da odgovoriš; vraća kontrolu.

**5 pravila reframe-a** [RVbvhPGFi6E]:
1. **Prospekt ne veruje skoro ničemu što TI kažeš, a veruje skoro svemu što SAM kaže** → ne govoriš im da su dobar fit; pitanjima ih "breadcrumb"-uješ do zaključka.
2. **"No one can disagree with a question." / "Be like smoke."** — *"You never win a sale by winning an argument."*
3. **Tell them what their question MEANS.**
4. **Use straw men for tough truths.**
5. **Retain childlike curiosity at all times. "Seek to understand, not to win."**

**Tri načina straw-man/foil asocijacije** [RVbvhPGFi6E]:
- (a) **treće lice danas** — *"I had someone earlier today who asked the same question — can I share with you what I just shared with them?"* (sada možeš biti brutalan prema TOM čoveku);
- (b) **uspešan prošli klijent** — *"That actually makes you just like Sarah, who had a similar plumbing business and crushed it with our marketing agency."*;
- (c) **appeal to authority (ne svom)** — *"Alex just told me this thing earlier today, can I share it with you?"*

**Labeling → naplati labelu na kraju** [RVbvhPGFi6E]: dodeli mu labelu rano (*"you seem like a really honest person"*, *"family man"*), pa je iskoristi u close-u (*"smart people buy this thing"* / "because he's a family man, he should buy").

**"The angry boat"** [RVbvhPGFi6E] — u customer service nikad ne umanjuj ljutnju, idi IZNAD nje: *"That's a totally reasonable concern."*

**Broj traženja prodaje** [RVbvhPGFi6E]: **najbolji closeri = oni koji traže prodaju najviše puta.** Ograničenje: svaki ask ne sme da smanji rapport → zato reframe. **"The best closers make the most asks."**

**"Huh?"** izgovoreno naglas kupuje **2–3 sekunde** [RVbvhPGFi6E].

**Permission frame** [RVbvhPGFi6E]: *"Can I put my coach hat on instead of my friend hat?"* — **UPOZORENJE:** ne radi ako si mlad prodavac pred starijim vlasnikom; tada koristi straw man ili appeal to authority.

**Kontekst autora:** **100+ prodavaca u portfoliju, $250M+ godišnje**; skill radi od **$100 do $1M+** paketa, uživo, telefon, Zoom, sa bine [RVbvhPGFi6E].

## Katalog close-ova

### Decision-making framework (3 yes/no pitanja)
[okA9Yt2KZuk, bgmh7p1s7zs, WrCt0R3FBFs, q32-l3Yoqg4]
*"Do you think that we can help you solve this problem? Do you like me / do you want to work with us? Do you have access to the amount of money required to start?"* → *"If it's yes, yes, yes — let's rock and roll. Because at the end of the day, you don't need more time. You need more information to make a decision, and I'm your source of information, and I can do this all day."*
Verzija [q32-l3Yoqg4]: *"Do you feel like what we're doing can meet your needs and solve the problem? Do you want to work with us as a company — do you like our values, do you believe what we believe? Do you have access to funds or know someone who does?"* → **"Do you like the product, do you like us, and do you have the money. That's it."**

### Decision-maker close, 3 koraka
[okA9Yt2KZuk, JE2_7elAcxM, cy2k1GdA-9o]
1. **Circumvent** — *"What if they say no?"* / *"What would happen if they said no?"* — **1 od 3 puta** prospekt kaže *"I'd probably do it anyway"* → deal je zatvoren.
2. **Divert** — *"What do you think their biggest objection would be?"* pa napadneš stvarnu prepreku iako osoba nije na pozivu.
3. **Past agreements** + *"Sometimes it's better to ask for forgiveness than permission. Fair enough?"*
Okvir: decision maker je **support, not permission** [StVqS0jD7Ls].

### Best case / worst case ("play it out")
[cy2k1GdA-9o] — **najjača tehnika**: *"What are you afraid of happening?"* pa *"Let's just play it out."* *"I've probably closed more sales on best case / worst case than any other close."*
Van prodaje isto [rc7cxL7ql7Y]: na strategy sesijama *"what happens next, what happens next"* dok ne vidiš da realan worst case nije strašan. *"Fear is a mile wide and an inch deep."*

### Looping
[cy2k1GdA-9o]: reši prigovor pa **traži prodaju ponovo**, neograničen broj puta, ali SAMO ako si prethodno rešio konkretnu brigu. *"If you do not ask for the sale you will not get it."*

### Concession → reciprocity → "fair enough"
[okA9Yt2KZuk] — univerzalni obrazac, **do 3 puta**: *"I don't know if I can do this." — "How about I throw this in?" ... "Fair enough."* — *"all we're doing is concession, reciprocity, 'fair enough'. And people don't like to feel like they're not being fair."*
**"Fair enough" close** (Jordan Belfort) radi na **reciprocitetu** [ETEFhDICm5o]: napraviš ustupak → "fair enough?" → teško je poreći da je fer → *"then let's move forward."*

### No-based pitanja (tri tona agreement pitanja)
[ETEFhDICm5o] — izvor: Chris Voss.
- **Dva tipa pitanja:** *mining* (izvlačenje informacija) i *agreement* (postavljaš tvrdnju i tražiš saglasnost). *"If you get enough agreements, it's going to be natural that they're going to want to move forward."*
- **Yes-based** (*"Sounds great?"*) — najteži yes; **Neutral** (*"Fair enough?" / "Does that sound reasonable?"*) — lakše; **No-based** (*"Would you be opposed to…?"*) — **najlakše se dobija saglasnost.**
- Psihologija: *"People feel safer saying no. People feel like they retain their autonomy... But if you phrase your questions properly, you can get people to say yes by saying no."* Posebno jako kod decision fatigue.
- Ne mora SVAKO pitanje biti no-based — preformuliši **ključne milestone tačke**.

### Ostali closes
[WrCt0R3FBFs, bx48qPlaGvE] — doslovne verzije u §8 (SKRIPTE): **Reason Close** (bilo koji razlog koji navedu postaje razlog ZA), **Hypothetical Close** ("skeleton key"), **1-to-10 Close**, **Unicorn Close**, **Burn-you-twice**, **Past close**, **Fear close**, **Worst-case close**, **Found money close**. Takođe **two-call close / skidanje pritiska** [fSbqaTlWaYI] — vidi §8.

## Assumed close i upsell
[xy1h4qqF778]

- **Cela poenta:** *"**I didn't ask her WHETHER she wanted them. I asked her WHICH ONE she wanted.**"* Definicija: *"the prospect is choosing between **two options that both are buying from you**, rather than whether or not they're going to purchase."*
- **Radi najbolje kao UPSELL, ne kao primarno zatvaranje.** *"It is one of the strongest upsells in all of business."* Zašto: (1) kupac te već zna i veruje ti, (2) **kupovina se već desila**, (3) **kartica je već na fajlu**.
- **Metod za pronalaženje šta upsell-ovati:** *"look at the normal things that a customer will buy in addition to your services **as a result of the decision**."* Fitness: odeća, patike, oprema, aplikacije, namirnice, priprema hrane, crockpot, suplementi. *"If you can predict what those are — which you should know — **and weave those into your sales process as an either-or decision**, you will usually get a very big percentage uptake."*
- **Close rate kontrast:** primarna prodaja hladnom prospektu = *"one out of three, or one out of four, or if you're really good, **one out of two**"*. Sa assumed close-om kao upsellom = *"**nine out of ten, nine and a half out of ten** — and you can close them at **higher tickets at higher percentages**."*
- **Rezultat:** *"I ended up making **just as much if not more from the supplement sales in profit than I did from my actual facility**"* i *"**I was able to cover ALL of my advertising expense simply from the upsells** — so that took all of what used to be my service revenue and just turned that into profit."*
- **Dijagnostičko pravilo:** *"whenever there's a sales process that generates tons and tons of traffic and they're closing anywhere in the **80%+ range**, I usually know that it's an **upsell**."*
- **Ključne mikro-taktike:** on **preporučuje** kad ga pitaju (*"I like the kiwi"*) — uklanja odluku umesto da je vraća; **namerno zaustavlja prodaju** (*"I didn't want to sell any more because I was afraid she would somehow back out"*); fizički **gurne proizvode preko stola**; **nijedna rečenica o proizvodu** (*"I didn't even talk at all about what these even did, or how to take them, or what the benefits were"*).
- **Kontekst neuspeha pre proboja:** ~15 od 15–20 konsultacija tog dana završilo *"no after no after no"*; prethodni pitch je bio *"hypertrophy and maintaining lean body mass and a whole bunch of jargon that she would never even understand."*

## Ghost products i prescriptive close
[bx48qPlaGvE]

- **Ghost products ("sacrificial lambs")** — namerno stavljaš na listu preporuka stavke koje **NE prodaješ ti**, i šalješ kupca da ih kupi jeftinije drugde (ili mu kažeš da mu uopšte ne trebaju). Nastanak: ostao bez proizvoda, rekao ženi "možeš to jeftinije u Costco" → kupila sve ostalo. **Pravilo izbora: proizvodi sa najnižom maržom.**
- **Rezultat:** *"I ended up selling 100% of people"*; ljudi koje je obučio, a nikad pre nisu prodavali, zatvarali su **80–90%** onih koji uđu u objekat. Kontekst: launch sa **100 novih klijenata** u jednom danu.
- **"Move to their side of the table"** — počneš preko puta stola, završiš rame uz rame. Ghost product je najbrži način da se psihološki obiđe sto.
- **Prescriptive close** — ne pitaš da li hoće, nego **propisuješ kako će koristiti**, pa tražiš potvrdu naplate: vizija koristi → tačna uputstva → **habit stacking** → one-click upsell. Habit stacking: *"getting someone to make a new habit is incredibly difficult... so we just want to piggyback on habits they already have."*
- **Budget descoping:** poređaš stavke po važnosti i skidaš odozdo, ali **uvek reintroduciš problem** koji ta stavka rešava. Nikad ne pokazuj da je izbačena stavka bila nevažna, *"because then it looks like you're selling stuff that doesn't matter."*
- **Reps > kursevi:** ubaci se u prodajni proces biznisa sa velikim brojem jeftinih transakcija (perionice, masaže, nokti, frizeri) da nakupiš ponavljanja.
- **Manipulacija vs. pomoć = namera:** *"like all persuasion, the difference between manipulation and help is intention."*

## Zatvaranje plaćanja (ID-first close)
[Ul87yrDKZ78, StVqS0jD7Ls]

**ID-first close:** posle *"Fair enough?"* i "Yes" → traži **ID, ne karticu** (*"much easier to ask someone for their ID than it is to ask them for their payment method"*) → kad otvori novčanik/telefon, **vizuelno potvrđuješ da ima sredstva plaćanja** → uzmi ID (uživo) ili slikaj (Zoom), napravi ličnu opasku o mestu odakle je (rapport); vraćanje ID-a gradi poverenje — *"I grabbed it from you and I'm giving it back"* → **trade close**: držiš ID i mašeš u pravcu njegovog novčanika. **Ovaj korak sam rešava ~80% slučajeva.**

Na Zoomu: *"Can you put your ID next to the camera, just to make sure that we associate the right account profile with the right payment… we try to be PCI compliant."*

**Hijerarhija fallback-a:**
1. **ACH preko banking app-a** — *"What bank do you bank with?"* → *"Can you pull up your banking app real quick?"* → Statements → bilo koji mesec → broj računa gore desno. Verovatnoća da klijent ima banking app: *"virtually **100%**"*.
2. **Apple Wallet** (drugo, ne prvo).
3. **Delayed charge** na "pogrešnu" karticu + zakazana zamena (*"I'll delay the payment for 24 hours"*).

**Premisa:** *"realistically, no one ever leaves their home without something to pay with… it's not actually real"* → prigovor je lažan izgovor. Koren-uzrok: *"you probably messed something else up in the sale earlier."*

**Uzajamni alarm:** *"What time you going to get home? Okay, cool. I'll set an alarm on my phone. Why don't you set an alarm on your phone… And I'll call you at that time."*

**Pomeranje pritiska na spoljni autoritet:** *"Why don't you put one of the cards you don't want to use down right now? Cuz I have to do that. That's policy… this is just how we've always done it."*

**Diagnostic pivot** kad je otpor stvarno u ponudi: *"Hey, let's just pivot for a second. Don't worry about signing up. I just want to talk to you. What did you come in here for?"*

**Ćutanje posle dogovora:** *"The moment you have agreement, you shut your mouth, you ask for payment information, and you close."* (*"Save your Lego Star Wars lightsaber for after she gets back."*)

**Nikad ne šalji fakturu/ponudu mejlom** [mRlSb0O5QNU]: *"I have very few rules of law, but a rule of sales is: never present an invoice via email. It should always be over the phone, because they're going to have questions, and you want to be the person who can answer those questions."*

## Conviction — najveća poluga
[bHnVItVGJPA, JE2_7elAcxM, 42SOlVhDwWE, oi7bnS8uyJM, iwskrByMIqo, okA9Yt2KZuk, AGCtZmgJ1JA, OmDv6-651SM, q32-l3Yoqg4, QbDFCbpmI6w, F3NyhOtRpOE, 0BZ7-PZR8jE]

### Dve jednačine [42SOlVhDwWE]
1. **Closing % = Skill of sales × Conviction**
2. **Total deals closed = (Skill × Conviction) × Work ethic**
→ **"Conviction counts TWICE"**, jer visoka konvikcija sama diže i work ethic: *"If you believe that what you are selling is genuinely going to help people, you will follow up faster, you will follow up more religiously."*

### Skale nisu iste
Skill **1–10**, conviction **1–100** [42SOlVhDwWE]. *"Even if your skill of sales is a 2, you could have 100 belief — and I guarantee you that you will close."*

### Conviction je skalarna, ne binarna
[AGCtZmgJ1JA, JE2_7elAcxM, okA9Yt2KZuk, q32-l3Yoqg4]: *"It's not do you believe in the product or not, but to what extent… That is the predictor of your ability to close."* / *"It's not 'do my sales guys believe in my product' — it is HOW MUCH do they believe."*
Kalibracija [q32-l3Yoqg4]: *"Would I bet a thousand dollars on this product? Ten thousand? Would I sell my mother this product? **The most convicted person will always win the fight** — they have conviction in their skepticism, you have conviction in your product, and the question is whose belief is stronger."*

## Sales motion — operativa oko poziva
[JE2_7elAcxM, cy2k1GdA-9o, mRlSb0O5QNU, 3yAiVjcImQ4, AGCtZmgJ1JA, q32-l3Yoqg4]

### Tri posla prodavca
[JE2_7elAcxM, StVqS0jD7Ls, cy2k1GdA-9o] (1) maksimizuj broj prilika → (2) konvertuj najveći % njih → (3) radi to konzistentno jako dugo.

### Kanta 1 — maksimizacija prilika [cy2k1GdA-9o]
- **Maksimum raspoloživih sati/dana.** *"You should be available when the prospect is available"* — uključujući vikende. *"Businesses also pay rent on Sundays."* **Najjači prediktor ukupne prodaje kroz sve firme = ukupan broj raspoloživih time slotova.**
- **Pull-up:** termin za četvrtak, danas ponedeljak, imaš rupu → prevuci ga na danas. *"Today appointments always have higher show up rates."* Obrnuto: nepotvrđen termin za par sati → oslobodi slot i popuni ga responzivnim leadom.
- **Dva SOP-a: "on-the-call" i "off-the-call".** Velika većina firmi NEMA off-call SOP — tu se gubi najviše novca [cy2k1GdA-9o, JE2_7elAcxM].
- **BAMFAM — "Book A Meeting From A Meeting"**: nikad ne završi poziv bez sledećeg zakazanog termina [JE2_7elAcxM, cy2k1GdA-9o]. **Handoff → Handshake:** svaka interakcija završava imenovanom osobom koja već zna ceo kontekst.
- **Ne uzimaju odbijanje lično** — mere se po broju outreach/follow-up pokušaja u CRM-u.
- **"Kill list"** — vidljiva lista van CRM-a sa **2–3 whale prospekta** kojima svaki dan daju ekstra pažnju [cy2k1GdA-9o, JE2_7elAcxM].
- **Sami prave prilike — referrals.**
- **Solar rep koji je odmah zvao svaki novi booking prodavao je 4x više jedinica od drugoplasiranog.**

### Kanta 2 — konverzija [cy2k1GdA-9o]
Priprema, beleške, **slušanje 2:1**, "breathe the script", ubijanje zombija unapred, best case/worst case, **traži prodaju ponovo**.

### Kanta 3 — meta-veštine [cy2k1GdA-9o]
Konzistentnost, entuzijazam, "kill for sport", praćenje podataka, nikad ne krivi okolnosti.

### Pre-call i between-call nurture
- **Personalizovani pre-call touch** [JE2_7elAcxM]: voice memo ili video text ujutru na dan poziva; *"which gift card would you prefer — Amazon, Starbucks or X?"* (preference question → veći show rate); three-way intro između settera i closera. **Morning-of nurture je obavezan.**
- **VSL između poziva 1 i poziva 2** [JE2_7elAcxM] — social proof + benefiti unapred; prodavac onda samo rešava zabrinutosti.
- **Više call-ova za zatvaranje = premalo edukacije pre prvog poziva** [JE2_7elAcxM]. Rešenje nije bolji closer nego više pre-marketing sadržaja (*"get them to the 6-inch putt"*).

### VSL sistem
- **VSL pre svakog razgovora, jedan po tipu razgovora** [mRlSb0O5QNU]: *"Before you ever talk to a customer you should have a video sales letter. If you have two conversations, there's two VSLs."* Kod klijenta iz case-a tri: **top-of-funnel** (avatar + pain + prag za saradnju), **bottom-of-funnel** (za zauzetog CEO/CFO koji odlučuje a ne dolazi na poziv), **post-event** (pre debrief poziva, sa drugim prigovorima).
- **VSL formula** [3yAiVjcImQ4]: **5–7 minuta**, "who, what, where, when, why" + FAQ. *"You just answer the questions in the language that they present to you on sales calls... you take all those questions, you say it back to them in the video."* **Piše se iz jezika prospekta**, ne iz marketing jezika.
- **VSL engagement trik** [mRlSb0O5QNU]: ubaci CTA unutar videa — *"text me your head count / the date of the event"*. Time (a) znaš da je gledao, (b) angažuješ ga, (c) **podižeš show rate**.
- **Sistem umesto ad hoc-a** [3yAiVjcImQ4]: kvalifikacija forma → VSL 5–7 min → strukturisan poziv po skripti → snimanje poziva → **dodavanje novih FAQ pitanja u VSL i u skriptu** (kontinuirani optimization cycle).

### Outreach i kanali
- **Setter/closer split** [mRlSb0O5QNU]: dva čoveka SAMO bukiraju, vlasnik zatvara; zadržavaju proviziju na ono što donesu. Argument prema njima: *"Listen, you'll make more money if I'm selling than you are."* Podatak iz case-a: **vlasnik je konvertovao 10x bolje od svog sales tima** (*"if you work leads part-time, you were getting more than two guys full-time"*).
- **Outreach stack** [mRlSb0O5QNU]: (a) svaki dan **maksimalno iskoristiti platform limite** za DM-ove, klonirajući tačno ono što je vlasnik radio — *"if they're not doing that, then either they're the wrong people or you're not good at training"*; (b) **AI scrape kontakata za brojeve telefona** → (c) **feed u dialer**. DM-ovi imaju bolji response, pozivi daju volumen.
- **Jedan kanal, ne svi** [mRlSb0O5QNU]: *"how do we get really narrow on the one that you have the best LTV:CAC on? LinkedIn. So let's do that, and let's do way more of it."*
- **Speed kao diferencijator** [mRlSb0O5QNU]: *"If it takes you a week to get me a quote, I'm like: man, is this what it's going to be like working with these people?"* Umesto *"let's meet in five days"* → *"I'll have this to you by — what time tomorrow works best for you?"* *"That speed I can almost guarantee you will increase close rates."*

### Posle prodaje
- **Prvih 48 sati posle prodaje** odlučuje da li klijent veruje firmi dugoročno [JE2_7elAcxM, q32-l3Yoqg4]: *"people are making their decision about whether they believe in you as a company **within the first 48 hours after the sale**... and it's usually at that point that people fuck up and just ignore them."* Protivmera: **personalizovani video** (*"Hey Johnny, saw you just signed up today, super pumped — you're meeting with Heather tomorrow, she'll get you kicked off, and look out for a special gift in the mail"*) + **rukom pisana čestitka**.

## Menu close, cash flow quadrant i sales choreography
[-UvmyixFszw]

**4 načina da povećaš prihod po kupcu, ugrađena u "sales choreography":**
1. **Products** — fizički proizvod visoke marže uz uslugu.
2. **Add-on services** + **"menu close"** (pokaži ceo meni usluga za istog avatara).
3. **Prepay / financing** — treće lice za finansiranje, ili ~**10% popusta** za prepay.
4. **Continuity** — pretplata uvedena kroz **"down-selling the upsell"**: isto što su dobijali, ali recurring i sa popustom.

**Choreographed handoff** — svaki deo firme zna da mu je zadatak predaja štafete: sale 1 → sale 2 → sale 3 → sale 4.

**Reverse-engineering do $1.000+ po showu** (polazna tačka $19 new-client special):
- 50% close na paket od **$2.000** → **$1.000** po showu
- +**$200** proizvoda → **$1.200**
- **2/5** dodaju uslugu od **$1.000** → +$400 → **$1.600**
- **2/5** uzmu continuity **$200/mes** uz **10% mesečni churn** (≈$2.000 svaki) → +$800 → **$2.400 po osobi koja je ušla na free/$19 ponudu.**

[JE2_7elAcxM, StVqS0jD7Ls, okA9Yt2KZuk, bgmh7p1s7zs, oi7bnS8uyJM, q32-l3Yoqg4, 2lA_A8BGRRs, cy2k1GdA-9o, spXH1kJ6q-Q, OmDv6-651SM, NcD2t9qt-fM, mRlSb0O5QNU]

### Dnevni trening: 60 minuta dnevno × 5 dana nedeljno
(njegov tim i **dvaput dnevno**) [okA9Yt2KZuk, JE2_7elAcxM, q32-l3Yoqg4]:
- **25 min** — čitanje skripte i pitanja **naglas sa ispravnim tonalitetom**; **ako pogreše, kreću ispočetka.**
- **5 min** — drill obstacle overcomes (*"I need to think about it"*, *"I need to talk to my partner/spouse"*, *"this is expensive"*, *"that's a lot of money"*).
- **ostatak / druga polovina (30 min)** — slušanje snimaka poziva, game tape review. *"As a closer you've got to talk and you've got to listen — you've got to train both skills."*

### Šta se uči napamet
**Samo dva bloka** [JE2_7elAcxM, okA9Yt2KZuk, q32-l3Yoqg4]: (a) **30-sekundne anegdotske priče / priče o proizvodu** za tri bulleta, (b) **obstacle overcomes**. Sve ostalo su pitanja i miljokazi, ne skript. *"Those are the things you're in the **red zone** with — that's not where you start thinking about what you're going to say."* Njegova knjiga ima **35 strana obstacle-overcome-a u apendiksu** [q32-l3Yoqg4].

### Trening na prospektu, ne na proizvodu
[q32-l3Yoqg4, 42SOlVhDwWE, WrCt0R3FBFs]: *"when you're training salespeople, you don't train them on the product — you train them on the prospect."*

### Role play i drill tehnike
- **10–20–30 ponavljanja jedne rečenice** dok je *"ne diše"* [JE2_7elAcxM]. *"If your salesman is not talking back to you in the script during your one-on-ones, you're wasting time."*
- **Tri tehnike drilovanja** [2lA_A8BGRRs, cy2k1GdA-9o]: (1) role play svako jutro; (2) radi **samo jedan deo skripte dnevno**; (3) kad ispraviš grešku, teraj ih da **ponove ispravno 3 puta zaredom**. *"No one can improve multiple things at the same time."*
- **Memorizacija skripte** [spXH1kJ6q-Q]: crtaš po jednu reč dok cela stranica ne bude crna → *"you've said the script for as many times as there are words in the script."* Norma prekidanja u roleplayu: **50–60 prekida do kraja skripte.**
- **Ceo 1:1 je role play** [mRlSb0O5QNU]: *"Just saying it at them will do literally nothing. You have to get them to try so that you can correct them."*
- Tempo [spXH1kJ6q-Q]: **role play svaki dan, svaki član tima; 1-na-1 sa game tape nedeljno.**

### 14-dnevni onboarding
[2lA_A8BGRRs]: novi prodavac ceo dan sluša dobre snimljene pozive (*"bathing in good sales"*) — cilj **~40 poziva**; softver pokazuje da li je stvarno gledao. Ako nemaš dobre pozive: *"go and sell and do it well, and then record those calls."* Roleplay svako jutro. Kad prođe roleplay test → **pola rasporeda**, sastanci **2x dnevno.**

### Struktura 1h coaching sastanka (30/5/25)
[2lA_A8BGRRs]: **30 min pre** — slušaš 1–2 njegova poziva na **2x brzinu** i pišeš beleške; poziv odmah posle; **5 min** — saopštiš **samo 1–2 stvari sa najvećim uticajem**; **25 min** — roleplay sa tim izmenama, drilling konkretnih prigovora.

### Game tape review
- **Dnevno** [JE2_7elAcxM]: prodavac **PRVI kritikuje sebe**, tim onda kaže šta je bilo dobro; uvek završiti sa **jednom** stvari za popravku. **Jedna stvar odjednom** (golf analogija: 6 ispravki = ne može da zamahne).
- **Feedback šablon** [bgmh7p1s7zs]: *"What went right? What went wrong? What are you going to do next time?"*
- **Na svakom 1-na-1 prodavac unapred tagira tri poziva: svoj najbolji, svoj najgori i svoj prosečan** [bgmh7p1s7zs]. Struktura revizije, uvek ista 4 tačke: gde si **razjasnio problem**, gde si **izneo bol**, gde si **tražio prodaju**, gde si **prevazišao prigovore**.
- **Gledaj snimke SEBE kad si bio "hot"**, ne random snimke [OmDv6-651SM]. *"When you are hot, those are the videos you should watch."* Ako nemaš svoje — gledaj tuđe snimke ljudi koji su bili hot. Isto [JE2_7elAcxM]: **snimi svoje pozive kad si na hot streak-u** — to je tvoj trening materijal kad zahladi.
- **Record all your sales calls** [NcD2t9qt-fM]: (1) kad padneš iz hot streaka, vraćaš se na snimke i vidiš gde si pauzirao, koje si šale rekao, kad si tražio prodaju; (2) najlakši način da treniraš nove ljude.
- **Snimaj SVE pozive** (Gong) [JE2_7elAcxM, okA9Yt2KZuk]: talk vs. listen ratio, broj pitanja, AI analiza. *"Everyone's got mouse tracking on their pages — where's the mouse tracking on the calls?"* Snimanje je (1) usklađenost sa propisima i (2) instrument disciplinovanja skripte.

## Izgradnja, vođenje i skaliranje prodajnog tima

### Šest C za skaliranje (5 C's / 6 C's)
[JE2_7elAcxM, StVqS0jD7Ls, bgmh7p1s7zs, oi7bnS8uyJM]: **Closer sequence · Consistent daily training + Conviction · Call recordings · Communication cycles/feedback · Cuts (cut the fat fast) · Competition + career path.**

### Sales team scaling = Recruit → Train → Manage
[WrCt0R3FBFs]: (1) **recruit** — *"championships are won in the draft"*; (2) **train** — **oko prospekta, ne oko proizvoda**; (3) **manage** — oko **conviction**.

### Regrutacija i intervju
- **Grupni intervjui** umesto skrininga CV-jeva; zapošljavaj po **najmanjem skill deficitu** [JE2_7elAcxM].
- **Šta se testira na intervjuu (5 stvari)** [2lA_A8BGRRs, spXH1kJ6q-Q]: (1) **brzina odgovora** — *"you can test work ethic by how quickly they get back to you, because you are a lead to them"*; (2) **inteligencija**; (3) **sposobnost slušanja** — traži **ambiverta**, ne "motor mouth"; (4) socijalna svest; (5) **coachability** — testira se roleplayom: daš feedback i tražiš drugi pokušaj. Kriterijum: **prima li feedback bez ega i da li se popravlja u drugom pokušaju.**
- **Kratak ciklus za sales hire** [AGCtZmgJ1JA]: *"I have a very short allowing-for-people-to-fail-at-sales cycle... I've never had a killer salesperson who didn't do pretty well the first week. We turn through this quickly, but as a result the team is just killers."*

### Organizaciona struktura
**Tri-nivoska "Christmas tree" org (baseball analogija)** [okA9Yt2KZuk]:
1. **BDR** ("Double A") — prvi kontakt, hladni pozivi. Zamenjuje reklamu: *"if you replace a BDR mentally with what an advertisement used to do, that's exactly how this functions."*
2. **SDS** ("AAA") — kvalifikuje lead i **isporučuje vrednost** koju je BDR obećao. Kod njih su to skoro isključivo **bivši klijenti** (domain expertise).
3. **BC** ("the bigs") — closer. (+ **četvrti tim samo za ascensions / prodaju $40k proizvoda**.)
Piramida: baza BDR-ova hrani upola manje SDS-ova, koji hrane upola manje BC-ova.

**Live "handshake" transfer umesto zakazivanja** [okA9Yt2KZuk]: SDS na kraju svog calla **poziva closera uživo na speakerphone**, sa skriptiranim dijalogom koji prospekt čuje. Cilj tog poziva NIJE prodaja nego rapport + zakazivanje. Rešili su problem no-showa (*"we tried three-way text, we tried a bunch of things"* — samo live je proradilo). **SDS call: 45 min = 30 min vrednosti + 15 min tranzicije** (*"can I switch hats for a second?"*).

**Razdvajanje** [oi7bnS8uyJM]: **outbound i inbound razdvojeni**, i u svakom **setters odvojeni od closers** — jer su prospekti različiti.

### Career ladder / milestone sistem
[okA9Yt2KZuk, oi7bnS8uyJM, JE2_7elAcxM]
- Svaki nivo ima **4 podnivoa** — Junior → Associate → (plain) → Senior. Napredovanje po **objektivnom kvalifikatoru**, ne diskreciji. **"You don't get promoted, you get the ability to be promoted."** Relegacija radi u oba smera.
- **Prodavac ima 6–8 nedelja "steama"** pre nego što klone → svaki milestone je kalibrisan na **~8 nedelja produkcije** (junior → 50 showed appointments; sledeći 100, itd.).
- **Povećanje plate po milestone-u je namerno beznačajno:** **$50 → $55 po showu.** *"The pay bump is virtually inconsequential... the title is more important than the cash."*
- Verzija po zatvorenim dilovima [oi7bnS8uyJM, JE2_7elAcxM]: milestones **50 / 100 / 150 zatvorenih poslova → komisija $500 → $525 → $550** po dilu.
- **OTE po nivou** [okA9Yt2KZuk]: BDR ≈ **$40k**; SDS ≈ **$55–70k**; BC/closer ≈ **$120–225k**. Zašto neko radi za $40k: jer vidi jasan put do **$200k za 9–12 meseci**, bez diplome, *"pure meritocracy and the numbers don't lie."*
- **Promocija u closera: "try or die" — 10 dealova / 2 nedelje** [okA9Yt2KZuk]. *"I have yet to find a killer who doesn't close in the first two weeks. Not once. Tons of guys who start hot and then fizzle, but no one who starts cold and then gets good."* Ako closer padne ispod ~**25% close rate** → degradacija na AAA.

### Kompenzacija
- **Ratchet komisije** [JE2_7elAcxM]: **50% close = zadržiš posao; 60% = pohvale; 70% = komisija 10%→15%; 80% = 25%.** *"Winners win."* **Ukupna komisija ispod 10% revenue** (sa sales direktorom = **9%**; **20% je previše**).
- **Plaćaj po naplaćenom kešu (cash collected), ne po zatvorenom** [cy2k1GdA-9o]. Kod $10.000 proizvoda sa 5 rata po $2.000 → closer dobija **$200 ako je rata, $1.000 ako je paid-in-full = 5x više.** Kompenzacija: **10% od naplaćenog.**
- **Kompenzacija = novac + ne-novac** [JE2_7elAcxM]: leaderboard, javno priznanje, parking mesto, kape sa prugama po **100/1000 prodaja**, career path **50/100/150 deals → $500 → $525 → $550 po dealu.**
- **Plaćaj i settere komisijom** vezanom za throughput biznisa, ne za komfor closera [JE2_7elAcxM].

### Kultura, takmičenja i vidljivost
- **Tri stuba kulture** [spXH1kJ6q-Q]: (1) **leaderboard koji je vizuelan i uvek ažuran**; (2) **jasni KPI + bonus/takmičenje na svakih 30–42 dana** — **metrika koja je najbolje radila u svim portfolio firmama je CASH UP FRONT**; (3) **rezanje donjih 10% kvartalno** — *"if you're not cutting, you're communicating to the team that it's okay to suck."*
- **Takmičenja: ciklusi od 6 nedelja** [JE2_7elAcxM, okA9Yt2KZuk] — *"long enough that they believe they can accomplish something, but short enough they believe it's going to happen soon."* **Timovi, ne pojedinci**, da ostane kamaraderija. **[protivrečno]** veličina tima: **4 čoveka, 4 tima po 4, draft tako da se najbolji spari sa najslabijim** [JE2_7elAcxM, oi7bnS8uyJM] vs. **timovi od 3 čoveka** [bgmh7p1s7zs].
- **Grupni cilj ~10–15%** iznad baze [okA9Yt2KZuk].
- **Budžet za nagradu: 25% jedne mesečne plate po osobi** — ako prodavci zarađuju $7.000, ~**$1.500 po čoveku** za dvo-/trodnevni izlet; ukupno ~**$5.000 za tim.** *"It's not expensive, five grand, but it is worth every dollar."* Korišćena nagrada: vikend u Vegasu [okA9Yt2KZuk].
- **Kudos za najviši show-up rate: ~$500 nedeljno flat** [JE2_7elAcxM].
- **Javni leaderboard** ažuriran svakodnevno; svaka prodaja generiše notifikaciju svima. Uživo = zvono; remote = **Slack nit gde svi šalju sliku ili GIF zvona.** Poenta je vidljivost aktivnosti i momentum, ne rangiranje [okA9Yt2KZuk].
- **Slack kudos hack** [JE2_7elAcxM]: ne dozvoli ljudima da sami objave zatvaranje — **Zap između CRM-a i Slacka objavljuje *procesiranu* prodaju.**
- **Dnevni huddle sadržaj = testimonijali** [okA9Yt2KZuk]: *"All you do is share testimonials, so they remember why they're selling. They get beat up every single day with nos."*

### Cuts
- **Cut fast** [JE2_7elAcxM]: ako u **14 dana** ne zatvara — pusti ga. *"Lakše je uzeti šesticu i napraviti devetku nego dvojku i napraviti peticu."* / *"It's much easier to take a six and get them to a nine than to take a two and get him to a five."* [okA9Yt2KZuk] / *"it'll take you three times as much time to get someone from a 2 to a 5 than from a 7 to a 9."* [WrCt0R3FBFs]
- **Cut the bottom 10% redovno** [okA9Yt2KZuk]: *"It's the number one thing that will drive sales teams. Even if the team is going okay, when you cut the bottom you'll see a **30% jump in productivity every time**."* Logika: *"if guys can't close in the first week to two weeks, they ain't closing."*
- **Performance ladder** [2lA_A8BGRRs, spXH1kJ6q-Q]: 14 dana trening → pola rasporeda → kvota → pun raspored → **ispod KPI dve nedelje → PIP** (nazad u trainee status) → još dve nedelje bez popravke → otkaz. **Saopštava se pri zapošljavanju.**

### Kadenca komunikacije
- **Dnevni huddle-i, nedeljni 1-na-1, mesečni pregled** [bgmh7p1s7zs]. Prošireno [spXH1kJ6q-Q]: dnevni jutarnji huddle (**obavezan za trainee i PIP**), **end-of-day huddle** (check-in + čišćenje pipeline-a), **nedeljni 1-na-1 (1 sat po čoveku)**, mesečni, **kvartalno sečenje**.
- **Daily drive umesto weekly** [mRlSb0O5QNU]: ujutru — *"What's your plan? How many touch points are you going to do? Walk me through the leads you're working. What kind of prep have you done so you can personalize them?"*; kraj dana — *"How did the conversations go? Where are we at with pipeline?"*
- **Struktura weekly 1:1, 3 nivoa tim redom** [mRlSb0O5QNU]: (1) **personal** — *"how you doing? if your mom just died, let's talk about that, because you're not thinking about your KPIs if you just got dumped"*; (2) **short-term** — *"you're messing this up in the intro, in this part of the scripting"*; (3) **long-term/rast**, samo ako ruši KPI.
- **Manager tracking** [spXH1kJ6q-Q]: tab po prodavcu sa **datiranim listama "2 stvari za ovu nedelju"**; sledeće nedelje: *"we said these two things, did you do it? Did it result in you making more deals?"*

### Menadžment prodaje
- **Ne promovišite najboljeg closera u menadžera** [JE2_7elAcxM, spXH1kJ6q-Q]. Sales direktor: even-keeled, ponizan, servant, *"Energizer Bunny of positive vibes"*, metric-driven. *"Sales managers tend to be more giving... less emotional highs and lows. They need to be the rock of the team."*
- **"Sales management is a skill of tolerance."** *"The person who tolerates the most is the one who has the weakest team."* [spXH1kJ6q-Q]
- **No prima donnas** [JE2_7elAcxM] — čak i ako jedan radi 3x, ako ne poštuje proces ruši kulturu i ukupni output pada.
- **Autoritet procesa nad ljudima** [spXH1kJ6q-Q]: *"I would rather have a team of all eights closing on a 10 out of 10 process"* nego jedan star bez procesa.
- **Delegation vs. abdication** [4GQLJjH9-oA]: *"When you hand stuff off and stats go down, you have abdicated, not delegated."*
- **"Better" > "New"** [4GQLJjH9-oA] — poboljšati postojeći dokazani sistem je risk-adjusted bolji potez.

### Alokacija leadova
**Ruthless prioritization** [4GQLJjH9-oA, cy2k1GdA-9o, StVqS0jD7Ls]: najbolji leadovi i najbolji termini najboljim closerima. *"If your best closers get the best leads you'll sell more; if your worst closers get the worst leads you'll lose the least."* **Novajlije treniraju na najlošijim leadovima.** Ispod KPI-ja → **prepolovi im kalendar.**

### Tri tipa prodavca — Dogs / Horses / Tigers
(od Mike Arcea / Mike Ary) [JE2_7elAcxM, cy2k1GdA-9o]: **psi** umiru ako ih ne hraniš i maziš; **konji** galopiraju samo dok ih bičuješ pa padaju u kas; **tigar** ubije zeca koji mu pređe put i kad je sit — iz sporta. **"Hire tigers." / "You want tigers."** Uz to: **"kill for sport"** — tretiraj i nekvalifikovan lead kao besplatnu praksu.
