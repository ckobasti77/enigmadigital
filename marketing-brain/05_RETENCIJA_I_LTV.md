## 1. Centralna teza — zašto retencija
`[3P1XjUvo1b4, K8MFC9t7snY]`

- "The way to create a fortune in business is to only sell things that people do not stop buying."
- "You don't get customers to make sales. **You make sales to get customers.**"
- "We only transact in order to create the relationship so that we can have revenue again and again."
- **"Reorder business, not the selling business":** "The big problem that most people have when they are starting their business is that they are in the selling business, not in the reorder business."
- **"Wall of glue" model:** biznis je zid od lepka na koji duvaš ljude; ako niko ne otpada, baza se akumulira bez povećanja akvizicije. "You've got this glue and you're blowing all this traffic at this glue and it just sticks."
- **Problem nije broj kupaca:** "Most of the time [more customers] is not the problem. It's usually because the customers you have keep leaving — and that's the real problem." `[KQuyQpFANpA]`

**Matematika akumulacije** `[K8MFC9t7snY]`
- 1 kupac nedeljno koji ne odlazi = **500 kupaca koji plaćaju mesečno posle 10 godina**.
- **10 novih kupaca mesečno bez odliva = 600 mesečno posle 5 godina.**
- Bez retencije: "If you sell 100 people this month, you have to sell 100 people next month just to maintain."

**Retencija kao marketinška poluga**
- LTV 10x veći od konkurencije uz isti CAC → "Who's going to win in a bidding auction for who can buy the next customer on Facebook or YouTube? You win every time." / "When you get customers who do not leave you, you can afford any marketing. You can afford to outspend everyone." `[3P1XjUvo1b4]`
- "If your cost to acquire stays the same but you double your LTV, **you can sometimes market 10 times more, not just twice as much.**" `[yPDQCfrwh8E]`
- **Benchmark ambicije:** "If you sell 100 clients a month B2B, you should be able to build that into a $100 million enterprise. You have the sales velocity" — ako ne gubiš klijente. `[3P1XjUvo1b4]`
- SaaS metrike važe i za servise: "it's just software doing the job that service is supposed to be doing." `[3P1XjUvo1b4]`

**Kao investitor / due diligence**
- Prvo što gleda: **koliko prošlogodišnjih klijenata ti i dalje plaća.** "If we're not keeping anyone, then what's the point in adding gas to this fire? We're just letting more people know we suck." `[afbP6sB_Atc]`
- Pitanja pre akvizicije: "Is there a way that we can get someone to buy this and never stop paying for it? Is there a way that we can make this so good that the moment their card changes they call us ahead of time?" `[3P1XjUvo1b4]`
- Pitanje portfolio kompanijama: "If tomorrow you could not market anything… and any new customer could only come from referral and word of mouth — how differently would you treat them? How different would their experience be? And how much better would your product be?" `[3P1XjUvo1b4]`
- **Churn je razlog zašto se biznis ne može prodati:** "This is what makes your business unsellable." `[y3T4GqoH0Us]`
- **Revenue retention > akvizicija** `[afbP6sB_Atc]`: dve firme, obe 300 klijenata u 3. godini — jedna gubi 100% svake godine, druga zadržava sve. "There's a reason that people in the information / direct response space can't grow past a certain amount — they have basically the marketing team of a $300 million business but they're only doing $30 million, because they can only do 30 that year and lose everybody every single year."

## 2. Recurring vs. reoccurring i "imaš proizvod, nemaš biznis"
`[3P1XjUvo1b4, K8MFC9t7snY, F84olnKkseM]`

- **Recurring** = pretplata. **Reoccurring** = kupac se vraća i ponovo poručuje bez pretplate.
- "Facebook is a massive company. They do not have a recurring revenue model. They have a reoccurring revenue model — I go to the Facebook store today, I buy some eyeballs, and tomorrow I might go back again."
- Coca-Cola nema pretplatu ali zna da ćeš kupiti opet. `[F84olnKkseM]`
- **Dijagnostika:** "if people are not buying your product again, figure out why"; ako industrija to ne dozvoljava, gledaj milijarderske firme u tvom prostoru — "what have they solved that you haven't"; ako takve firme nema — **"not all businesses are worth owning".** `[F84olnKkseM]`

**"You have a product, you don't have a business"** `[F84olnKkseM]`
Prodaješ jedan widget jednom, jednoj osobi: nema backend-a, upsell-a, recurring-a, revenue retention-a. Tehnički jeste biznis, ali **nije asset** — "a slightly fancier, higher leverage job for yourself".

Recurring revenue je "**the single greatest way to increase your enterprise value**". `[jOM0m34dxz4]`

## 3. Model > Method
`[3P1XjUvo1b4]`

- "People will definitely buy the method and then never need the method." Metod je one-and-done; **model** može da sadrži mnogo metoda i evoluira.
- Franšize prodaju modele: "the marketing will change — it was Groupon, then email, then Facebook ads, Instagram ads, YouTube ads… media will change but the model will remain the same."

## 4. Tri faze rasta (Mark Roberge)
`[3P1XjUvo1b4, K8MFC9t7snY]`

Mark Roberge, rani HubSpot lider, "took them from zero to $100 million".

1. **Promotion (0 → $1M):** cilj je samo dobiti kupce. "We're not worried about pricing… priced high enough that people will care, but not so high that we're trying to maximize profit."
2. **Product ($1M → $10M):** optimizuješ dok ljudi ne prestanu da odlaze. "In theory we should never really leave here until we have figured this piece out."
3. **Scale:** tek kad proizvod drži, marketing počinje da se kompaundira.

Isti redosled u `K8MFC9t7snY`: (1) promote just to get consumption → (2) improvement loop (**NE** marketuj jače i **NE** pravi novi proizvod) → (3) make it harder to leave, pa gas na marketing.

**Kritika:** "This unfortunately is where most of the internet marketing community stops — they learn how to promote and they think that that's how they're going to make money… You'll make some money, but you won't build a fortune that way."

## 5. Customer surplus
`[3P1XjUvo1b4]`

Razlika između vrednosti koju klijent dobija i cene koju plaća — **najveći faktor lepljivosti**.
- "Price is what you pay. Value is what you get. And that discrepancy is going to be the customer surplus."
- **Test:** klijent te **zove kad mu kartica ne prođe** (Wi-Fi, telefon, Netflix test).
- **Surplus se pravi podizanjem vrednosti, ne spuštanjem cene:** "Most people try and cut their prices in order to get there when in reality we should be increasing the value." Jedini izuzetak: proizvodi sa ~nula troška reprodukcije (Dropbox: "virtually zero dollars to reproduce more storage").
- Odnos vrednost:cena ~**10:1** — "I've got a $500,000 house, I'll sell it to you for 50 grand, and I learn how to build it for 10." / "If this price is a million and they get 10 million of value, they will pay you for it."
- Netflix za $12/mes i Spotify za $8/mes i dalje imaju otkazivanja — kod siromašnijih kupaca **odnos vrednost:cena mora biti VEĆI nego kod bogatih**. `[thDTmy7VGIw]`
- **8 C's za stickiness** se pominju u `3P1XjUvo1b4` (referiše svoj Netflix video) ali nisu tamo nabrojani — razrađeni su u §12.

## 6. Churn — definicija i matematika

**Definicija** `[yPDQCfrwh8E]`: "the percentage of customers that leave from this month to next month. If I start with 100 customers and 95% renew, I have 5% churn."

**Formule**
- **Godišnja retencija = (1 − mesečni churn)^12** `[y3T4GqoH0Us]`
  - **3% mesečno** (top 1–2% teretana) → 0.97^12 = **69% zadržano, 31% izgubljeno godišnje**
  - **9% mesečno** (prosek industrije) → 0.91^12 = **32% zadržano, 68% izgubljeno**
- **LTV = mesečna cena ÷ mesečni churn** `[y3T4GqoH0Us]`: $100/mes ÷ 0.03 = **$3.300 (33 meseca)**; ÷ 0.09 = **$1.100 (11 meseci)** → **3x više prihoda po klijentu**, a profit nesrazmerno više jer je CAC isti.
- **Dve formule za LTGP** `[jzKpAtzKQ54]`: **cena × broj meseci** ILI **cena ÷ churn**. ($2.000/mes × 5 meseci = $10.000; ili $2.000 ÷ 20% = $10.000.)
- **Equilibrium formula** `[LvHDT0ZxSmw]`: broj korisnika u ravnoteži = **mesečni signup ÷ churn rate**. Primer: 500 ÷ 10% = **5.000 korisnika** kao plafon.
- **Cap formula prihoda** `[-UvmyixFszw]`: broj novih klijenata mesečno × LTV = mesečni plafon. Primer: 20 klijenata × $2.000 LTV = **$40.000/mes**. Ako biznis ne raste, kriv je tačno jedan od ta dva broja.
- Izračun plafona za agenciju `[jqo0lVveh98]`: 10 prodaja/mes × $2k × 5 meseci churn = **~$100k/mes i onda staneš** ("you will stall"). Zatim moraš ili povećati broj prodatih jedinica ili LTV.

**Nelinearnost — mala promena churn-a je ogromna promena LTV-a**
- $100/mesec i **10% churn = 10 meseci = $1.000 LTV**; spuštanje na **5% udvostručuje LTV na $2.000**. "It doesn't seem like a big difference — 10 to 5" ali je duplo. `[sPkMHh8zTMI]`
- **Churn 20% → 10% NIJE poboljšanje od 10%, nego UDVOSTRUČENJE LTV-a.** "The smaller the number, the bigger the change by percentages." `[-j8_YCWZ05Q]`
- Efekat na ad spend `[y3T4GqoH0Us]`: uz **3:1 LTV:CAC**, sa CAC $300 plafoniraš na ~**$1.000 dnevno** ad spenda; sa dozvoljenim CAC-om od **$600–650 možeš do $100.000 dnevno.**

**Hipotetički LTV za mlade biznise** `[jzKpAtzKQ54]`
"I had 10 customers last month; 30 days later how many of those 10 are still with me? If nine are, you have 10% churn — divide your price by 10%, which is the same as multiplying by 10, so the average duration is 10 months." Ne treba čekati godine.

**Kohortno merenje, ne agregatno**
- **Repeat-purchase benchmark** `[qsXxckCbci0]`: "If you can get **60–70% to buy again after the first purchase**, you likely have a product that's pretty strong" — mereno **po kohorti, ne agregatno** (brz rast razvodnjava agregat).
- **Kohortni churn** `[-j8_YCWZ05Q]`: meseci 1–3 = **20%+ churn**; posle **dana 90** pada na **~10% ili manje** (prepolovljen); do **meseca 6** pada na **~2%** = **5x povećanje LTV-a.**
  - **Mehanizmi po pragu:** **mesec 1** = aktivacija, onboarding, postavljanje očekivanja, prodaja pravih obećanja na front-endu, svakodnevno prisustvo. **Dan 90** = mora da isporuči neki ishod / promenu ponašanja. **Mesec 6** = povezivanje sa drugim ljudima u zajednici.
  - **Upozorenje na merenje:** ako je biznis mlađi od 90 dana (ili 6 meseci), churn ti je **veštački naduvan.** "Don't overhaul your entire business because your month-one churn sucks."
- **Prati prosečnu dužinu trajanja klijenta mesečno:** "3.7 months → 4.2 → 4.7 → 5.6". `[sPkMHh8zTMI]`

**Merenje je preduslov**
- "They actually track it and focus on the metric, which 95% of people don't even do, which is why most people are poor." `[sPkMHh8zTMI, y3T4GqoH0Us]`
- "Metrics can be an intervention in and of themselves. If I want someone to lose weight, I don't even have to tell them anything to do besides weighing themselves every morning." / **"If you don't track, you don't care."** / "If you don't measure a metric, you're not going to improve it." `[afbP6sB_Atc, yPDQCfrwh8E]`
- Paul Graham: "You can solve almost every business problem by talking to your customer more." `[afbP6sB_Atc]`

## 7. Churn benchmarks po industriji

**Teretane / fitness**
- **Ciljni churn: 1–2% mesečno maksimalno**, umesto "10 or 20% which is what most people have". `[3P1XjUvo1b4]`
  - **[protivrečno]** `[XC_lklN9KmE]`: **churn ispod 1–2% mesečno je praktično nedostižno u service biznisu**; ako jeste toliko nisko, rasteš čisto na organskim preporukama — "but if you want to be massive you need to grow off everything: organic, outbound, paid acquisition." Razrešenje koje izvori nude: 1–2% je aspiracija/gornji ideal, dok je operativni benchmark za top izvođače **ispod 3% mesečno** (uzorak za "Five Horsemen of Retention": teretane sa **ispod 3% mesečnog churna kroz 6–8 meseci** `[UGEc9-7X3OQ]`).
- **Industrijski prosek churn korišćen u primerima: 10% mesečno** `[XC_lklN9KmE]`; **9% mesečno** kao prosek industrije `[y3T4GqoH0Us]`.
- **Planet Fitness / Crunch ($10/mesec) imaju 5–6% mesečnog churn-a** `[rj7rzOdj84A]` — jeftina cena ne rešava retenciju.
- **Gym Launch: churn sa 11% na 6% mesečno** posle implementacije svih 7 taktika.

**Softver / SaaS**
- **Softver se meri GODIŠNJE:** "if you have 10% or more annual churn on your software, you're [screwed] — you're just a service business." `[pw1PcHfYP2w]`
- **Licensing i software:** treba **80%+ godišnja retencija** licencijata/korisnika za atraktivan multiple ("six plus multiple on the exit"); ispod toga "no one's even going to consider you a software, they're just going to consider you a normal service that just has some tech element". `[O1As2zxy0es]`
- **VSB SaaS benchmark** `[rc7cxL7ql7Y]`: **3% mesečno je odlično; 33% je "11 times worse"; realan cilj ~10%** (nivo Shopify-ja). Deo je **structural churn** — inherentan segmentu, "not to lose sleep over".
- **Shopify: ~60% kupaca ostaje godišnje** — i to je best-in-class, jer služe prosumere. `[thDTmy7VGIw]`

**Private equity / prodajivost**
- **80% godišnje retencije** je ono što traži većina private equity firmi `[f_ceDZqhKbw]`. **13% mesečnog churn-a = zadržavaš manje od 20% klijenata na kraju godine** → firma praktično nema enterprise value.

**Zajednice / membership (Skool podaci, 22.265.736 korisnika)** `[-j8_YCWZ05Q]`
- **Prosek: 80% mesečna retencija (20% churn).** Najbolje grupe: **<10% churn (>90% retencije).** Loše: **<70% retencije.**
- Prag za akciju: "if fewer than 7 out of 10 people want to stay on a monthly basis, you probably need to work on retention more than anything else — it'll be the highest ROI lever."

**Agencije / SMB retaineri** `[jqo0lVveh98]`
- **SMB agencijski retaineri na $1.500–3.000/mes: prosečan stick 4–6 meseci.** "You're right in the sweet spot of churn."
- **Low-ticket SMB: $300–500/mes → stick 30–40 meseci.** LTV sličan kao kod skupljeg retainera, ali **CAC ostaje mnogo niži** i sales velocity je mnogo veći. Primeri: "I can get you ranked on the first three of maps in your local area and I charge **$400–500 a month**", review management, SEO. **Prag: "$400 a month or less for an SMB if it's a nuisance style."**
- **WaaS firma:** **$20M revenue, $450/mes po klijentu, 29 meseci prosečan stick** ("It's usually 30 to 40; the highest I've seen was 38 for this type of business"), **$3,6M EBITDA — "lowish"**, 100% rasta iz outbound cold callinga.

**E-commerce / pretplata**
- **ButcherBox** `[orMbq2LtzKE]`: **~430.000 aktivnih pretplatnika**, **churn ~3% mesečno** = ~12.000–12.900 otkaza mesečno → **treba ~400 novih kupaca DNEVNO samo da bi stajali u mestu**. Retencija posle godinu dana **~60%**. **NPS 63.** Miks kanala: ~30% referral, ~30% Meta ads, ~20% organic, ~10% affiliate/influencer.

**LTV benchmark**
- **Starbucks: LTV po kupcu $14.099** `[jzKpAtzKQ54]` (u `reisEL_D7xc` navodi ~$14.000) — "when you boil hot water and you sell it for six bucks you make a lot of money". **38.000 lokacija**, gotovo sve korporativne.

## 8. Churn je lagging indicator — leading indikatori
`[yPDQCfrwh8E, afbP6sB_Atc]`

- **"Churn is a lagging indicator — you don't fix churn, churn is a result.** You have to do things **before** the bad result happens."
- Primeri leading indikatora: klijent nije došao u teretanu mesec dana; korisnik se nije logovao na Netflix. Osnovni leading indicator = **konzumacija / atendencija**.
- "**One of the biggest unlocks in a business, especially in a recurring revenue business, is figuring out what are the leading indicators that get someone to leave or stay, and then driving towards those.**"
- **"You're not going to fix churn with one thing. It's going to be many small things — many golden BBs, no silver bullets."** `[neTSqOAMgao]` / **"With retention it's not the bullet — it's all of these things put together."** `[y3T4GqoH0Us]`
- **"No one who has bad churn stops selling."** — signal da proizvod nije loš koliko klijent misli. `[jqo0lVveh98]`
- **Churn dijagnostika** `[kloJJeiysxg]`: "Talk to your customers. Talk to the people that cancelled, talk to the people that stayed, and figure out what differences they have. If the people that stayed did these things — emphasize those."

## 9. LTV / LTGP — definicija i poluge
`[jzKpAtzKQ54, i7bLRKwKSms, MwhsM7qijy8, hHkdbr6_JJs, Q2VUuUGfpNY]`

**Samo dva načina rasta, na najvišem nivou:** "Number one, you get more customers. Number two, you make them worth more. That's it." / "**you get more customers, or you make those customers worth more. That's it.**"

**LTV → LTGP (Lifetime Gross Profit)** `[Q2VUuUGfpNY]`
"Lifetime value is a very loosely defined term... what I'm going to do is redefine this as lifetime gross profit." Primer: obrok se prodaje za **$10**, isporuka košta **$9** → zarada **$1**. Ako akvizicija košta **$5**, to izgleda kao "2:1" na prihod, ali stvarno je **$5 potrošeno da bi se zaradio $1 bruto profita.** LTGP nije net profit — "net profit is after you've paid rent, payroll... This is just the gross margin that comes in."

**Kanonske poluge** (izvori nabrajaju istu listu kao 8, 7 ili 6 poluga; ispod su objedinjene sa svim detaljima)

1. **Increase price** — najmoćnija. Ako isporuka košta $10 a naplaćuješ $20 → 50% margina, $10 GP. Podigneš na **$30** → **udvostručio si LTGP.** `[Q2VUuUGfpNY]`
2. **Decrease COGS / cost of fulfillment** — "invest in technology, invest in processes, invest in outsourcing... I deliver the same thing for less". Trošak sa **$9 na $2** automatizacijom → drastičan skok LTGP-a "but I didn't change anything besides decreasing the cost." `[Q2VUuUGfpNY]` Dva načina u uslužnom biznisu `[MwhsM7qijy8]`: (a) zadrži isti odnos coach:klijent ali **outsource-uj deo posla jeftinijoj radnoj snazi**; (b) zadrži iste ljude i **povećaj odnos** — 1:10 → 1:20 **prepolovljava cenu isporuke**.
3. **Increase purchase frequency / get them to buy MORE times** — u recurring modelu to je isto što i smanjenje churna.
4. **Cross-sell** — **susedna potreba**, ne bolja/veća verzija istog: burger → fries; burger uz Coke; "a burger with fries rather than a bigger burger". "**an adjacent need that I am solving**". Dizajniraj ga po **uskim grlima koja ljudi doživljavaju TOKOM izvršenja core ponude** `[MwhsM7qijy8]`. "**Oftentimes these cross-sells are actually where the majority of the profit happens**" → otvara **loss leader** strategiju. `[Q2VUuUGfpNY]`
5. **Upsell** — "selling the same thing but a **more valuable version**"; "either better of the same or more of the same, but the same category" (Honda → Lexus; 20oz → 32oz; sirloin/wagyu burger). "You'd be surprised — a lot of people just want more." `[MwhsM7qijy8, Q2VUuUGfpNY, jzKpAtzKQ54]`
6. **Sell higher quantity** — 2 burgera umesto 1. `[jzKpAtzKQ54]`
7. **Downsell** — pretvara "ne" u prodaju (junior Whopper). Najzapostavljenija: "all this traffic is coming to their site or getting on sales calls, but those people have needs too... **You're still paying for this traffic.**" Dva načina `[Q2VUuUGfpNY]`: (a) downsell na **tuđi proizvod** uz affiliate/referral proviziju; (b) **jeftinija verzija svoje usluge** sa nižim troškom isporuke — "done-for-you" → "done-with-you" → "do-it-yourself". Mentalni model: "**one person's trash is another person's treasure**" / "**what can I do with the sawdust?**" **Oprez** `[i7bLRKwKSms]`: downsell može kanibalizovati skupe kupce.
8. **Fewer / lesser version.** `[jzKpAtzKQ54]`
9. **Financing** — povlači keš unapred. `[i7bLRKwKSms]`
10. **Promeni uslove naplate.** `[i7bLRKwKSms]`

**Doslovna skraćena lista** `[jzKpAtzKQ54]`: "more, better, smaller, worse, cross-sell, different, increase price, decrease COGS."

**Kada se koristi:** 6 poluga kao **mentalna čeklista na 15–20-minutnom screening pozivu.** `[MwhsM7qijy8]`

**Aritmetika poluga**
- Klijent koji ostaje 3 meseca, zadržan još jedan = **+25% LTV.** `[MwhsM7qijy8]`
- Cross-sell: ako **20% kupaca uzme dodatak od $1.000/mes** na osnovnih $1.000/mes → prosečan mesečni prihod **$1.000 → $1.200 (+20%)**. `[MwhsM7qijy8]`
- **Portfolio primer: LTV 1,9x** (sa $2.000 na $3.800) → biznis otišao sa **$2M mesečno na $2M nedeljno.** `[y3T4GqoH0Us]`
- **"Tripling LTV by just getting them to buy more of the thing they just bought tends to always work... offering them to buy more of what they just bought almost always does."** `[mRlSb0O5QNU]`
- **"$3M → $10M ne zahteva ništa novo osim veće LTGP po klijentu"** — bolji proizvod, manji churn, optimizacija cene, ili jedan high-value dodatni service level. `[neTSqOAMgao]`

## 10. LTV stacking — front end / back end
`[jzKpAtzKQ54]`

Uzmi ponudu sa **najboljim LTGP:CAC** kao **front end** i **prestani da oglašavaš ostalo**; ostalo postaje **back end** u koji uzdižeš kupce, i **njegov CAC postaje nula**.

**Nova formula:** front end cena + (% konverzije u back end × back end cena).

**Hair extensions case:**
- Kurs $2.000, CAC ~$67 → **30:1**
- Business coaching $15.000, CAC ~$2.000 → **~7:1**
- Rešenje: prestani oglašavati coaching. Ako **20%** kupaca kursa kupi coaching: $2.000 + (20% × $15.000 = $3.000) = **$5.000 LTV** → ratio skače sa **30:1 na 75:1.**

## 11. 30-day cash, payback period i OPM
`[Cr0KdqZ954c, i7bLRKwKSms, reisEL_D7xc, -UvmyixFszw]`

- **Treći obavezan broj: 30-day cash** — koliko keša klijent donese u prvih 30 dana. Formalno = **payback period**.
- **Prag: 30-day cash : CAC ≥ 1:1.**
- **OPM mehanizam (Other People's Money)** — zašto baš 30 dana: "virtually every business can gain access to a credit line... **credit cards are all interest-free for the first 30 days**." Ako je 30-day cash ≥ CAC, akvizicija se finansira tuđim novcem bez kamate, a ti ostaješ sa kupcem i celim back-endom "for free". "This is why we've had such big growth — I don't actually need to use my own money to acquire customers."
- **Vizuelni model:** dan 0 → **−$200** (CAC); prva uplata možda samo **$50** (dan 1–7, još si negativan); do **dana 30** uz 1–2 upsella → **+$200**, CAC pokriven; dan 60/90/120 recurring = **čist back-end profit**.
- **Poluge za 30-day cash:** upsell, "some sort of liquidation thing", special offer u prvoj nedelji ili dve, ancillary products, affiliate/referral aranžmani.
- **CAC recovery pravilo** `[reisEL_D7xc]`: cilj je vratiti CAC u prvih 30 dana; ako ne ide, uvedi **jednokratni upfront upcharge** koji pokrije akviziciju.
- **Blended primer teretane** `[i7bLRKwKSms]`: **$600 unapred** (6-week challenge) + **~$200 suplemenata u prvih 48h** + **~$2.000 prepay za godinu u 3. nedelji** → **~$1.000 u prvih 30 dana** po kupcu, naspram industrijskog **$21 za 21 dan** modela.
- **Dva prozora** `[-UvmyixFszw]`: **koliko zaradiš u prvih 30 dana** (određuje koliko smeš da trošiš na akviziciju) vs **LTV** (određuje mesečni prihod).
- **Prepay kao offset CAC-a** `[jqo0lVveh98]`: "I would just see if you could get them to **prepay for the quarter** so you can offset CAC." Uz dovoljan cash flow: "**You'd be willing to go negative for a quarter in the acquisition knowing you're going to get 29 [months] on the back.**"

## 12. 8 C's of Recurring Revenue
`[jOM0m34dxz4]`

**Ključno pravilo: nije binarno** — "How recurring is your business is a better question." Svaki C je kontinuum.

1. **Consumption** — što više koriste, to duže plaćaju (Netflix, Spotify — bez ugovora).
2. **Collateral** — obezbedi prihod nečim što **držiš**: skladište, Dropbox/Drive (fajlovi), **CRM** (kontakti i podaci).
3. **Cost of switching** — vreme, novac, trud + **namerno pojačan sunk cost**. "if I told someone that they had to spend $5,000 one time to begin working with me, and then it was $100 a month, the likelihood that they would cancel would be significantly lower." **"The bigger the head, the longer the tail."**
4. **Choice (nedostatak alternativa)** — patenti, IP, jedinstven proces. Zato je **nišovanje** toliko vredno na startu.
5. **Control of the money flow** — naplata koja je **covert, ne overt** (Uber uzima proviziju pre isplate; država uzima porez pre nego što zaposleni vidi platu).
6. **Cause** — ljudi plaćaju jer se identifikuju sa idejom. "The product is the feeling that people get when they pay."
7. **Community** — **commitment & consistency**; cilj: "make people feel like they're leaving a community, not a business."
8. **Contracts that are enforceable** + collateral iza ugovora.

**Brojke i strukture**
- **2,5x povećanje LTV-a** biznisa sa fizičkim proizvodima **samo dodavanjem recurring komponente.**
- **Packaging test:** identična usluga od **$2.000/mes** koja "drži" 4 meseca → prepakovana u **"$12.000 program sa 6 rata od $2.000"** verovatno donosi 1–2 dodatna meseca → **2–3x veći lifetime gross profit samo od promene pozicioniranja.**
- **Preporučena struktura: 6-mesečni ugovor koji posle prelazi u mesečni.**
- "People are going to in general be more consistent with a commitment that they make... even if not all of them do." Uz iskrenu napomenu: "that may be harder on the sale, and this is part of business — balancing those things."

## 13. Mehanizmi zaključavanja

- **"For life" ponude** `[orMbq2LtzKE]`: besplatna slanina u prvoj kutiji → **"bacon for life"** (u svakoj kutiji dokle god si pretplatnik) → kasnije "ground beef for life". **Beneficija se gubi ako otkažeš.** Najstariji kupci imaju **6–7 naslaganih "for life" ponuda**: "Why would I ever go anywhere else to buy my meat?" Ekonomija: slanina košta **$4**, a dodavanje "bacon for life" spušta CAC za **više od $4** i produžava zadržavanje. Kasnije su ih i prodavali (**$50 za "ground beef for life"**) i naučili da ih ograniče: "we've gotten a lot smarter — it's for a year, not for life."
- **Kontrola toka novca u B2B** `[K8MFC9t7snY]`: "If you're in a B2B situation, you want to add in collateral or controlling the money flow… **The person who controls the money has leverage.**" (Stripe naplaćuje umesto klijenta pa ne brine o isteklim karticama.)
- **Renewal by opt-out** `[8VqSFBMMS4M]`: prebaci sa 12-mesečnog na **biannual recurring** (3+3 ili 6 meseci intenziva + aftercare) — obnova postaje automatska. "We want to get the legal right to do it here, but we want to have the psychological right from here."
- **Annual plan sa popustom** `[-j8_YCWZ05Q]`: odluka se donosi **jednom umesto 12 puta**; kupci su posvećeniji. **Standard: 16–17% popusta = "buy 10 get 2".**
- **Meet & Renew** `[N5MExtki_VI]`: obnove se rade **uživo, na eventu.** Zatvaranje obnove: "the easiest way to do that is just ask them when they're going to come back. This is the next date. Let's do it." Renewal incentive: prvoklasne karte za sledeći event, ili fast-action bonus (skini $5K).
- **"Escape valve" umesto neograničenog pristupa** `[N5MExtki_VI]`: "you've got three 911 calls." Eskalacija umesto "hit me up whenever".
- **Consumption kao uzrok churn-a** `[K8MFC9t7snY]`: "If people don't consume your product, it's not that they're numbnuts — it means that you suck. It means you made it so difficult for someone to onboard that they never even experienced the benefit." Sadržaj/edukacija koji teraju potrošnju direktno smanjuju otkaze `[orMbq2LtzKE]`.
- **"Easy in, easy out"** `[XC_lklN9KmE]`: usluge se lako prodaju i lako otkazuju; softver/CRM je "sticky" jer je migracija bolna. "If you have easy in and you're not maximizing that advantage, you're not using one of the primary advantages of being a service provider."
- **Prodaja tokom isporuke, dok je klijent "in flight"** `[8VqSFBMMS4M]`: analogija maratona — dok kupuješ patike kupićeš i majicu i traku za telefon; posle trke nećeš. "You want to sell when the pain is still elevated… they're still mid-decision, not making a new decision. 'Hey, let's just keep the party going.'"
- **Upsell timing** `[8VqSFBMMS4M]`: samo na kraju → **~1/3**; na polovini + 2/3 + kraj → **1/2 do 2/3**.
- **Telefonski poziv posle isporuke** `[orMbq2LtzKE]`: "Hey, how are things going? Do you need any recipes? Would you like to become a subscriber?" → **~40% konverzija.**

## 14. Onboarding

**Zašto je najveća poluga**
- **Sam onboarding dodaje 2–3 meseca LTV-a** `[afbP6sB_Atc]` / "you could probably add one or two months to your recurring revenue" `[sPkMHh8zTMI]`.
- **Prvi utisak se formira u 24–48h posle kupovine** i uokviruje ceo ostatak iskustva `[afbP6sB_Atc]`; "prvih 48 sati posle prodaje odlučuje da li klijent voli tvoj biznis" `[oi7bnS8uyJM]`; "People will make a lasting impression about your business **within the first 24 hours after the transaction.**" `[yPDQCfrwh8E]`
- **Dva trenutka u kojima kupac odlučuje da li ostaje** `[hnfh2jqNVEQ]`: (1) **razmak između prodaje i početka isporuke**; (2) **sam onboarding**. Odluka se donosi u **prva dva-tri kontakta**.
- **Onboarding odmah posle potpisa** `[8VqSFBMMS4M]`.

**4 komponente onboardinga** `[yPDQCfrwh8E]`
1. **Sales ↔ CS sinhronizacija sa formalnim handoff procesom.** Pravilo: **"Book a meeting from a meeting. There should never be a no man's land."**
2. **Praćenje klijentovih ciljeva** — pitanje sa sales poziva prosleđeno CS timu. Efekat: "I didn't even have to tell this person — **these guys are on it.**"
3. **Reset očekivanja posle plaćanja** — ponovi tačno ono što je prodavac rekao. "**Cold feet, back-outs, or early refund requests are usually because there's a disconnect between the first conversation and the second conversation.**"
4. **Resell them on the purchase** — ponovo ih prodaj na njihov "why", ispričaj priče ljudi u istoj poziciji, pa **outline the steps**.

**Mehanika prvih 24h** `[yPDQCfrwh8E]`
**Otvori i zatvori 4–6 obećanja u prvih 24h.** "Even if it's something that you would do all at once — take that one thing that has six checkpoints and make it **six individual promises** and fulfill all of them."

**Ostali principi**
- **CLOSER se primenjuje i na ONBOARDING** `[afbP6sB_Atc]`: na onboarding pozivu ponoviš **C, L, O** i ponovo prodaš "vacation" — sad kao ono što će doživeti kao rezultat već donete odluke.
- **Skill-transfer onboarding** `[hnfh2jqNVEQ]`: cilj nije informisati nego **preneti veštinu** — uradi to zajedno sa njima, pa ih pusti da ponove pred tobom. "If your onboarding is rushed, expand the time period, because it's going to massively multiply the lifetime value."
- **Onboarding kao koreografija** `[hnfh2jqNVEQ]`: svi znaju šta se dešava pre nego što se desi i šta sledi; handoff od prodaje do isporuke mora biti bešavan. Post-sale komunikacija kao deo proizvoda: **personalizovan video, swag, pismo, i obavezno SMS u razmaku između prodaje i početka onboardinga.**
- **Choreographed check-in, ne "arbitrary check-in"** `[yPDQCfrwh8E]`: znaj koji problem klijent IMA u toj nedelji programa. Suprotno ostavlja utisak: "you don't seem informed, you don't seem on top of it, you don't seem proactive."
- **Više malih ciklusa umesto jednog velikog izveštaja** `[sPkMHh8zTMI]`: agencija koja svaki dan javi "danas radimo X" pa sutra "završili smo X, danas radimo Y" dobija **7 pozitivnih ciklusa u 7 dana** umesto jednog nedeljnog izveštaja. Ponavljanje je nužno jer "they're not listening, they're on their phone, they've got their kids in the backseat."
- **Onboarding vezan za pravi razlog izvučen na sales pozivu** `[neTSqOAMgao]`: umesto "your goal is you're trying to make an extra ten thousand dollars a month" → "**what you're trying to do is trying to retire your wife**". Rezultat: veći buy-in, veći compliance, **veće back-end konverzije bez ijedne promene proizvoda.**
- **Dve faze prodaje istog klijenta** `[XC_lklN9KmE]`: "there's the 'I'm going to try it' phase and then there's the 'I'm going to commit' phase" — čak i kad neko potpiše recurring, ne ostaje dok ne napraviš **drugu prodaju**. Konverzioni sastanci moraju biti **zaseban blok u kalendaru**.
- Dosadno pobeđuje `[neTSqOAMgao]`: "No one's like 'oh man, we should improve our onboarding process' — that's not sexy. **You do a hundred of those and all of a sudden a vast majority of your clients are getting results.**"

**Onboarding case — SaaS u portfoliju** `[afbP6sB_Atc]`
90 dana posle lansiranja: spike u churn-u, pad CSAT-a, pad ascension-a. **Za 6 meseci uštedeli $2M bottom-line profita.**
- Loš onboarding: **25 novih klijenata po pozivu**, 1 osoba radi 6–8 poziva dnevno; ~**1/3 poziva** trošeno na resetovanje lozinki; ostatak na misiju/viziju/osnivačku priču ("all the stuff that customers don't care about"); tvrd upsell na kraju bez next steps ako odbiju.
- **Prelazak 1-na-25 → 1-na-1 + CLOSER u onboardingu: ascension rate 20% → 48%.**
- **Companywide: 2,4x ukupnih ascensions. 61% smanjenje otkazivanja/refundova.**
- **Pilot na 15% novih klijenata**, ne na celoj bazi. Naučni metod: hipoteza → **definiši UNAPRED koje brojke moraju da izađu** → izvrši na malom uzorku → meri. "If the numbers had only marginally improved, I wouldn't have said 'we shouldn't fix them' — I would say 'this isn't the WAY we fix them.'"

**Time-blocking i kapacitet za onboarding tim** `[XC_lklN9KmE]`
- **2h/dan outbound ka postojećim klijentima** (retencija) + **2h/dan onboarding novih** + orijentacije + prodajni sastanci.
- **Kapacitet jednog zaposlenog:** 30 onboarding konsultacija × 30 min = **15h nedeljno = 3h dnevno**; + 30 konverzionih sastanaka × 30 min = **još 3h dnevno** ⇒ **6h dnevno**; ostaju **2h dnevno za reach-out ka postojećim klijentima**. "That is a role."
- Zaseban full-time prodavac: **60 sastanaka × 30 min preko 6 dana = 10 dnevno = 5h dnevno.**

## 15. Time to First Value i activation point

**Princip** `[yPDQCfrwh8E]`
- "There's a key moment that transforms the customer relationship into a long-term commitment with you, and **your goal is to pull that as far forward to the present as you possibly can.**"
- **Ne moraš isporučiti krajnji rezultat odmah:** "break it into many small chunks and show leading indicators of success. **The more times that you can break down an outcome into tinier chunks, the more reinforced the customer will be for staying with you.**"
- **Ista isporuka, drugačija komunikacija:** "If every two weeks I correspond with them, they're going to get reinforced every 2 weeks. Now consider: I onboard them and then **every day I send them what I did to work on their behalf**. At the end of that 2 weeks they would have had **14 different positive interactions with me versus one.** You can take what you're currently doing, not change much about it, and simply communicate it differently so that they perceive a faster time to first value."
- **Breaking Bad retention point:** ko stigne do **3. epizode ima ~87% šanse da završi svih pet sezona.** Svaka usluga ima svoju "3. epizodu".
- **Kolaps time delay-a kao lek za komoditizovanu uslugu** `[neTSqOAMgao]`: "as soon as the contract is signed, if the prospect's phone rang or their inbox was filled and they got a booked call 30 minutes after they got off the phone with you — how much more valuable would that be than somebody that says it's gonna take 30 days to ramp up, another 30 days to see results, 30 days after that to break even, and then by month six... **I've already lost you.**"

**Kako se nalazi activation point** `[sPkMHh8zTMI, rc7cxL7ql7Y]`
Pogledaj klijente koji su ostali najduže i pitaj **šta su rano uradili što je dalo najviše vrednosti** (regresija po kohortama). Kad znaš aktivacionu tačku, **SVI resursi idu na to**: "I'm not doing any growth initiatives, I'm not doing any new features — everything is how do we get more people to that activation point." **Ako churn ne padne, aktivaciona tačka ti je pogrešno definisana.**

**Primeri aktivacionih tačaka**
- **Gym Launch: $2.000 prodaje klijentovoj teretani u prvih 7 dana** → dramatično veća retencija `[sPkMHh8zTMI]`; varijanta: **jedna high-ticket prodaja u prvih 7 dana → LTV se utrostručuje** `[yPDQCfrwh8E]`.
- **Ekonomika te jedne stvari:** ako LTV ide sa **$17.000 na $50.000**, imaš **$33.000 gotovo čistog profita** da potrošiš da se ta jedna stvar desi. "How many resources do you think I should put towards that? **A ton.**" `[yPDQCfrwh8E]`
- **Weight-loss analog: 5 funti u prvoj nedelji.** `[sPkMHh8zTMI]`
- **"The first five clients problem"** — dovedi treneru prvih 5 klijenata i nikad ne otkaže. `[sPkMHh8zTMI]`
- **SaaS za landing pages:** prebacivanje na custom URL → **ostaju 5x duže.** `[sPkMHh8zTMI]`
- **KPI koji su vozili kao kompanija** `[yPDQCfrwh8E]`: koliko keša klijent generiše **u prvih 14 dana**; trenutni prosek **$15.500 cash collected u prvih 14 dana.** Prosečan klijent zaradio **$30.000 u prvih 30 dana** (prosek — znači pola je bilo bolje) `[neTSqOAMgao]`.
- **Pin the fast win up front — vrednost u prvih 24 sata.** `[-j8_YCWZ05Q]`

**4 stvari koje želiš od kupca** (i koje treba da budu unlockables) `[sPkMHh8zTMI]`: **activate → testimonial/review → referral → ascension.**

## 16. Sedam retention taktika (Gym Launch studija)
`[sPkMHh8zTMI, y3T4GqoH0Us]`

**Metod istraživanja:** anketiraj celu populaciju (**5.000+ licenciranih teretana**) → izdvoji **top 1% po najnižem churn-u koji ga drži 6+ meseci** → intervjui → svaki navede ~30 stvari, ali **onih 5 koje SVI rade** je signal. (Srodan uzorak za "Five Horsemen of Retention": teretane sa **ispod 3% mesečnog churna kroz 6–8 meseci** `[UGEc9-7X3OQ]`.)

1. **Exit interviews** — obavezu ugovoriš **unapred, na potpisu**. **Preko 50% ljudi na exit intervjuu se proda u viši paket.** "If half your customers, or half of that half, upsell to something 2x or 3x more expensive, your revenue churn just disappears." "A lot of times people just need to vent, and the things that they're venting about — it sounds like pain, means it can be SOLVED."
2. **Reach outs** — sistematizovan lični kontakt, kadenca **14–21 dan**, potpuno neprodajni. Jedan trener zadužen za **~40 klijenata** = **10–15 kontakata nedeljno**.
3. **Member events** — događaji za klijente + njihove goste (lead gen); **kvartalno** "usually works". **Open loop** obavezno.
4. **Handwritten cards** — rukom pisane, **obavezno personalizovane**, kadenca **svakih ~6 nedelja** (poklapa se sa eventovima), sa **PS**-om za referral.
5. **Attendance / consumption tracking** — rano upozorenje. Ako klijent ne dođe u ponedeljak i utorak, do srede se **eskalira** i uporno kontaktira dok se ne zakaže dolazak. **"The easiest correlation with cancellation is lack of consumption."** / **"No matter how cheap a gym membership is, a gym membership you never use is never worth it"** — i obrnuto, "almost no one complains about a gym membership they use every day, no matter how expensive it is."
6. **Onboarding** — expectation setting + reconfirm the buying decision. (Detaljno u §14.)
7. **Unlockables & milestones** — nagrade po aktivnosti ili po stažu. Tempo: **mesec 3, pa mesec 8, pa mesec 15** — **razmaci se produžavaju, nagrade rastu.**

**Nulti preduslov koji su svi imali: oni uopšte MERE churn.**

**Redosled implementacije po odnosu trud/rezultat:** 1) onboarding, 2) unlockables, 3) exit interviews, 4) attendance tracking (**najjači efekat, najviše posla**), 5) reach outs, 6+7) events + handwritten cards zajedno.

**"Do not automate what you should systematize"** `[y3T4GqoH0Us, sPkMHh8zTMI]`
"As soon as someone finds out that communications are automated, they stop paying attention... it actually WITHDRAWS goodwill from the goodwill bank account of your customers rather than depositing. It's better to say nothing at all than to automate things." / **"If they know it's automated, it will not work. It has to be systematized, not automated."**

**Dunbar number kao objašnjenje plafona** `[y3T4GqoH0Us]`
Servisni biznisi zapnu na **100–150 klijenata** jer čovek održava ~150 odnosa; reach-out sistem veštački produžava taj kapacitet.
- **"It's a lot easier to quit a membership than it is to quit a relationship."**
- **"People pay you to pay attention. That is what they are literally paying for."**
- "The best way of getting people to think you give a [damn] is to actually give a [damn]."

## 17. Taktike retencije u zajednicama (Skool podaci)
`[-j8_YCWZ05Q]`

1. **Leaky bucket dijagnostika** — mesečno prati samo dva broja, **joins vs cancels**, i prisili sebe da označiš biznis kao **growing / flat / declining**.
2. **Znaj benchmarke** da bi znao da li si dobar. (Vidi §7.)
3. **Menjaj pitanje:** ne "kako da smanjim mesečni churn" nego **"kako da ih zadržim preko dana 90? kako preko meseca 6?"**
4. **Pitaj otkazane zašto — i traži OBRASCE.** Ritam: **na svakih ~20 otkaza pregledaj top 1–2 razloga i popravi ih.** Tagovi: **price / overwhelm / not using / missing a feature / not what I expected.**
5. **Pitaj najbolje članove zašto ostaju — i radi više toga.** "If you can only do one thing that gets people to stay, why would you do five?"
6. **Fix by subtraction, not addition.** "We believe that in order to fix things we need to fix them through addition, when often we need to fix them through subtraction... If you were to simply fulfill the promise you originally made, they'd probably stay." **Definicija kvaliteta: daš obećanje, pa ukloniš SVU frikciju ka tom obećanju.**
7. **Price objection → tier na osnovu dokaza:** ako **IDEALNI** kupci kažu da je skupo → dodaj niži tier. Ako **NE-idealni** kažu da je skupo → ignoriši, cena radi svoj posao kao filter.
   - **[protivrečno]** u odnosu na `BZQtuK-ucDM` ("nikad ne spuštaj cenu") i na pravilo iz §5 ("podiži vrednost, ne spuštaj cenu"). ## 20. Baton Pass, BAMFAM i eskalacije
`[afbP6sB_Atc]`

- **BAMFAM — "Book A Meeting From A Meeting"** (od Chirona Sivathanua): "Bamfam is a way of life. No customer should ever not be bamfam'd — they should always know when the next time they're going to talk to you is. Never get off a call being like 'yeah yeah and then we'll coordinate the next thing.' You're both there, pull up your calendars, pick the time, get it done."
- **Baton Pass — jedan point of contact u svakom trenutku.** Sales rep John → onboarding Olly → account rep Alex. Tri osobe dodiruju nalog, ali **klijent nikad nema više od jednog kontakta istovremeno**, i uvek zna ko je sledeći. "No one should ever fall into no man's land." Handoff treba da bude **handshake**, ne handoff.
- **Escalations tim kao triage** (analogija hitne pomoći) — odvojen tim koji preuzima nezadovoljne klijente od prodaje. **Prodavci NE smeju da rade support:** "If you want to destroy your sales team and annihilate any kind of goodwill and morale on that team, have them talk to all the dissatisfied customers that they sold last week while they're trying to sell new customers on how great the product is today."
- **25% (1 od 4) ljudi na escalation pozivu KUPI viši nivo usluge.** Ako imaš proizvod od **$1.000** i upsell od **$4.000**, i od 4 nezadovoljna jedan uzme $4.000 — **pokrio si gubitak sva tri.** "You can eliminate the revenue loss you have by simply seeing the conversation for what it is, which is: it's also a sale."
- **Provizija 10–15% za SPAŠENOG klijenta.** "If I have to pay $200 to save a $2,000 sale, then I would certainly rather have $1,800 than zero. Some of you guys get greedy on this, but just do the math."
- **ROI na CS headcount:** rep za **$70k–$100k** godišnje koji spašava **$500k–$2M** = **5:1 do 10:1** interne alokacije. "If you have something that can get you a 5x or 10x within the company, you make that bet every time."
- **Rule of the angry boat** `[sPkMHh8zTMI]` — "only one person can be the angry boat": kad je klijent ljut, ti budi **ljući od njega**, ali na **PROBLEM**, ne na njega. On se oseti validirano i počne tebe da smiruje.
- **Proaktivni downgrade kao retention alat:** "those people never cancel... 'they were ethical about it, they reached out to me, they helped me save money — I love these guys.'"
- **Scriptovanje svake ponovljive konverzacije, ne samo prodajne:** "Every conversation with a customer has a perfect way that that conversation could and should go... if we script all this stuff and then we don't script all this stuff, why do we think that this stuff would somehow be better than this stuff?" CS pitanja su predvidiva kao prodajne primedbe — **90% ljudi dolazi sa istih par stvari.**

**Metrike → komisije** `[yPDQCfrwh8E]`
"A lot of people think that the only people who should get commissions are salespeople, and I think that's silly, because believe it or not a lot more of your profit is driven from customer success." Ponderisana komisija: **100% cilja → 2×, 50% → 1×, 0% → 0×**; kod 16-nedeljnog programa klijent koji dođe do **12. nedelje = 75% ponder.**

**Kvalifikacija CSAT-a** `[yPDQCfrwh8E]`: mereno odmah posle koreografisanog onboardinga, "I'm a little sus"; predlaže merenje na **week 0, 2, 4, 8, 12** — ali previše anketa izaziva otpor, i "the only people who choose to respond to a customer satisfaction survey are typically either very happy or very sad — you usually lose the middle."

## 24. Referral petlje, recenzije i testimoniali

**Growth flywheel** `[4twK8Yl4iUI]`
Linearno je `trud + novac → kupci → rezultat` pa iz početka. Petlja: `trud+novac → kupci → rezultat → **recenzije & word of mouth** → VIŠE kupaca`. Cilj: "a self-reinforcing system where every action builds momentum and drives the next... without the continuous input of more effort and money."

**Referral brojke**
- Referali zatvaraju **80–90%**, **1 od 3–4 klijenta da referal.** `[JE2_7elAcxM]`
- **ButcherBox referral** = probna kutija za **$20** prijatelju, **bez nagrade preporučiocu**, ~**30% akvizicije.** `[orMbq2LtzKE]`
- Hiropraktičar: **$2,4M revenue, 30% margina, $600k profit, ~50% klijenata iz referrala**, 20% Meta ads, 20% Google. `[jqo0lVveh98]`

**Četiri taktike za recenzije** `[4twK8Yl4iUI]`
1. **Surprise gift + third-party ask** (razdvojenost je ključna): (1) posle isporuke daš **neočekivani poklon**, srazmeran potrošnji; (2) **onaj ko je dao poklon NE traži recenziju** — menadžer dolazi i kaže da *taj čovek* dobija bonus ako gost ostavi recenziju. "The further away the person who makes the ask is from the business owner, the more compelling I believe it is."
2. **Merch za recenzije:** majica od **$5** se menja za **3–4 akcije** (Yelp + Google + Facebook + check-in). Dupli efekat: digitalni word of mouth + organski (nose majicu javno). **Dizajn kad brend još nije jak:** ne logo napred nego **inspirativan citat/slogan** ("Beast Mode Engaged"), logo ide **na leđa ili rukav**.
3. **Popust na licu mesta, nikad "sledeći put".** Redosled: (1) predstaviš cenu, (2) dobiješ prihvatanje cene, (3) **tek onda** ponudiš popust za recenziju sad, (4) verifikuješ da je ostavljena, (5) prekalkulišeš i doneseš račun. **"Latency is one of the strongest multipliers of a persuasive tool. Risk and delay actually work very similarly in people's minds."**
4. **Unlockables (najbolja za B2B/digital/national):** (a) **media unlock** — trening, event, snimak; (b) **VIP trial unlock** — **30 dana višeg tier-a** usluge. "Not only do you get the review, you also give the customer a taste of the higher-end version of the service — **so you literally get paid to get reviews**." IRL varijanta: godišnji event čija je ulaznica ostavljena recenzija.

**Tri meta-mehanizma iza sve četiri:** (1) dodaješ nešto dobro bez razmene (**reciprocitet ide OSOBI, ne firmi**), (2) direktna razmena (quid pro quo), (3) uklanjaš nešto loše (popust).

**Recenzija kao interna metrika kvaliteta:** meri **% posluženih klijenata koji ostave 5 zvezdica po zaposlenom.** "It gives you a second metric to look at customer satisfaction in the realest way possible that generates revenue." Ko odskače — pitaš šta radi drugačije i naučiš ostale.

**Rotiraj platformu preko QR koda:** prvo napuni Yelp, pa prebaciš QR na Google, pa na Meta, pa na IG follow, pa na SMS listu. **Jedan mehanizam, promenljiva destinacija.**

**Brojke** `[4twK8Yl4iUI]`
- **Skaliranje poklona:** potroši **$20 → bombone; $100 → desert od $5; $1.000 → poklon od $50.** Ono što plaćaš za poklon + bonus timu treba da bude srazmerno vrednosti jedne recenzije — a ta vrednost je znatno veća kad imaš 0 recenzija nego kad imaš 10.000.
- **Yelp anegdota:** 5.0 sa 20 recenzija vs. **4.6 sa 1.000 recenzija — otišao je na 4.6/1000.** "If the person who has the most reviews ends up getting 90% of the search volume, how much was each incremental review worth? A hell of a lot."
- **Restoran matematika:** 10 stolova/sat × 10 sati = **100 stolova dnevno**; menadžeru treba **~5 min da obiđe 10 stolova** (20 sek po stolu); **10% konverzija = 10 petica dnevno ≈ 4.000 recenzija godišnje.** Bonus konobaru: **$10 po petici sa pomenom imena.**
- **Merch primer:** majice po **$5**; jedan post grupi članova → **15 petica preko noći** za novu lokaciju.
- **Diner popust:** **$1 popusta po osobi ≈ 5% prosečnog računa** — "you'd pay $5,000 for 5,000 reviews — that's a good deal."
- Poklon pre ili posle: "to be honest with you, they both work."
- **"You are lighter fluid, not the log."** "You want the engine to run without you — because if you're the log, you'll eventually get burned out."
- **Make it easy:** mali A-frame sa QR kodom pored računa; svaki dodatni korak ubija konverziju. Kod fizičkog proizvoda: **QR ili link u kutiji.**
- **Prikaži proof svuda:** lobi sa 5-zvezdica recenzijama **od poda do plafona** — "someone walks into your lobby of all five-star reviews and it just anchors what type of business you are". Pokrij Yelp, Google (SEO + PPC) i Meta: "Even though we're asking for three, every new customer is only going to see maybe one of these."

**Kako se dobija dobar testimonial** `[pLhQOYMGa88]`
Ne "kako je bilo pre nego što ste počeli da radite sa nama", nego **"what was your worst moment?"** — "because that's what all of these were: a moment. **The moments are what makes hooks, the details are what make them powerful.** 'We were two months away from shutting our doors' is much more visceral than 'stuff was hard'."

- **Proizvod radi većinu brendinga posle kupovine** `[VQM3DrnVTcs]`: "if there's a hole in the armpit... this product sucks, therefore Nike sucks — and then I start to hate LeBron for recommending it"; asocijacija ide unazad. Brend može da nosi proizvod koji je **"good enough"**, ali ne i loš.
- **Buffett, sa Hormozijevom korekcijom** `[VQM3DrnVTcs]`: "Your premium brand had better be delivering something special, or it's not going to get the business." → "it's not going to **KEEP** the business. You'll get the first purchase, you just won't get the ones after that."
- **Virtuous cycle of branding** `[9unucIBuNio]`: daš obećanje → održiš → oni to kažu drugima → drugi probaju → održiš i njima. "Anybody can get somebody to buy one thing one time, but it takes an exceptional business to keep their promises and get them to buy again and again."
- **Konzistentnost** `[TIH1w-KuATk]`: "People value consistency. People want to know that when they exchange money they're going to get the thing that they liked last time."

## 26. Quad Marketing Calendar i talent funnel
`[QtE6kk0158o]`

**Matrica 2×2: internal/external × employees/prospects.** Četvrti kvadrant se najčešće propušta.
1. **Internal → Employees:** stalno marketiraš viziju i misiju timu, "so that people continue to want to work for you". Mora se dešavati sve vreme.
2. **Internal → Customers:** stalno im marketiraš da kupe opet — "we need to keep them buying."
3. **External → Prospects:** klasična akvizicija. "Most people understand this one."
4. **External → Talent:** **"the one that people miss the most"** — nema akvizicionog procesa za nove ljude.

**Talent funnel = kopija sales funnela:** run ads → generate leads → nurture/work leads → set interviews → **"interviews ARE sales"** → make job offers → employee onboarding → ascend/manage. "These are parallel processes that happen in every business and most people don't know about it."

## 27. APP proces — poboljšanje rezultata kupaca
`[thDTmy7VGIw]`

1. **Anketiraj** kupce da nađeš one sa najboljim rezultatima.
2. **Intervjuiši** ih da otkriješ šta su radili drugačije — demografija, korisnička putanja (kako su ušli, uz koje obećanje) i **radnje koje samo oni imaju zajedničke.**
3. **Primoraj nove kupce da namerno ponove ono što su najbolji uradili slučajno.**
4. **Meri poboljšanje prosečnog kupca.**
5. **Veži uslove garancije za baš te radnje.**

**Before / during / after (three-term contingency)** — analiziraj šta se dešava neposredno pre kupovine, pre upotrebe, tokom upotrebe i odmah posle. **To su tačke najveće poluge.**

**Dve škole iteracije proizvoda:** *rank-and-build (PC)* — kupci glasaju za feature, gradiš najglasaniji (vodi ka Frankenštajnu, ali neki kupci to žele); *Mac* — zamisliš idealno iskustvo i unazad izvedeš elegantno rešenje koje **NAMERNO izbacuje stvari.** Oba prave trilionske firme, ali **ne možeš oba istovremeno.**

## 29. Case studies

**Case: $6M enterprise value za 60 dana** `[yPDQCfrwh8E]`
- Kontekst: prodaja udvostručena u prethodnoj fazi (**56 → 93 prodatih jedinica mesečno za 60 dana**) → isporuka pukla.
- **Tri metrike koje je uveo (niko ih nije pratio):** (1) **Time to First Value**, (2) **Customer Satisfaction**, (3) **Ascension rate / backend revenue.**
- **Rezultati za 60 dana:** Time to First Value **47 → 34 dana**; CSAT **ne prati se → 98%**; Ascension **$0 → $140.000** backend prihoda; Ascension rate **10% → 27%.**
- **Prevod u EV:** $140k / 2 meseca = **~$70k mesečno profita = ~$840k godišnje × 6× multiple ≈ $6M enterprise value.**
- **Redosled je obavezan i sekvencijalan:** onboarding → CRM → metrike → komisije vezane za metrike → **tek onda backend.** "We could not have done this one if we hadn't fixed all of these other things first."

**Gym Launch — churn i exit** `[y3T4GqoH0Us]`
Churn **11% → 6% mesečno** posle implementacije svih 7 taktika — to je učinilo firmu prodajivom (**prodata za $46.2M**, **5.000+ lokacija pod licencom**).

**Gym Launch — propušteni $900M** `[3P1XjUvo1b4]`
**5.000 teretana** kupilo licencu. "If I had 5,000 of those gyms still paying right now, that company would be worth over a billion dollars. That company is probably worth now close to about a hundred million. So I've missed out on about **$900 million** during my career just from that one company alone." Lekcija mu je trebala **7 godina.**

**SaaS onboarding case** `[afbP6sB_Atc]` — vidi §14: **$2M bottom-line profita za 6 meseci**, ascension 20% → 48%, **2,4x ukupnih ascensions**, **61% smanjenje otkazivanja/refundova.**

**WaaS firma — Hormozijev recept** `[jqo0lVveh98]`
$20M revenue, $450/mes, 29 meseci stick, $3,6M EBITDA. "I would probably spend the next 6 months reorganizing the workflow, probably **reduce headcount by 50% using AI workflows**... **increase the margin from 3.6 to like 7 or more**. With the added cash flow you wouldn't have to change the price on the front end. **You'd be willing to go negative for a quarter in the acquisition knowing you're going to get 29 [months] on the back.**"

**Zajednica koja je obrisala sadržaj** `[-j8_YCWZ05Q]`: churn **30% → 5% mesečno = 6x povećanje LTV-a.**

**Hair extensions LTV stacking** `[jzKpAtzKQ54]` — vidi §10: ratio **30:1 → 75:1.**

**Kvalifikacioni zaokret Gym Launch** `[hnfh2jqNVEQ, 5MjjpB8SPMo]` — vidi §22: prodaje **100+ → 40–50** mesečno, eskalacije **22 → 0.**
