## 1. Client-Financed Acquisition — jedina jednačina
`[XwZH-lOKG9c]`

Jedina jednačina cele keynote:
> **30-day cash > (CAC + COGS) × 2**
> "I have to generate more cash in the first 30 days of running an advertisement than it costs me to acquire two customers and the cost to fulfill both of them."

**Logika broja 30:** "everyone has access to one thing, which is a **credit card**. So I basically know that I have 30 days against the clock that I can spend whatever I want, and as long as I have it back plus some in 30 days, **I never have to use my own money to grow**… I get my customers to pay for the growth of my business."

**Definicije:**
- **CAC** = svi troškovi akvizicije (oglasi, plate, media buyer, kreativni tim, softver)
- **COGS** = direktni trošak isporuke
- **gross profit** = ostatak
- **gross profit payback period** = vreme dok se uloženo ne vrati
- **30-day cash** = bruto profit izvučen iz novog kupca u prvih 30 dana

"The more you can condense the cash conversion cycle, the faster you can grow — especially if you don't have funding, which I've never had."

**Numerička demonstracija:** pozajmiš **$100** na kreditnu → 2 kupca po **$50 CAC** → svaki potroši **$300** = **$600** → isporuka $50 × 2 = $100 → ostaje **$400 keša + 2 kupca, nula duga**. Pa dupliraš. "It's literally just going black on black in Vegas again."

**Cilj:** "execute client-financed acquisition **until your operations break**."
**Posledica:** "you can compete against anyone… you can still crush it right now against people who have way more money because they don't know the game."

**Samo dva načina za rast biznisa:** (1) get more customers; (2) make customers worth more. "There's no other way. That's it."

Četiri frameworka kao ciklus: "we have the math, we make our offer, we go to the traffic, we make money — which means we update our math, and around we go." (Framework 3 = šest izvora saobraćaja i Framework 4 = back-end LTV nisu bili u dostupnom delu transkripta.)

**Payback curve** `[Kk6Vdfdwmxw]`: "$1 in → day 15: $1, day 30: $3,50, day 60: $7."

## 2. Tri broja: CAC, LTGP, 30-day cash
`[Cr0KdqZ954c]`

Sve ostalo — CPL, CPC, CTR — su "detalji u fioci" ispod ova tri:

1. **CAC** — koliko košta da se stekne **KUPAC**, ne lead. Mora uključiti sve: "your sales commission... your ad spend... the marketing team that created the inquiries... your software that's associated with it."
2. **LTV, ali definisan kao LTGP (lifetime GROSS PROFIT)** — "LTV is not the total revenue... for me it's the total gross profit that I'm going to get over the lifespan of a customer." **LTGP, ne revenue, je imenilac za CAC** `[Q2VUuUGfpNY]`.
3. **30-day cash** (payback period).

**Pragovi:** **LTGP:CAC > 3:1** ("I want to always have greater than three to one") i **30-day cash : CAC ≥ 1:1**.

**Radni primer 1 (hrana, loš slučaj):** obrok $10, cost $9 → **10% gross margin**. Klijent naruči $700 mesečno → izgleda kao veliki LTV, stvarni LTGP je **$70**. "We're spending $200 to make $70."
**Radni primer 2 (usluga, dobar slučaj):** gross margin 80%, lifetime revenue $1.000 → LTGP $800. Za 3:1 CAC mora biti ispod **$266** ("$265.66"). Ako 30-day cash > ~$260 → break-even odmah, ostatak (~$500) je besplatan back-end.

**Cap formula** `[-UvmyixFszw]`: novi klijenti mesečno × LTV = mesečni plafon.
**Minimum po klijentu** `[-UvmyixFszw]`: **$100–200** koje biznis mora moći da plati po klijentu — inače je to "business problem", ne "marketing problem".

**Input-output jednačina** `[jqo0lVveh98]`: "Every business needs to know **what are the core actions that I do that increase how much money I make**. And if you can't define that for the business, then for sure as f*** your employees don't know what it is." Bez attribution trackinga nemaš tu jednačinu.

## 3. LTGP:CAC — pragovi, poluge, primeri
`[jzKpAtzKQ54, i7bLRKwKSms, reisEL_D7xc, 9unucIBuNio, sPkMHh8zTMI, FMzKk73iUhw]`

- **"The fundamental economic unit of the business"** — "how much it costs you to make more money." Predviđa koliko daleko biznis može da skalira. U `9unucIBuNio`: "the fundamental arbitrage of business is how much it costs you to make money."
- **3:1 LTV:CAC** je odnos koji preporučuje `[sPkMHh8zTMI]`; LTV određuje koliko smeš da trošiš.
- **Fixed costs se NE ubrajaju u gross profit:** "if you sell an additional unit that additional gross profit goes to you." / "This is how much money you make from the customer for real — **not your wink wink Stripe screenshot**."
- **CAC formula:** (marketing payroll + ad spend + sales komisije za period) ÷ broj stečenih kupaca. Računati **godišnje** da bi se izbacila volatilnost. "$10,000 in ads + $10,000 in payroll = $20,000; 20 customers = $1,000 CAC."
- **Back-of-napkin gross profit** `[i7bLRKwKSms]`: ukupan COGS za godinu ÷ broj kupaca, oduzeto od lifetime revenue po kupcu. Sendvič: prodaje se za $100, košta $20 → **80% gross margin**; 10 kupovina → **$800 LTGP**.

### Pragovi po stepenu automatizacije
`[i7bLRKwKSms]` — Hormozijeva korekcija SaaS pravila 3:1. Tri komponente biznisa: **lead generation, conversion (sales), delivery/fulfillment**. Automatizovano = "one to many" (content, oglasi, word of mouth); manuelno = ljudi.

| Automatizovanih komponenti | Minimalni LTGP:CAC |
|---|---|
| sve tri | **3:1** |
| dve od tri | **6:1** |
| jedna od tri | **9:1** |
| nijedna (ljudi u svakom koraku) | **preko 12:1, minimum** |

### Zašto ratio mora da raste sa skaliranjem — 3 razloga
`[i7bLRKwKSms]`
1. **CAC uvek raste** — CPM-ovi rastu, konkurencija ulazi, algoritam ide u hladnija auditorijuma ("whatever you have today is likely going to be the best cost per customer you're ever going to get"). Isto: **"CAC will never be cheaper than it is today."** `[jqo0lVveh98]`
2. Slojevi infrastrukture i menadžmenta dodaju trošak, a kasniji kupci su manje ubeđeni i vrede manje.
3. **Svaki novi čovek u sistemu je lošiji od postojećih** — 6. i 7. prodavac nisu kao prvih 5, a trošak ide odmah dok povraćaj kasni mesecima.

**Koliko CAC sme da raste:** samo za rast CPM-a, **~15% godišnje** `[afbP6sB_Atc]`; udvostručen CAC = **anti-referrals**, ne tržište.

### Scenario test
`[i7bLRKwKSms]` Biznis 1: LTV $5.000, CAC $500 → **10:1**. Biznis 2: LTV $1.000, CAC $200 → **5:1**. Biznis 1 je bolji iako je kupac 2,5× skuplji. "You want the customers that are worth the most relative to what you pay."

### Redosled optimizacije
`[jzKpAtzKQ54]` **CAC se popravlja front-to-back, LTV back-to-front:** "I build LTV back to front — I want to think about the most expensive thing and work my way backwards. I think about CAC front to back, because I want to optimize the things in the earlier parts of the funnel because they affect a higher percentage of people." Izuzetak: očigledan outlier — "If I have a 5% show up rate but everything else is amazing and I know the industry average is 60%, then I see that I have a 12x there."

**CAC je između igrača sličan, LTV nije** `[jzKpAtzKQ54]`: "the reason that some businesses stay around forever and others don't is that CAC between the biggest businesses is often about the same — the difference is how much LTV they've been able to stack. That is what creates that long-term competitive advantage."
**LTV kao "arms race of advertising"** `[FMzKk73iUhw]`: CAC benchmarkovi u istoj industriji variraju 1–2×; razlika je LTV. Lokalni kafić verovatno pobeđuje Starbucks po CAC-u, ali gubi jer Starbucks ima **40–50 godina optimizacije LTV-a**.

**Poluge za smanjenje CAC** `[i7bLRKwKSms]`: bolja ponuda; bolji ad creatives (volume + quality, remake winners); **CRO** (split-test stranica, follow-upa, telefonskih skripti, obuka tima); jeftiniji CPM-ovi.
**"Marketing budget" kao simptom lošeg modela** `[i7bLRKwKSms]`: ako gubiš na akviziciji, moraš da budžetiraš. Ako od $100 praviš $1.000 — budžet je "spend as many dollars as you possibly can and keep those numbers."
**Golden Ratio** (referral % ÷ churn %): **ne skaliraj marketing dok referral % ne pređe churn %.**

### Realni ratio primeri
- **Gym Launch prva godina: 100:1.** "I spent $100,000 and made $10 million back." `[jzKpAtzKQ54]`
- **Cookie company:** lokalni oglas "free cookie with a beverage", cena leada **ispod $1**, 1 od 10 dolazi u radnju → **$10 po kupcu**; uz $14.000 LTV to je **~1.400:1** `[jzKpAtzKQ54]`
- **Luk (onion):** gross profit $500, lead $20, konverzija 1 od 5 → CAC $100 → **5:1** `[reisEL_D7xc]`
- **Obroci:** prodaja $10, trošak $9 → $1 GP; 5 nedelja × 10 obroka → **$50 LTGP** → CAC ≤ **$15** `[reisEL_D7xc]`
- **Starbucks: ~$14.000 LTV**, čak i pri $500 CAC to je **28:1** `[reisEL_D7xc, FMzKk73iUhw]`. (U `BSwJQsogah0` Starbucks LTV naveden kao ≈ **$8.000** — LTV ≠ ticket price. **[protivrečno]** — dve različite cifre za isti primer u različitim videima; izvor ne nudi razrešenje.)
- **Outbound CAC** `[okA9Yt2KZuk]`: $50 po showu × 10 showova = **$500 po klijentu** pri LTV $40.000.
- **Cleaning biznis: CAC $6** `[rj7rzOdj84A]`. **Affiliate CAC: $270 za kupca od $2.000** `[FMzKk73iUhw]`. **Group funnels obaraju cenu zakazanog poziva sa ~$250 na ~$40** `[fSbqaTlWaYI]`.
- **MedSpa uživo** `[rc7cxL7ql7Y]`: front-end gross profit $58 (Botox), CAC $202, upsell konverzija 40%, upsell gross profit $1.300 → 0,4 × 1.300 = $520 + $58 = **$578 LTGP na $202 CAC ≈ 2,9:1.** Dijagnoza: CAC je fer, konverzija je fer — **poluga je povećanje veličine bundle-a** ($5–6k umesto $1,3k), ne smanjenje CAC-a.
- **Referral fee matematika (roofing/windows)** `[rc7cxL7ql7Y]`: $8.000 gross profit po kupcu → "you could pay a grand for a customer and be happy with it... if you give landscapers a thousand bucks a referral they will beg, borrow and steal to get you referrals."
- **Outbound margin prag** `[rc7cxL7ql7Y]`: sa $1.500–2.000 gross profita po dealu "it's tight, it's very tight for outbound" → zato ideš na afilijate: "it costs me 10 grand to acquire an affiliate but then the affiliate sends me 20 customers a month."
- **ROAS** `[4GjwtnA76ig, neTSqOAMgao, q32-l3Yoqg4]`: lifetime **36:1**, prve godine **100:1**; **$100M+ prihoda uz "just under $3 million"** ukupno na oglase.

**"All the crazy money I've made came from crazy LTV:CAC ratios"** `[reisEL_D7xc]` — najniži takav bio je **30:1**, gore do **100:1 i 200:1**. Ti prozori se zatvaraju; dok traju, ubaci maksimum novca. Održavanje je posle 5:1, 8:1, 10:1. Pri skaliranju 10:1 često padne na 5:1 — **veliki početni ratio je indikacija koliko prostora imaš da skaliraš.** Uz `jzKpAtzKQ54`: "If you do crack this code, I highly recommend jamming as much as you possibly can through that machine."
**Izuzetak od pravila "skaliraj najbolji ratio": mali TAM.** "But most of you aren't even close to saturating your market — I've sold 100 customers and my market is 1 million people." `[jzKpAtzKQ54]`

### Facebook ads agencija — kako se GREŠI
`[jzKpAtzKQ54]` Tvrde 5:1 ROAS ($2.000 CAC → $10.000 klijent), a gube novac. Razlaganje: $2.000/mes cena × 5 meseci (20% churn) = $10.000 revenue. Ali $1.000/mes ad spend uračunat u cenu → ostaje $1.000. **1 od 250 biznisa dođe do $10 miliona godišnje** `[jzKpAtzKQ54]`.

## 4. Hypothetical Max Equation — churn, LTV, plafon
`[f_ceDZqhKbw]`

Šest varijabli koje svaki vlasnik mora znati napamet, "back of napkin": (1) broj novih prodaja mesečno, (2) trenutni prihod, (3) cena, (4) **churn**, (5) **LTV**, (6) bruto marža.

**Formule:**
- `churn % = broj otkaza mesečno ÷ broj aktivnih klijenata`
- `LTV = cena po mesecu ÷ mesečni churn %`
- `hipotetički maksimum mesečnog prihoda = broj novih prodaja mesečno × LTV`
- `lifetime gross profit = LTV × bruto marža`
- `maksimalni CAC = lifetime gross profit`

**Radni primer (uživo):** 100 novih prodaja mesečno, $400K prihoda, 380 aktivnih klijenata, cena $1.000/mesec, 13 otkaza nedeljno ≈ 50 mesečno → churn = 50 ÷ 380 = **13%** → LTV = $1.000 ÷ 0,13 = **$7.700** → hipotetički max = 100 × $7.700 = **$770K mesečno ≈ $9M godišnje** (trenutno rade $400K). Marža >90% → lifetime gross profit ≈ $7.000 → **maksimalni CAC = $7.000.**

**Efekat smanjenja churn-a sa 13% na 3%:** LTV **$7.700 → $33.000**; hipotetički max **$770K → $3,3M mesečno** — bez ijedne promene u akviziciji, i skoro sve je marža. Alternativa (10× akvizicija) daje $7,7M mesečno ali je nesrazmerno teža. **"Which of these do you think is easier to do?"**

**Asimptota rasta:** biznis raste brzo pa usporava jer churn jede sve veći procenat novih klijenata. "If you're a business owner and you don't know what your hypothetical max is, you are fooling yourself."

**Enterprise value triangle — tri varijable koje se MNOŽE: growth × stickiness (retencija) × profit.**

**Retencija je ~17× jeftinija od akvizicije** ($60 vs $1.000 za istu vrednost) `[yPDQCfrwh8E]`. **2× LTV pri istom CAC → "you can sometimes market 10 times more."** `[yPDQCfrwh8E]`
**Prvo proveri churn, ne lead flow** `[KQuyQpFANpA]`.

## 5. Tri linije: VALUE / PRICE / COGS
`[MA-5y9s80Qs]`

- **Customer surplus** = razmak VALUE − PRICE. "The bigger this discrepancy is, the more viral your business will be."
- **Gross margin** = razmak PRICE − COGS.

**Tipologija:**
- (a) visok surplus + visok COGS = biznis koji se skoro ne mora reklamirati ali je operativno težak, raste **10–15% godišnje**, nema novca za reinvestiranje;
- (b) osrednji surplus + osrednja marža = tipičan mom-and-pop;
- (c) **ogroman surplus + minimalan COGS = "Unique, expensive, sticky air"** (njegov cilj).

**Redosled dijagnostike:** (1) *Kako da pružim najviše vrednosti?* (2) *Kako da spustim COGS do apsurda?* — "I'd rather have something that costs me a penny, that I can sell for 10 cents, that has the value of $10."

**Kvadratni vs. linearni rast:** ako je surplus dovoljno velik da ljudi ne mogu da ne pričaju o tebi (remarkable = "remark-worthy"), **CAC pada na nulu i rast postaje kvadratni.** "That is the difference between making money and building a fortune." Eksplicitno napominje da je ovo **antiteza onoga što ti ceo marketinški svet govori.** Zašto većina nema word of mouth: **surplus jednostavno nije dovoljno velik.**

**Netflix kao kanonski primer:** ceo Blockbuster katalog on-demand po ceni nižoj od jednog naslova; inkrementalni trošak dodatnog korisnika ~nula.

Povezano `[BSwJQsogah0]`: "It doesn't have to be your ticket price that has to be low — you have to find ways to make customers worth more." / **"Unique, expensive, sticky"** + habit forming. / "Acquiring customers is just too difficult to not have a high LTV." / "It's easier to start with premium things… it's easier to just over-deliver like crazy for a client rather than trying to think about how do I make this scalable. You don't need to worry about that yet."

## 6. Big Four Levers i rangiranje poluga
`[kloJJeiysxg]`

Jedina četiri stvari koje mogu da rastu biznis: **Traffic, Conversion, Price, Churn.** *"If we want to grow the business, those levers have to change or the business will not grow."*
Test za svaku aktivnost: **"The things you're doing have to ladder up to one of these four. If it doesn't ladder up to one of these four things, why are you doing it?"**

- **Constraint thinking:** *"Which of these is the biggest constraint in the business?"* — tu ulaži vreme. Ako je conversion rate na landing page-u nizak a nikad nisi radio na njoj, dan-dva rada = potencijalno duplo rast.
- **Redosled prioriteta za solo operatera (~$50k/mesec):** kreni od **traffic/promotion** — *"this is how you have lifeblood in the business."* Ali samo **dovoljno** prometa da vidiš šta ljudi zaista žele, pa onda proizvod/churn, pa se vrati i skaliraj.
- **Zamka malog preduzetnika:** *"The reason most entrepreneurs, especially small ones, get stuck is that they find something that works and they stop doing it, and then they get distracted by six other new things and get completely spread thin."*
- **MORE → BETTER → NEW → WHO:** nakon što odabereš polugu, odredi **ko je vlasnik** i meri da li radi svoj posao.

**Money game rangiranje poluga** `[F3NyhOtRpOE]`: 2× cena (**6× profita**) > 2× broj kupovina / churn na pola (~2×+) > 2× novih klijenata (**2×, najgore**).
**Multiplikativnost offera** `[F3NyhOtRpOE]`: 2× leadova × 2× konverzija × 2× cena = **8×**.

## 7. Reverse math — dekompozicija cilja i inflow

### Goal Decomposition Ladder
`[BSwJQsogah0]`
"The number one way that I know that someone has no chance is **they don't even know the math behind how to accomplish their goals**." / **"Fuzzy targets don't get hit."**

**Lanac:** godišnji cilj → mesečno → **nedeljno (preporučeni operativni ciklus)** → dnevno → ÷ LTV = broj deal-ova → ÷ close rate = broj održanih poziva → ÷ show-up rate = broj zakazanih termina → ÷ outreach-to-appointment rate = broj outreach akcija dnevno.

**Primer:** $1M/god = **$83.333/mesec ≈ $21K/nedelju ≈ $3.333/dan**. LTV $1.000 → 21 jedinica/nedelju → ~**5 deal-ova dnevno** (namerni overshoot sa 4,2). Close rate 1 od 3 → **15 održanih poziva dnevno**. Show-up 66% → **22 zakazana termina dnevno**. 50 outreach = 1 termin → **1.000 outreach akcija dnevno**.
**Sanity-check:** "a $1,000 deal would not be worth any kind of effort like that… this would probably need to be closer to like **$10,000**."

**Poređenje težine za isti $1M:** **1 × $100K deal mesečno** ("pretty chill") · **9–10 × $10K** ("wow") · **83 × $1K** ("a little harder") · **833 × $100** ("that's a lot of work").

**Ostale brojke:** **top 1% prihoda u Americi = $400.000/god**; **40% net margin = "very good"**; prodavac sa provizijom $1.000 po dealu treba **83 prodaje mesečno** za $1M; dnevni brojevi su previše volatilni **dok ne pređeš $10M+/god** → koristi nedeljni ciklus.

### Inflow model
`[XC_lklN9KmE]`
**Teza: "Your inflow is your bottleneck."** "Most people have goals and are disconnected from the actions required to get there."

**Model:** cilj (broj klijenata) → churn → koliko mesečno moraš potpisati samo za break-even → conversion rate iz front-end programa → koliko front-end prodaja → close rate → koliko sastanaka → show-up rate → koliko zakazanih termina → koliko leadova dnevno → CPL → ad spend. Zatim isto to prevedeš u **vreme na kalendaru** i **broj ljudi u timu**.

**Kompletan primer (300 članova, 10% churn):**
- 300 × 10% = **30 novih mesečno samo za break-even**
- konverzija front-end → članstvo 50% ⇒ **60 front-end signup-a mesečno = 15 nedeljno**
- close rate 50% ⇒ **120 razgovora mesečno ≈ 30 nedeljno**
- prodaja 6 dana nedeljno ⇒ **5 sastanaka dnevno = 5 × 30 min = 2,5–3h prodaje dnevno**
- show-up rate 50% ⇒ **60 zakazanih termina nedeljno**
- unazad do leadova: ~240 konsultacija ⇒ **15 leadova dnevno**
- **15 leadova/dan × $10 CPL = $150/dan = $4.500 ad spend mesečno**
- **i sve to samo da ODRŽIŠ 300 — da RASTEŠ, treba ti duplo.**

**Ostale brojke:** alternativni scenario sa 10 trials nedeljno kao kapacitetom → morao bi konvertovati **75% nedeljno, svake nedelje** — nerealno. Rast od 200 na 400 uz gubitak 20 mesečno: **ne udvostručuješ inflow, treba ti i više**, jer gubiš efikasnost na back-endu. **Prag: ako ne možeš da upišeš 20 novih klijenata nedeljno, nećeš doći do objekta od 200–500 ljudi.** Interni podatak: **prošle nedelje 10 teretana pauziralo GLX — sve zato što je bilo PREVIŠE inflow-a.** Najbolji klijent: **130 novih klijenata za 2,5 nedelje**; istorijski: teretana sa **80 → 280 klijenata za 21 dan**.

**"Acceptable metrics":** "You're not going to have orders of magnitude improvements. You're not going to sell 100% of people who show up... **If you wait for your improvements, you will never start.**"
**Inflow se nikad ne gasi:** "I'm going to turn my ads on for a couple weeks and then turn them off and go service those people — no. If you want to scale, your inflow never stops. You need to be doing both things concurrently."
**Univerzalnost:** "Whether that's a gym or remote or launching anything, or you have a B2B business — it doesn't matter. The metrics are all the same."

## 9. Supply vs. demand constraint — triage i rešenja
`[sGv2BTUCcCM, jqo0lVveh98, N5MExtki_VI]`

**Triage pitanje:** "Is this a business that is **supply constrained** or **demand constrained**?" / `[jqo0lVveh98]`: **"Do you need more customers, or can you not handle the stuff you got?"**
- Demand problem = imaš kapacitet, nemaš kupce. Supply problem = imaš kupce, nemaš kapacitet.
- **Aksiom:** "You can either have too few customers or too little capacity, **but never both at the same time**."
- **DOUBLING TEST:** "If you doubled your ad budget tomorrow, or doubled your outreach, or doubled your content — would you be able to double your sales, or would you just create a mess you couldn't even handle?"
- Ako imaš dovoljno leadova a ne stižeš da isporučiš → marketing NIJE rešenje `[jqo0lVveh98]`: "You're supply constrained and so you're like 'how do I market more?' I'm like '**you can't even take people.**'" / "All of these are not things that you would solve with marketing."
- "I'd rather be supply constrained than demand constrained. But both suck." / Buffett: "There's nothing worse than doing well with something that shouldn't be done at all."

### Rešenja za supply constraint (4 izlaza)
`[sGv2BTUCcCM]` daje tri: (1) drastično dići cene (**50–100%, ne 5%**); (2) promeniti service-to-delivery ratio (1-na-1 → 1-na-više); (3) zaposliti i obučiti još izvršilaca.
`[N5MExtki_VI]` daje četiri, kad si "maxed out on time": (1) **podigni cenu**, (2) **promeni ratio isporuke** (1:1 → 1:4 → 1:6 → 1:many), (3) **produktizuj**, (4) **zaposli ljude.**
**Svako te vodi u DRUGI biznis:** hiring → "recruiting, training and culture business"; productize → "marketing and sales business"; price → "branding business"; ratio → "you just keep doing what you're doing and drive more profit". Pitanje nije koje je tačno, nego **"which problem would you rather solve?"**
- **Greška koju je dijagnostikovao** `[N5MExtki_VI]`: bili su supply constrained a napravili ceo novi jeftiniji proizvod čija je cena isporuke bila ista → ekonomija se raspala. Prvo pitanje timu: *"What problem are we solving?"* Drugo: *"Can we just solve this problem in another way that doesn't include starting an entirely new business?"*
- **Ratio prag:** "after like seven-ish, it's now not enough individual time" — **1:4 je sweet spot za small group.**
- **Kapacitetska matematika:** 10 sati isporuke po klijentu godišnje; 1:1 = 5 poziva/dan × 12 nedelja = max 60 klijenata; 1:4 u 90 min = 4 sesije (6h) dnevno = 24 para/dan → **288 kapacitet.** Cilj 150 klijenata × $30K = **$4,5M (10× trenutnog).**
- **Compound efekat dizanja cena** `[sGv2BTUCcCM]`: više novca + manje klijenata (oslobođeno vreme) + to vreme se ulaže u kreiranje novog kapaciteta. Konsultant: cena ×2, **30% klijenata ode** → "double his income working 15 fewer hours a week".

### Mispriced-by-time model (restoran)
`[sGv2BTUCcCM]` Kapacitet postoji ali je loše raspoređen kroz vreme. Marže obično 10%; **20% poskupljenje = trostruki profit** na tim danima; ako petak+subota nose pola prihoda → **1,5× profit celog biznisa**. Sva tri poteza (poskupljenje vikendom, plaćanje za preskakanje reda, duže radno vreme) → "**100% increases in profit, sometimes more, just from three small changes**." Čekanje vikendom: 90+ minuta.

### Skriveni supply constraint = veština prodaje, ne kapacitet
`[sGv2BTUCcCM]` Vodoinstalater: "they have a supply problem with their sales skills rather than how many jobs they can do." Alat: "give them a two-, three-line script… role play back and forth every morning", meriti % upsell-ovanih. **Upsell 50% → prihod +50%** uz skoro iste troškove.

### Supply constraint kod fizičkih proizvoda
`[sGv2BTUCcCM]` (1) prodaj nešto nematerijalno postojećoj bazi da generišeš keš (garancija/warranty, white-label ili affiliate deal sa komplementarnim proizvodom, osiguranje, dodatne usluge); (2) tim kešom plati dobavljaču više i preskoči red: "Hey, if I paid you 20% more, do you think you'd put my stuff first?" COVID primer: prodaja ×4 preko noći, rokovi 2 → 8 nedelja. "Supply chain is **the** constraint" — veliki igrači imaju redundanciju da eliminišu single points of failure.

### "Avatar constrained" — treća kategorija
`[sGv2BTUCcCM]` Prodaješ pogrešnoj osobi. **Principal-Agent Problem:** vlasnici zgrada su hteli uštedu, ali su **upravnici zgrada** stvarno odlučivali i nisu imali nikakav interes ("I don't want to do any more work… they're not going to get paid more"). Rešenje: prepiši prodajni proces tako da **uzdiže status stvarnog donosioca odluke**. "The foreman of the factory, the guy on the floor, was the one making the call. **So he's the real customer.**"

### Demand leak / funnel audit pre povećanja budžeta
`[sGv2BTUCcCM]` "they're opting in but not scheduling", "scheduling but not showing", "showing but not buying". "If people are the right type of people that are opting in, we have a sales process problem."

### Ostalo
- **~2/3 biznisa je na istom prihodu dve godine** `[sGv2BTUCcCM]`.
- **Marketing budžet benchmark** `[sGv2BTUCcCM]`: softverska firma troši **5%** prihoda na marketing dok konkurenti troše **20–30%** — "They built a great product, but almost nobody knows it exists. They're fighting against irrelevance."
- **Product-vs-Marketing asterisk:** dobar proizvod raste sam SAMO ako korisnici imaju "high surface area exposure to other people that could use that product". Facebook = da; "IT consulting for Fortune 100 companies" = ne — "you're probably going to have to go out and get business."
- **"Products get worse through addition":** "often times you can improve a product a lot by just deleting things… the constant change often times actually erodes the product quality more than the incremental improvement."
- **"Rock and a hard place" stalemate** (nemam para da rastem, ne mogu da rastem bez para) je uvek **strateški problem — cena ili ponuda**, ne operativni.
- **Redosled operacija za servis zaglavljen u "swampu"** `[jqo0lVveh98]` (hiropraktičar, $2,4M/5 godina): (1) **pricing i packaging** — da oslobodiš cash flow; (2) **attribution tracking**; (3) **ads funnel + sales motion**; (4) **content cadence koji demonstrira thought leadership** kao dugoročni "bunar". "**If you're in the swamp, cash flow is actually the biggest thing that you need.**"

## 10. Constraint case studies

### Hiring case — kompletna matematika
`[QtE6kk0158o]` Prodajni menadžer dva kvartala zaredom promašio cilj "dodati 6 outbound repova". Constraint je bio u HR-u:
- kvalifikovanih kandidata nedeljno: **1**
- intervjua po jednom kvalifikovanom kandidatu: **5**
- finalnih intervjua po zaposlenju: **4**
- ⇒ **20 ljudi kroz proces za 1 novog repa**
- churn na toj poziciji: **~20% po kvartalu**
- ⇒ 10 outbound ljudi × 20% = **−2/kvartal**; proces daje 1 novog mesečno = **+3/kvartal** ⇒ **neto +1/kvartal**, a cilj je bio +6. **Matematički nemoguć cilj.**
- **Rešenje:** 1-na-1 intervjui su bili nepotrebni ("miscommunication, she thought we had to do that") → prešli na **5 grupnih intervjua u 1 sat nedeljno**.
- **Cena greške:** svaki outbound rep generiše **~$2M godišnjeg prihoda** → cilj od 6 repova = **$12M**; izgubljeno **$10–12M** kroz dva kvartala.
- Lekcija: "I had been driving this guy and putting my pressure on this guy when he was **NOT the constraint of the system**."
- **Frontline churn benchmark:** "Frontline positions in general tend to churn more than manager and leadership positions."

### Enterprise software case (najoštriji broj)
`[sGv2BTUCcCM]` **10 demonstracija nedeljno, 2 zatvorena deala GODIŠNJE** = "you're closing 2 out of 500 demos"; stagnacija **8 godina**, retencija 98%. Njihov plan: udvostručiti sales tim. Hormozi: "Let's just figure out how we can get 500 demonstrations to turn into 100 deals instead of 2. **It's a 50x right there.**"

### Constraint kod mega-influencera
`[KQuyQpFANpA]` 30–40M pratilaca, **100M pregleda mesečno čak i kad ne objavljuju ništa novo**; hteli su "još bolje videe". Hormozi: **97/100 u marketingu, 0/100 u prodaji, proizvodu i customer success-u.** Constraint nije 97→99, nego **0→20 na ostalim odeljenjima**.

### Funnel pre intervencije (challenge model)
`[N5MExtki_VI]` 511 leadova po challengeu → 26 attendees (**5,25% show rate**) → 12 booked calls → 10 held → 6 sales. Cena po attendeeju **$815.** 6 challengea godišnje, prosečno **$19.440 ad spenda po challengeu**; 90–95% prodaje dolazi sa vrha funnela.
**Nova ekonomika:** 25K paid in full / 30K na payment planu; **9K down (kvartal unapred)**; ostatak $2.500–3.000/mesec počinje ODMAH (pauza pravi churn).
**Procena efekta izmena:** offer = 5× na ceni; date-night angle = 1–4×; organic→ads = 1,5–2× niži CAC; renewals = produžen LTV od druge godine.
**Ad Machine / "fusion reactor":** normalna funkcija biznisa treba da PROIZVODI marketing. *"One person advertises. That's me. That starts the engine. Customer comes in. Customer has great experience. That experience gets documented. That experience gets advertised. That experience from that customer gets the next customer."*

### Ne skaliraj pre nego što jedinična ekonomija radi
`[_8olk4-VFP8]` "the most important piece, and this is where everyone fails, is they're just not profitable enough." Klijent: 3 lokacije, **~$100k profita godišnje** (≈$30k po lokaciji); **sa jednom lokacijom je ranije zarađivao $40.000 mesečno.** Benchmark za franšizu: **30%+ marže.** *"If you're doing $50,000 net per facility, no one's interested in that."* **Konsolidacija umesto ekspanzije.**
- **"Asset on the balance sheet" test:** tek kad jedna jedinica raste **bez tebe**, sa incentiviziranim timom, i daje visok povrat na kapital — imaš imovinu koju možeš prodati, licencirati ili franšizirati.

## 11. Model > metod
`[i7bLRKwKSms]`

- "This is not a tactic. It's not a funnel. It's actually a **ratio**." Metode su one-trick ponies i istaknu; modeli traju. **"The model is the competitive moat. A method is a one trick."** / "Methods expire."
- **Pravilo #1 biznisa: "Don't go out of business."** Ono što te drži u igri je **cash flow**. **99% vlasnika nema investitore** — model MORA da pravi keš.
- **Dijagnostika:** "If you don't understand your business math, you'll continue to blame other things — 'Facebook doesn't work for me', 'outbound doesn't work for me', 'content doesn't work for me'. **Is it the method that's not working, or is it the model?**"

**Teretane — dva modela:** industrijski standard "LBO / low barrier offer" = **$21 za 21 dan**, pa konverzija na **$99/mesec**; CAC ~$100, konvertuje **1 od 3** → realno **~$300 za $99 članarinu** koja traje **6 meseci**. Hormozijev model: **~$1.000 u prvih 30 dana** po kupcu. Poređenje **$21 vs. $1.000** — ko koga može da nadmaši u aukciji pažnje.

**Black box otvaranja teretane:** $5.000 u banku + potpisan lease → **$100/dan na oglase → 10 leadova/dan → 2 zatvaranja → ~$600 svaki = $1.200 dnevno**. Bilans: 4.900 → 6.100 → repeat. Pri $100/dan **~$30.000 profita u prvom mesecu**; on je trošio **$500/dan** → **~$100.000 prodaje u pre-sale periodu**, a otvaranje teretane je koštalo "nešto manje od $100.000". Rezultat: **cash-flow pozitivna 6 nedelja kasnije; 6 lokacija otvoreno iz keš flow-a u ranim dvadesetim.**

### 5 faza skaliranja (Dave Ramsey)
`[-HJg4TYBgtI]` Iz coachinga ~10.000 malih firmi kroz 30+ godina. Namerno **nisu vezane za prihod** ("HVAC sa 20 kamiona i 70 zaposlenih i dalje može da radi glupe stvari tri sloja ispod sebe").

3. (srednja) — **strategic thought:** "You don't have to run into the wall. If you turn right and then turn left, you go around the wall. It's a lot less energy." ("I taught them how to work, they taught me strategic thought.")
4. **Pathfinder** — sistemi, procesi i planiranje na mestu; "you're bailing money now".
5. **Legacy** — sukcesija, endgame, **bench depth**, family-office mentalitet.

**6 DRIVERS** koji te guraju kroz faze (ciklično, **10–15 puta** kroz 5 faza): (1) **personal growth** — "**I am the problem and I'm the solution. I'm the thing holding the business back at every stage**", (2) purpose, (3) **people**, (4) kultura/core values, (5) profit, (6) planning.
Zašto je jasan put sam po sebi proizvod: 7 Baby Steps (**12 miliona prodatih primeraka**) — "when people have a clear path, it gives them hope and they don't have **paralysis of analysis**."

`[KQuyQpFANpA]` (1) What problem are we trying to solve? (2) **Over what time horizon?** ("If you want to make more money tomorrow, the number of things you can do is very limited. If you want to make more money in 10 years, you take a very different play.") (3) Which problem would we PREFER solving / feel more equipped to solve? (4) Odgovori u odnosu na **svoj skill set, resurse i iskustvo**.

**Size vs. shape of the slice** `[KQuyQpFANpA]`: "It's the size of the slice of the pie that matters, not the shape of the slice." + **"The more people you make money, the more money you ultimately make."** Prosečan osnivač pri IPO-u poseduje **12,5%** kompanije koju je osnovao.

## 13. Četiri "rock and a hard place" problema
`[F84olnKkseM]`

1. **Ne znaš ko ti je idealni kupac / ne umeš da kažeš NE** (4-kolonska vežba, vidi §6.8 originala).
2. **2A — Previše plaćaš (comp struktura).** Jedna jedinica (jedan rep, jedan terapeut, jedna lokacija) mora biti profitabilna **na punom kapacitetu**. Primeri neodrživosti: frozen yogurt gde radnik dobija **20% revenue**; fizioterapija gde terapeut dobija **50% revenue**. Pravilo: "**at scale, your salesman shouldn't be able to make more than the owners**." Unit-of-capacity primeri: **jedan rep drži 10 klijenata za agenciju; jedan fizioterapeut drži 50 pacijenata.**
   **2B — Premalo naplaćuješ.** Simptom: "puni smo kapacitetom a ne zarađujemo".
3. **Over-expansion / "getting over your skis"** — skaliraš problem, ne rešenje. Uzrok: cilj postavljen kao **output** (revenue) umesto kao **input**. Dijagnoza: **"You have bodies and not brains"** — "They're dumb because you didn't train them, and the real dummy is you."
4. **Fokus.** **"Commitment is the elimination of alternatives."** Focus se meri "by the number and quality of things I say no to". Etimologija: *decision* od *decidere* = "to cut off / to kill off". Pitanje za sebe svaki dan: "What are you killing today? What really juicy, incredibly sexy opportunity that you know you could crush do you turn down today because you made a commitment?"
5. **Bonus: product, not business** (vidi §5.2 originala).

**Prateća pravila:**
- **"Revenue is a consequence, not a goal."** / **"Quality creates growth; growth creates bloat. If you make growth the goal, bloat is what occurs. If you make quality the goal, growth occurs as a result."** / "Growth is not a goal. Growth is an outcome of inputs." `[reisEL_D7xc]`
- **Push or pivot:** "Nine times out of 10 you have to push."
- **Owner vs. CEO test:** "Do I own this business, or does the business own me? If it owns you, then you're a CEO."
- **The Fallacy of Success:** "the real question is not 'did it work' but 'was there an alternative that would have also worked'."
- **Rast od 50% s postojeće baze je daleko lakši nego od nule do te baze.**
- **Kill the vampire** `[3yAiVjcImQ4]`: ubij deo biznisa koji siše keš i pažnju. "Every hour you were spending on the unprofitable work was an hour not spent on scaling the services." Kontra-iluzija o softveru: "People think 'oh, I put some code together, I'm going to have a 10x on my topline.' If you think that, you're not going to get it. People who are actually in software are going all-in and it's still incredibly hard."
- **Meeting framework** `[qel9bf653Es]`: "What's the point of this meeting, what problem are we solving?" i "Who's required to solve it? If we know what the decision is, then I'm not needed." Rezultat: sat vremena zakazano, gotovo za 28 minuta. **"The most expensive cost in almost all companies is meetings."**

## 14. Četiri tipa biznisa — oblici krive
`[qsXxckCbci0]`
Meta-teza: **"Most people think that there is something inherently wrong with their business when it is in reality a feature of this business, not a bug."** (Dostupan deo transkripta pokriva 2 od 4 tipa.)

### E-COMMERCE — stepenasta kriva
Platoi na tri ograničenja: (1) cash constraint (nema para za inventar), (2) traffic constraint (oglasi prestanu da skaliraju), (3) distribucija maksimalno iskorišćena. Skokovi dolaze od **supply chain ili distribution unlocka** ("we got 7,000 more 7-Elevens... then we stay at that level until we get CVS").
- **Rast je zaključan free cash flow-om:** "You might have 300 units worth of demand and you only have 10 units worth of profit. Your growth rate will be dictated by the amount of free cash flow the business kicks off."
- **"Smash and grab" (media arbitraža) vs. brand:** "you can buy eyeballs at $10 and sell them something where you make $11 in gross profit. That's just a smash and grab business. And if somebody else sees you smashing and grabbing, guess what they do?"
- **Redundancy kao obavezan dizajn:** raw materials, manufacturing, 3PL, lending partneri — u svakoj karici rezerva, jer "you have core dependencies on other businesses. And if those businesses fail, you fail." Njegov book launch: **tri 3PL-a u različitim državama** da isporuče milione primeraka za **72 sata**. Lead time forecasting: "if it's a 12-week lead time for you to buy stuff, you have to predict how much you're going to be selling in 12 weeks."
- **"Perfect e-commerce company":** sopstvena proizvodnja → trade secrets/unikatni sastojci → decentralizovana proizvodnja po tržištima → jak pick-pack-ship → mršav performance marketing tim uz brand-first tim → maloprodajna distribucija ili flagship radnje → **savage email marketing** → izuzetan proizvod.
- **Obrnuti redosled kanala danas:** nekad brick-and-mortar → online (Lululemon); danas online-first → flagship radnje (Gymshark). Kad je brand awareness zasićen, radnje svuda — "the actual cost of acquiring the customer becomes rent divided by sales per month".
- Paradoks: e-commerce firme idu "to hundreds of millions a year and pretty much only live on their salaries" jer profit stalno ide u inventar. "No gigantic businesses are drop shipping." / "Great product will always find great distribution. Great distribution will always find great product." / "The downside of physical products is that because anybody can own it, anybody can also sell it."

### SERVICE — spora, ravna, prava linija naviše
"Slow and steady", jer je people-heavy. **78% biznisa u SAD su servisni biznisi.**
- **Tri dela svakog biznisa: attraction → conversion → delivery.** Kod servisa **samo attraction može biti stvarno skalabilan**.
- **Centralna asimetrija:** "As you scale, it is easier to go find more product to sell than it is to find exceptional people. Significantly harder."
- **Servis MORA biti dizajniran sa višim gross marginama** — ne zato što ih prirodno ima, nego zato što mu treba **jastuk za volatilnost**: **"We need some cushion for the pushing."** Konkretno: "if our conversion rate drops in half because our new sales guy's not as good — he's got to get up to speed in 30 days — then we've got to be able to eat that."
- **Paradoks kvaliteta:** "The better you get at that thing, the harder it is to get more people who are as good as you at that thing — which makes it: the more you can charge, the harder it is to get more people that you can charge that money for and still deliver that quality."
- **Dve vrste servisa:** **coaching/teaching** (lako se zapošljava — "it's almost like hiring musicians, plenty of people would do it for free" — ali teško se prodaje i teško zadržava klijent) vs. **done-for-you** (krov, garaža, računovodstvo, pravo, izrada sajta): **lako se prodaje i lako zadržava** ("I'm going to give you the outcome and you don't have to do anything"), ali je **talenat usko grlo** — "you will almost always be supply constrained".
- **Zašto je servis najmanje rizičan:** "if revenue goes down, you can always cut headcount... you can always cut it all the way back down to just you doing work for money." Cash flow model: "You sold work for $100 grand, your cost was $50, there's $50 left over — and next month the same." Nema kapitalno intenzivnog reinvestiranja. Poređenje: "Two of the other business models — the founder almost never makes any money until an exit. And let's be real, exits are far rarer than people try and make them out to be."
- **Veliki problem = REKRUTACIJA, ONBOARDING I TRENING.** "If you're like 'we don't really do that very well in our business' — no, that's why most service businesses don't scale." **"You're actually in a teaching business."** "People expect 'I'm going to train some guy in a weekend to do something that's taken me 10 years' and then get upset that he's not that good. Ridiculous."
- **Definicija profita kroz value-add na ljude:** "A business is the difference between the value of the inputs and the value of the outputs... You take raw materials — people who are underskilled — you have a way of bringing them in, adding value, giving them skills, and then you can sell that skill set at a higher premium later."
- **Founder-as-best-technician je dvosekli mač:** "People tend to always come to you in order to solve the big complex problems, which makes your life big and complex all the time." Cilj: **"How many ways can you reduce the business's dependence on your technical expertise? How can I productize my own knowledge?"**
- **Put do pobede u servisu (redosled):** (1) izaberi avatar — "Most service businesses in the beginning do any kind of work for any kind of person with a pulse"; (2) svedi na **1–3 konzistentna deliverabla**; (3) sistematizuj/produktizuj isporuku → šire gross margine; (4) napravi trening za svaki korak; (5) **zameni sebe u operacijama**; (6) definisani career path-ovi za talenat. Cilj: **"get to the point in a service model where your DEMAND is the constraint. Very rare but doable."**
- **Gde reinvestirati keš:** **kvalitet ulaznog talenta** — "if you're training a genius versus training a dodo bird, training the genius is still going to work better. We want higher base level people, higher horsepower, higher intelligence."
- Spektar talenta: **maids** (najniži skill — a i tu "finding people who speak English, show up on time, are competent") → **trades** (HVAC, vodoinstalateri, električari) → **white collar** (računovođe, advokati, konsultanti) gde postoje višegodišnji learning path-ovi.

## 15. Vozila za skaliranje usluge
`[O1As2zxy0es]`

- **Private chain pravilo palca:** otvaraj sopstvene lokacije ako build-out košta **manje od 6 meseci profita lokacije u prvoj godini** (ilustracija: build-out $40–50k vs. $400k godišnjeg profita).
- **Franšiza:** startni trošak **~$750.000** samo na setup fees, pravne troškove i registracije ("and that's just to get started"); vreme do profita **1–2 godine**; **upfront franchise fee ~$50.000 i on ceo ode na akviziciju** franšizanata; **ima smisla tek preko 100 lokacija**, a **"90 to 95 percent of franchises don't hit over 100 locations."** "The franchise is not the end goal, it is the beginning of the next journey."
- **Licensing:** najniži trošak, najveći cash flow odmah, najmanja enterprise value. "For licensing it's speed… but you're going to sacrifice a lot of money on the exit." Treba **80%+ godišnja retencija** licencijata.
- **Software:** najskuplje i najsporije, najveći exit multiple; treba **80%+ retencija**. Upozorenje sa cenom: "$2 million, $3 million of development later I've made the mistakes, let me give you a three million dollar gift, don't do it." I: "if you don't have a software co-founder who knows how to code, knows user experience, knows how to build engineering teams — do not try and outsource it to a third party team."
- **Case study:** portfolio kompanija prebačena iz licensinga u private chain jer su licencijati previše zarađivali od sistema; **$0,5M → $12M godišnje za 14 meseci**, 12 lokacija, nova lokacija svakog meseca.

**Franchise trap — name + system + fee** `[VxKwz6hBVZU]`: sva tri = franšiza; sva tri bez registracije = **ilegalna franšiza**. Biraš dva od tri: name+system bez fee; name+fee bez sistema (CrossFit); **system+fee bez imena (Gym Launch)**.

## 16. Franšiza vs. sopstvene lokacije — matematika
`[BHMeYaHEMpc]`

**4-varijabilni checklist za skaliranje lokalnog biznisa:** (1) **cost vs. return po svakom uloženom dolaru**; (2) **effort po jedinici** — centralizovan (drag na tebe) vs. decentralizovan (radi franšizant); (3) **matematika na skali** — obrni cilj neto vrednosti u broj jedinica; (4) **tip preduzetnika** — *promotional* (voli prodaju, marketing, "sizzle") vs. *product/operations/leadership driven*.

**Brojke:**
- Teeth whitening franšiza — **cost to open $50k → $500k top line/god → $250k bottom line** = ~**5× return on capital** (50% margina).
- **Prag: ispod ~100% return on capital → obično ima više smisla franšizirati odmah.**
- Kontra-primer: **McDonald's — $1,1–1,2M da se otvori, prosečno $150k/god = ~15% rate of return.**
- **Franšizni ekonomiks:** **7,5% royalty na top line** + marketinški fond; 7,5% na $500k ≈ **$35k/god po lokaciji**, pri 50% margini → **~$20k neto po lokaciji**.
- **Multipli:** franšiza ~**15× earnings**, sopstvene lokacije ~**8× earnings** → **8× na $250k = $2,0M po sopstvenoj lokaciji** vs. **15× na $20k = $300k po franšiznoj**.
- **Reverse-engineering: $100M exit = 50 sopstvenih lokacija ILI 333 franšiznih.**
- Zašto investitor plaća 15×: ugovorom je **zagarantovano** otvaranje preostalih jedinica — "it's legal, it has to happen".
- Compounding pri 5×: "1 million to 5 million, five to twenty five, twenty five to 125".
- Realnost brzine: sopstvene lokacije **1–2 mesečno**; franšizori u praksi često samo **3–4 lokacije** jer je **ograničenje interno, ne eksterno**.

**Investor hat** `[BHMeYaHEMpc]`: "You have to switch from your business hat to your investor hat." / "We will always build an asset so that **it is sellable, not so that we sell it**." / Reframe ličnosti: "If you phrase the thing that you don't like as a deficiency in the business, then it becomes very solvable."

## 17. Komoditizovani biznis — froyo case
`[xZ8d9g6BcKM]`

Prosečna froyo radnja: **$2.100/dan**, ~500 čaša dnevno, prosečna čaša 8 oz → **$750.000–800.000 godišnje.** **Marža 10–15%.** Prosečan vlasnik nosi kući **$93.000 godišnje = ~$328/dan.**
**Franšizna naknada ~6% od top line-a** = na 10% marži **60% vlasnikovog profita.** *"franchises will structure their fees so you make just enough that you can keep going... but not so much that you're going to get rich."*
**Sastav čaše:** 75% jogurt, 25% dodaci. Trošak jogurta **~$0,08/oz**, naplaćuje se **$0,50–0,60/oz**; dodaci koštaju $0,10–0,40/oz a naplaćuju se $0,25–0,60/oz → na voću se izlazi na nulu ili gubi.
*"if you want to run a 50% margin business, you'd have to take 20% of your top line and pay every other bill — very hard to do."*
**Kupovina opreme sa foreclosure aukcija:** njegova prva teretana — **sva oprema propale teretane za $13.000** (osvežavao stranicu svake nedelje). Pre snimanja proverio: 3 propale froyo franšize, najskuplja kompletna oprema $20.000.
**Cena leada koju je bio spreman da plati: $2** (50% popusta na čašu za broj telefona).
**Beat the bottom 25%:** *"It doesn't take a lot to win most times. Just to beat everybody you got to be better than the bottom 25% and you make money."* **Komoditizovano tržište = prilika** — nema "Chick-fil-A sveta jogurta" jer su franšizori optimizovali za prodaju lokacija umesto za izgradnju brenda.

## 18. Fizički proizvodi, e-commerce i CPG

### Model fizičkog proizvoda od nule
`[LvHDT0ZxSmw]`
Cena na tržištu ~$0,30 po traci; pakovanja 30/60/90; ciljna maloprodaja **$19,99–$24,99** (split-test), premium sa custom reči **$30**. **Gross margin ~$16 na $20 proizvodu (80%)**, COGS je skoro u celosti shipping (**$2,50–$4** zavisno od 3PL-a).
**Model prihoda:** 10.000 poseta mesečno → **5% konverzija** = 500 kupaca → **10% churn** → ravnoteža **5.000 pretplatnika** → **$100.000 mesečno prihoda / $80.000 profita ≈ $1M godišnjeg profita**.
**Influencer ekonomija:** influenceru **40% marže**, njemu ostaje ~40% (20% COGS). **100 influencera po 1/10 njegovog volumena = 10× volumen ≈ $10M** → **~$3–4M EBITDA** → valuacija **$40–60M** (~10–15× EBITDA).

### ButcherBox
`[orMbq2LtzKE]`
Bruto marža cilj **≥30%**, neto marža **~6%**; ciljani profit po kutiji na startu **$20**; prva kutija 8–10 funti govedine za **$129**, danas **$169**; putanja prihoda: 2015 → $200K → $500K → $5M → $33M → $105M → $220M → COVID **$450M** → $550M → $570M → prva godina pada (2023) → ponovni rast; **1,7–1,75M domaćinstava** primilo bar jednu kutiju.

**"$500 million trap"** `[orMbq2LtzKE]`: desi se nešto loše → zaposliš gomilu ljudi → svi počnu da govore "ne" → firma iz "bunch of pirates, very little process" prelazi u proces-tešku organizaciju i rast stane. Konkretno: **85 → 240 zaposlenih**, menadžeri koji upravljaju menadžerima gde "one person had one person underneath them" → rez nazad na **~170**. **Barbell hiring:** "really young and really old worked incredibly well."

**VC kao sudar dva biznis modela** `[orMbq2LtzKE]`: VC zarađuje na *raspoređivanju* kapitala (management fee) → "Just tell them how much money you can spend over the next 18 to 24 months and just argue for a bigger number." Custommade: kupljen za $140.000 (depozit $2.500, 90 dana due diligence-a iskorišćeno za prikupljanje novca); sa **$35.000 godišnje na $60.000 mesečno**; runde $500k convertible → $1,9M na $7M pre-money → $4M na $16M → $18M; GMV $3–4M mesečno uz proviziju ~7% = ~$200k prihoda mesečno **uz burn od $500.000 mesečno**; 8 godina, 50 otpuštenih, friendly foreclosure, osnivač ostao sa ~8%.

**"Solving for zero"** `[orMbq2LtzKE]`: ne pitaj "kako da napravim veliki biznis" nego **"kako da zaradim novac koji mi ne troši vreme."** Paradoks: biznisi građeni kao "velika stvar" propali su; oni građeni kao hobi postali su veliki. "If you build something to be a hobby, it can remain a hobby."
**"Who, not what/how"** `[orMbq2LtzKE]`: umesto da uči industriju, našao je bivšeg šefa operacija Omaha Steaks kome je istekao non-compete. "You can either relive his life and then be 20 years older, or you can just find that guy… and everybody wins."
**Mentorski frame** `[orMbq2LtzKE]`: "Right before you go to take your business to market, switch sides of the table and say: you just bought your business today. What are all the things you'd fix about it? Well — maybe just do those first."

### CPG / RETAIL specifično
`[rj7rzOdj84A]`
- **Cocktail case: 1.000 distributivnih tačaka, samo $1,5M godišnje prihoda** → sell-through rate katastrofalno nizak, nula profita, otpušten ceo tim.

## 19. Valuacija, exit i prodajivost

### Isti trud, različit ishod po tipu biznisa
`[IFpHasZ7jN4]` Servis **~5× profita**; e-commerce **~2× top-line**; SaaS/software **10–20× top-line**. Konkretno: servis $10M top-line / $3M profita → ~$15M vrednosti; udvostručenje → **+$15M**. E-com isti brojevi → ~$20M, udvostručenje → **+$20M**. Software → $100–200M, udvostručenje → **+$200M**. **Isti trud, 13× razlika u stvorenoj vrednosti.**

### Institutional threshold
`[rc7cxL7ql7Y]` (koristi se kao declarative hook u prodaji): biznis od **$2M → $5M revenue: multiple skače sa 3× na 7×** → vrednost sa ~$4M na ~$35M *"just by adding another $3 million in revenue."*
**Scale premium** `[VxKwz6hBVZU]`: rast sa $100M na $150M je *"same level of work, sometimes easier"* nego 0→$1M, plus **veći EBITDA multiple kako rasteš.**

### Rollup matematika
`[VxKwz6hBVZU]` Pojedinačna računovodstvena firma se prodaje na **~4× earnings**; agregat od 10–20 firmi **~12× earnings**. Čak i ako organizator uzme **20–30%** od 12×, svako prolazi **dvostruko bolje** nego samostalno. Ritam: **exit svake 1–2 godine, po ~$100 miliona.**
**Volume premium u investiranju:** *"In investing it's a volume premium. Big money is lazy — they will pay a premium to not have to do as many deals."*
**Dental association case:** **700 stomatologa**, svaki troši **$20–30.000 godišnje** na potrošni materijal → group buying co-op gde **ušteda premašuje cenu članarine**. Zatim rollup **top 11 stomatologa** prodat za **$120 miliona**; PE je kupio i gusku i jaja jer je video preostalih 700 kao **"our egg pipeline for the next 5 years"**.

### Diverzifikacija kanala kao enterprise value
`[rj7rzOdj84A]` **TikTok ads primer:** gubitak **$100.000 kroz 6 meseci** da bi se otvorio drugi akvizicioni kanal na biznisu sa $2M EBITDA i 5× multiplom → dodaje **$10–15M enterprise value.** *"Will you invest $100,000 and wait six months for a $10 million return?"* / *"I don't see me losing money on the ads as losing money — I see that as investing in something that's going to increase the enterprise value of my company."*
**Diverzifikacija akvizicionih kanala povećava enterprise value čak i uz nula dodatnog profita.**
**Portfolio power law:** **22 deala u 2,5 godine → 4 deala nose 90% povrata** `[rj7rzOdj84A]`.

### Anatomija "$100M exita"
`[okA9Yt2KZuk]` $100M → ~$60M upfront + $10M seller note (5 god.) + $20M earnout + 10% rolled equity → minus broker 3–4% (~$2,4M) → minus bonusi timu → minus escrow → minus 20% capital gains ≈ **~$40M stvarno.** *"Your $100 million sale is $40 million bucks."*
*"The wealthiest people in the world buy and they build and then they REFINANCE. So you don't need to sell. The only reason that you would sell is because you think the party's over."* / *"Fuck the sale. Match the conditions. What does your life look like after the sale, tactically? Is there a way that we can make your life look this way BEFORE the sale and keep the damn thing?"*
**Prodaja firme** `[rc7cxL7ql7Y]`: *"businesses will always go up in value until the day they don't, and then they're worth nothing."*

### Goose vs. eggs i prodajivost
`[VxKwz6hBVZU]`
- **"There's something that creates value, and then there's the thing that is valuable."** Guska (edukacija/coaching/media/brend/audience) je vrlo vredna ali **neprodajiva**; jaja (operativni biznisi koje guska proizvodi) su prodajiva. **"Most people are trying to sell the goose rather than sell the eggs. You never want to sell the goose."**
- **Dva kriterijuma prodajivosti:** (1) **Zavisi li išta od tebe?** (keyman risk); (2) **postoji li sticky/recurring komponenta** — kod tebe ili kod tvojih klijenata.
- **Validacija kroz M&A aktivnost:** izguglaj **"[tip biznisa] M&A activity"**. Nema je → nema tržišta. **"Think of your business as a product, and an investor or potential acquirer as a customer."**
- **Ako si veći od firme — ne možeš izaći, ali možeš prodati manjinski udeo** (5–10% pri visokoj valuaciji): liquidity event, ne exit.
- **Zašto je Gym Launch bio prodajiv:** keyman uklonjen (nije bio u delivery-ju ni u ads-ima, imao leadership tim), **recurring revenue**, dobra godišnja retencija, **više akvizicionih kanala**, i firma je narasla da bude veća od njega.

### Valuacioni primeri po industriji
`[thDTmy7VGIw]` Treatment center na osiguranju **~10× multiple**, cash-pay **~5–6×**, ali cash-pay naplaćuje **2–3× više po danu (~$3.000/dan)** → povrat izjednačen ili bolji. B2B softver: *"teško je naterati 5.000 klijenata da plaćaju $1.000/mesec"* — istu sumu kroz servis je znatno lakše.

## 21. Arbitraža, kapital i kompenzacija

### Arbitraža kao suština biznisa
`[ovL6Z5z0jxQ]` **"All business actually comes down to some sort of arbitrage — you know how to do something for less than it costs someone else to do it... the arbitrage between what it cost you and what you charge them is how you make a profit."** Tri vrste: **geografska** (medijalni prihod Tajland ~$2.700/god vs. US ~$50–60.000 = **>10×**; "The number one money-making skill in 2024 is English"), **network** i **troškovna**.

### Wealth is a ratio
`[ovL6Z5z0jxQ]` "how much you spend relative to how much you make... You can either make more or need less."
Njegova lestvica po satu: **17 god $6/h → 21 god $25/h → 23 god $250/h → 26 god $2.500/h → 28 god $18.000/h.**
Pre teretane: $50.000 ušteđeno, ~$4.000/mesec od online treninga, soba za $400/mesec, hrana iz Costco.

### Formula za kompenzaciju
`[ovL6Z5z0jxQ]` "You will be compensated for the amount of value you create, your ability to negotiate, how replaceable that skill is, and most importantly, **the risk you're willing to take on**." ## 22. Opportunity score i izbor prilike
`[4pZwlE86A5Y]`

- **Formula:** **"Number of units sold × gross profit potential per unit ÷ competitive moat."** Dva legitimna ekstrema: malo ljudi × mnogo para, ili mnogo ljudi × malo para.

### "100 GOLDEN BBs, NO SILVER BULLETS"
`[IFpHasZ7jN4]` Munger "lollapalooza effect": *"It's two, three, five, six things that all have an incremental boost, but when you multiply all them together create this insane outcome."* **Silver-bullet fallacy** = verovanje da postoji jedna poluga koja rešava sve.
- **"One point of leverage" vežba** `[m5ordaa7NN4]`: provedi dan ili nedelju na pitanju — *"If I could only have ONE thing be true about our business, we will win no matter what."* Pošto je za vrh potrebno 100× rada, možeš biti dobar u **vrlo malo stvari.** *"When you realize how much work it takes to get good, you realize how few things you can get good at."*

## 23. Good hard vs. bad hard i reframe krivice
`[rj7rzOdj84A]`

- **Good hard** = osnovne pretpostavke iz prvih principa i dalje stoje; problem je iterativan (klikovi → opt-ini → kvalitet lida → messaging → targeting → lead magnet). **Guraj.**
- **Bad hard** = neka od osnovnih pretpostavki je **dokazano netačna** (podacima). **Pivotiraj.**
- *"Before we make this big investment, these are the assumptions that we believe to be true"* — zapiši ih unapred; to je litmus test.
- **Concrete wall + sledgehammer:** znaš da je iza zida nagrada, ne znaš debljinu zida.
- **Enterprise value framing:** svaki teški problem prevedi u dolarsku vrednost sa druge strane zida.
- *"Entrepreneurs get paid to solve big problems, and the bigger the problem the bigger the payoff."* / *"What got you from zero to a million, or 1 to 10 million, isn't what's going to get you from 10 to 30."* / *"The biggest opportunity is the one that's in front of you."*
- **Schwarzman "hard is a constant"** `[FMzKk73iUhw]`: mali biznis je teško graditi, veliki biznis je teško graditi — težina je ista, upside drastično različit. *"You might as well solve for the biggest thing. This is about resourcefulness, not resources."*

### Reframe krivice u deficit veštine

- *"Whenever someone says this business isn't scalable, you have to break it down to physics — tell me why physically it's impossible to scale."*
- **Frustration tolerance** `[rc7cxL7ql7Y]`: *"your ability to do things that you don't want to do without an immediate reward for an extended period of time."* To je veština, ne osobina.

## 24. Constraint-first učenje i prioritizacija eksperimenata
`[spXH1kJ6q-Q, rc7cxL7ql7Y]`

- **Constraint-first:** identifikuj JEDAN constraint, pa ga "bombarduj" svim kanalima odjednom (više knjiga + svi videi + plaćeni mentor koji ti pregleda rad uživo) dok ne prestane da bude problem. Sprint: **15 knjiga za 2 nedelje**, ne radeći ništa drugo. *"I take too little action around what the constraint is"* je najčešća greška.
- **Specificity ↔ skill inverse law:** *"the specificity of a request is inversely correlated with the skill of the recipient."* "Outreach" je bundled term sa 100 "how"-ova ispod.
- **Cost-of-test matrica:** svaki test ima **garantovan** trošak (cost of change, greške, retreniranje) ali **negarantovan** upside. Zato rangiraš: **low-cost / high-potential-output prvi.** Split test subject line-a = jeftin test; promena offera = skup test.
- **Idea quarantine:** sve ideje idu u jedan dokument i **odleže mesec dana** pre odluke — emotivna investiranost u svežu ideju iskrivljuje procenu. Filter: *"If I forget the idea, it wasn't that good of an idea."* Bezos: *"if I had 30 minutes I could write a 100 ideas on the whiteboard that would destroy my company."* Ima ~1 novu ideju dnevno, firma izvršava ~1 po kvartalu.

## 26. Beta / consultative proces — kako izvući proizvod iz baze
`[okA9Yt2KZuk, y3T4GqoH0Us]`

1. Pitaj klijente koje probleme imaju; oni **upvote**-uju najbolniji. Postavi **objektivni kvalifikator** ("ko je imao ispod 3% mesečnog churn-a šest meseci zaredom?").
2. Uzmi **top 1–2%** koji taj problem rešavaju dobro (**20 od ~1.000 članova**) → round table. Nagrada im je besplatan mastermind sa tobom.
3. Intervjuiši jednog po jednog u petlji: *"And then what do you do? And then how does that work? And then why do you do that?"* Zapiši sve (dobio 4 strane beleški), pa **rekategorizuj** i ukloni ponavljanja.
4. **Sistem glasanja:** ono što rade SVI = bitno. Ono što radi 1 od 20 = nebitno. **"The point here is not to do all of the things they do, but to do the things that ALL of them do."** (Lista za churn je imala **180 stavki; samo 5** koje su radili baš svi ušlo je u rešenje.) *"The goal is: how few of these things are the things that are actually driving the outcome?"*
5. Produktizuj i daj **donjih 10% + srednjim** klijentima kroz portal, bez dodatne pomoći (da ne kontaminiraš test), cadence 1–2× nedeljno, meri rezultate za **30 dana.**
6. Ako radi — **"A process drives the innovation."** Ako ne radi — **i dalje objavi rezultat.**

Logika: *"If you sit on top of a community, all of the solutions to all of the problems that you have already exist in that community. Someone has solved it."*
