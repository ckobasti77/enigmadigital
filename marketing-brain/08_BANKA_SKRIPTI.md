## 1. Outreach / cold call / DM / setting

### 1.1 Personalizovani otvarači (warm DM / engaged lead)
- "Hey, saw you got a baby" / "Hey, saw you switched jobs" / "Hey, saw you moved" / "Hey, saw you saw that movie last weekend." — *personalizovani otvarač u warm DM-u*
- **ACA (acknowledge / compliment / ask)**, fitness primer: "Oh, so you have two kids" → "Wow, you must be a super mom, working full-time and having two kids" → "Hey, so you're Superman — how do you have time to eat right and work out?"
- "Hey, saw you liked my content — what about it drew you in?" / "What made you do that?" / "Hey, I saw you commented — thanks for that comment and question. Are you looking to get in shape?" — *otvaranje sa engaged leadom; napomena: "don't just say 'thanks for the comment', that's not going to start a conversation — you ask a question"* [HsQeQM1jUeg]
- "Hey — you here for free stuff, or do you want help with your legal stuff?" — *proaktivan DM svakom novom followeru, binarna kvalifikacija* [3yAiVjcImQ4]
- "Tell me about your day." — *pattern-break umesto "how's it going"* [cy2k1GdA-9o]
- "Hey, I'm doing a live thing with other parents — that way you won't feel alone. Come attend." — *poziv iz DM-a na webinar*

### 1.2 Cold call / BDR
- **BDR cold-call otvaranje:** "Hey, we've been calling some gyms in the area. They've been struggling with XYZ, you know, biggest concern one, two, and three. And so what we're doing is like — if gyms don't exist, our company doesn't exist. And so we'd like to just give you some of the stuff that's working well for them. Is that okay? This is from our private client thing, which normally we charge for, but we just want to help gyms out." [okA9Yt2KZuk]
- **BDR zakazivanje:** "So what I'll do is I'll set you up with an appointment with Sean tomorrow, and he'll run through each of these systems for your gym so that you can [insert dream outcome that you want]." [okA9Yt2KZuk]
- **BDR personalizacija:** "Hey, saw that you posted that thing. Yeah, I'm calling from Gym Launch. Don't worry — just want to help you guys out. We're talking to a lot of gyms in the area." — *napomena: "tonality matters"* [okA9Yt2KZuk]
- **Benefit pitch da bi se pojavio na pozivu:** "If you had twice as many people sign up and nothing else changed your life, would that be cool? Cool. He's going to show you how we do it, because this is what the top 1% gyms are doing. We just want to help you out." [okA9Yt2KZuk]
- **Outbound voicemail preko konkurenta:** "Hey [business], I'm calling about [competitor]. Give me a call back." → kad uzvrate: "Oh, it looks like they're kicking your ass" / "I see that they're in the same industry as you and we've reached out to them as well."
- **Poziv opt-in leadu (dve opcije, ne otvoren kalendar):** "John? … It's Alex. I'm calling you because you opted in on Facebook for our promotion, and I just wanted to make sure that one, you weren't a crazy person on the internet — ha — and if there was a time that worked well for you to come in, and obviously to make sure that you knew that we weren't crazy people either." → "I've got 2:00 and 4:00 today, which works better?"
- **Edifikacija closera pri prebacivanju:** "I'm going to put you on with Shawn. Shawn's unbelievable, he helped 400 people just like you get [thing you just said you wanted]… Oh, you know what, I think he has an opening this afternoon, let me see if I can get you in. Hold on two seconds. …Yep, he confirmed, we're good to go, four o'clock. Works? Awesome."
- **24h reminder, B2B:** "Hey John, really excited to meet with you tomorrow. We outlined six things for your business that I think would really help you out… These were things that I pulled from my team and just from looking at the site before we get on the call. So stoked. We're at 4:00 tomorrow — hit me up if there's any issues."
- **5-minutni pre-call research na početku poziva:** "Dude John, how's it going? The Bucks are killing it. How's Joyce doing? ... And Mike — is this your VP of customer success? Hey Mike, nice to meet you man. I was just looking you guys up before I got on — really cool stuff that you guys are doing to [XYZ Corp]." [m5ordaa7NN4]
- **Kad se pojave neočekivani stakeholderi:** "Tell me more about that — oh, that would work well with page two. If you turn to page two in the prep file I have, I think this will be really applicable for you, and when we get there I'll definitely need your insight." [m5ordaa7NN4]
- **Otvaranje poziva sa pripremljenim materijalom:** "Hey, I made this deck — do you mind if I show it to you? I can walk you through kind of the strategy I was thinking about, the phases of rollout that we could do, and what I've already talked to your customers about, what they want." [IFpHasZ7jN4]

### 1.3 Free work → plaćeno (beta / trade / charity model)
- **Uslovi za besplatan rad:** "I'm gonna do all this stuff for free as long as you promise to do three things: one, you actually use my service; two, you give me feedback on it; and three, leave a killer review if you think it deserves one."
- **Beta faza:** "Hey, I'm making this trade. You're going to get [served]. It's probably not going to be that good. My only ask is that you give me feedback. Tell me how I can make this right. Tell me how I can make it better." [HxEQCHpZzHk]
- "I plan on going to this price point, but I'm willing to do it for free in exchange for X." + "If I do a good job, would you be willing to do these two things?" — *free-in-exchange za feedback + javni testimonijal* [reisEL_D7xc]
- **Prva ponuda ikad (charity model):** "Just donate to the charity of your choice, whatever charity you want to — in exchange, I will then train you for 12 weeks." → kasnije: "Would you guys be willing to pay me now after we finish this 12 weeks?" [reisEL_D7xc]
- **Prelaz free → paid:** "I am accepting somebody who's paying full [price] in order to do this. If you would like me to keep doing this for you, you would have to start paying to match them. Are you comfortable with that?" → ako ne: "No worries — can I get the killer review I asked you for?"
- **Prelaz sa charity modela na naplatu:** "Hey, you know how you paid for weeks to this charity? Want to pay me instead — whatever you paid them." [HsQeQM1jUeg]
- **Free-trial → paid:** "Well, I still want leads." — "Yeah, but now you can pay for them."
- "I only make money if I make sales, and I'll front the cash for the marketing." — *deal za besplatne turnaround-e* [reisEL_D7xc]
- **Na prigovor "ne prodaj vreme besplatno":** "You suck, you have never done this before, so it would be unreasonable for you to be good. Why would you want to sell sucky service to people?"

### 1.4 Hidden-cost / done-for-you outreach
- "I want to do short form videos for you, you don't have to do anything. I will take all the stuff you already have online and I will make them for you and I'll even post them for you — you actually just need to say yes." — *hidden-costs ponuda*
- "I'll send you a free box of meat if you just talk about it." — *cold outreach influenceru bez budžeta*
- "I'm out that way all the time so it's no big deal — I'm happy to stop by so we can meet one another and see if there's an opportunity that makes sense." — *ključna fraza je "I'm out that way all the time"*
- "I can't afford NOT to live here, because I get to meet people like you." — *network arbitraža* [ovL6Z5z0jxQ]
- "Hey, do you want to save 50% today by joining our text list?" — *lead capture na kasi* [xZ8d9g6BcKM]

### 1.5 Regrutovanje / preotimanje ljudi
- "Hey, you're currently an executive here. Would you like to own a slice of my thing?" — *"I'm happy to own 70% and get three of the best guys over"* [KQuyQpFANpA]
- **Senior hire pitch:** "I know that you built two outbound teams for similar type products as me. I need you to do the same thing here. Here's some really favorable upside potential for you, and I need you to build it from here, and I'll give you all the leeway in the world." [okA9Yt2KZuk]

## 2. Partnerstva, afilijati, influenseri, dobavljači, agencije

### 2.1 Influenser / brand partner
- **"The Magic Message" (DM ka influenseru):** "Hey — longtime fan, first-time messenger. I own a company that partners with influencers to start communities. Based on your following, it looks like you generate anywhere from X to Y per month in recurring income. Here are screenshots of people who started this month alone. You get the majority of the profits, we do the majority of the work. Would you be up for a 15-minute call for me to walk you through what something like this would look like?" [Z2tgZC_XkT4]
- **Pitch sa istraživanjem publike:** "I love this stuff about your brand. I think this would fit in in this way... I talked to a bunch of my friends who also follow you... And I think that if we did this, here's an estimate — if we convert 1% of your audience over, it's this much a month." [IFpHasZ7jN4]
- **Underdog / "ja sam mali ali sam samo tvoj" pitch:** "Listen, I started this with the intention of not having lots of customers. I want to find a partner and I'm going to devote everything I have to just this. Anybody else is going to split you and you're going to be an account to them, you'll get a rep. But with me it's just me, and I'm going to build this whole thing myself. You'll have my cell phone, we'll talk every day, we can do whatever you want." [IFpHasZ7jN4]
- **Ista logika, druga verzija:** "These companies don't care about you. You're just a number to him… you're going to get assigned to some account rep who clocks in every day. But with me — you're my only brand partner, you have my full attention. I don't want 100 partners, I just want one good one I can go all in on. You have my cell phone, you can come to my house, I'll cook you dinner." [Z2tgZC_XkT4]
- **Pitch kao lider (obrnuta pozicija):** "We have the best practices across a lot of accounts and I can feed them directly to you… Do you want to trust your brand to someone who's just getting into the industry, or someone with a track record? Do you want to risk what you worked so hard for on someone who might not even be here in six months?" [Z2tgZC_XkT4]
- **Anti-skripta (kako NE):** "Yeah, I was thinking maybe we like partner up on this thing, like you have this value and I have nothing, like this should be a great deal, right?" [IFpHasZ7jN4]
- **Trojan horse influenceru:** [tražiš recept] → "We'd love to include that in our box." → "By the way, what's Butcher Box?" → "Perfect, let's talk."
- **Rev-share bez kapitala:** "We don't have any money, we can't pay you upfront… What we can do is we'll pay you $10 or $15 per subscriber for every month that they remain a subscriber."
- **Projekcija close:** "Within 12 months we should be able to get to here. Does that sound interesting?" — a ako kažu ne, **pitaj zašto.** [Z2tgZC_XkT4]
- **Whitelisting ad (partner glasom):** "Hey, I just partnered with so-and-so. Their thing is awesome — I've actually been using it for years, finally came to an agreement, I think you guys might like it. Check it out." [oRqnTOT9ZG8]
- **Amazon affiliate ton:** "Hey, I was reading this really cool book, I think you'd like it, I've got a link here" [wR8KoE8u1p0]
- **Traženje intro-a od mreže:** "Do you know XXX person? Or do you know anyone who has that size audience, or who caters to this type of people? … I'm paying XXX for an intro."

### 2.2 Lokalna / B2B2C partnerstva
- **Korporaciji (masaže):** "Hey, Mr. Corporation, I want to give each of your new employees two free massages on the house. Here's a coupon. You can give it to them in their new employee packet." → argument partneru: "your employees will like you more, have a better onboarding experience… and you'll be perceived as a more caring employer."
- **Hiropraktičaru:** "Hey, some of your people get their backs cracked and they probably need someone to massage. Anybody who gets their back cracked with you, I'll give them a massage on the house."
- **Affiliate ponuda sa cenovnim pragom:** "Hey man, why don't you sell all your customers three massages — and you can sell it for whatever you want. You keep all the money. It's just got to be at least [$300], so I know it's the right people." [FMzKk73iUhw]
- **Brokerage pitch (uživo, sa krofnama):** "I set up Kelly — was that a good experience, Kelly? Great. I can give every single one of your agents VIP upgrades for all of our moves, for free. That's on me, just because she was so great to work with." + "I'll do a brokerage-level deal for you. We'll give everyone who's an agent of yours VIP for free, if we can just be the first people you call when you have a move. Can you integrate us into your packet? I want to be the only one on the list." [zzleYxkf39k]
- **Šta partner kaže SVOM klijentu — to je pravi proizvod koji mu prodaješ:** "My guy — I've got an amazing moving company, they give all my customers free upgrades." [zzleYxkf39k]
- **DM/SMS ka partnerima:** "Do you know anyone? We have a realtor special, which is a VIP upgrade." [zzleYxkf39k]
- **Event organizatoru — pristup email listi:** "Hey, I don't want to get your email list, that's your own thing. But if after the event I can just say 'if you had a great time at the event...' — I'll happily pay you a fee on anybody who decides to book through us." [mRlSb0O5QNU]
- **Pregovaranje ustupaka sa visoke ankor cene:** "I just quoted you 90 — but if you have five sponsors, do you have any issues with having a sixth? I'll pay you your sponsor fees, 5,000 a booth. It's at 95 now. How's that?" [mRlSb0O5QNU]
- **Kupovina speaking slota:** "If you'll let me just do an intermission — I'm talking 10 minutes, I'll just talk about how events work, we can frame it as value additive — I'll knock another 5,000 off." [mRlSb0O5QNU]
- **Ekskluziva kod velikih partnera / kupovina firme:** "I will sell you my product at a lower cost than other people" / "I'll add in a whole retail kit that'll move more product for you" / "I'll come out to your site and train your staff for free." [Lc8DNduiwKA]
- **Dobavljaču:** "Hey, if I paid you 20% more, do you think you'd put my stuff first?"
- "It would mean the world to me if you shared this event with your audience."
- "Click my link so that our community can have the prize."

### 2.3 Upravljanje afilijatima
- **TL;DR:** "Provide value to them just like you would a customer. Give them stats of where they're at. Be consistent with whatever promises you make. Be upfront about the expectations you have of them and what expectations they can have of you — and then stick to those."
- **Ka top performerima:** "Hey, how can we help you promote it and crush it even more? We want you to win. Because if you win and your audience wins, we all win." [DaRXece2ItE]
- **Reaktivacija prošlogodišnjih afilijata:** "Hey, you did this. Want to do it again? If you liked how it was last time, and you liked having the behind the scenes, you liked the bonuses, you liked competing for stuff… do you want to do it again? Let's run it back." [DaRXece2ItE]
- **Thank-you page CTA:** "Hey, do you want to send anybody else, or you have an audience? Would you like to get more free prizes?" [DaRXece2ItE]
- **Compliance u jednoj rečenici:** "Don't lie. Don't exaggerate. Just state the facts, tell the truth, and everyone will be fine."
- **Upozorenje o tonu prema afilijatima:** "Don't be like 'Oh man, we're going to sell a shitload of books here.' You can't say that — because your customers are like, 'That's how he sees me.'"

### 2.4 Agencija kao škola (insourcing) i ugovori
- **Agency-as-school — najvrednija skripta, zatvara se no-based pitanjem:** "I want to do what you do in my business, but I don't know how. I'd like to work with you for six months so I can learn how you do it, plus I'll pay extra for you to break down why you make the decisions you do and the steps you take to make them. Then after I get a good idea of how it all works, I'll start training my team on it, and once they can do it well enough, I'd like to change to a lower cost consulting agreement — this way you can still help us if we run into problems. **Are you opposed to this?**" [fd-hi3NqMYo]
- **Varijanta iz Q&A:** "I want to steal everything that you have, but I will do it over a longer period of time because it'll take me time to learn — and I will compensate you for the difference. Your normal clients churn out at month three or four; it'll probably take me longer than that, so you'll pay more — I'll pay more. And then after I absorb what you're doing, I'll pay you a consulting period after that." [rc7cxL7ql7Y]
- **Filter za agenciju:** "If I don't see results in 30 days, I'm out. But if I do, I'll keep paying you for a very long time." [fd-hi3NqMYo]
- **Dijagnostičko pitanje za agenciju:** "Of all of the companies that you've ever worked with, which of them made the most money off of [what you deliver]?" [KQuyQpFANpA]
- **Data-contingency klauzula:** "You have to do it in order to work with us. We have a proprietary technology and it feeds off of data, and in order for us to make your solution better we have to get this data so we can implement in real time. Right now we start with the vanilla version, but in two years this is going to be the super tailored version to your university. We know all universities benefit from these five things, but there's usually five to ten other things that we will learn from this data. So you have to do it. And these are the three things that we'll do to make it easier for you to get this data back to us so we can help you make more money."

## 3. Discovery / kvalifikacija (CLOSER: Clarify – Label – Overview)

### 3.1 C — Clarify (zašto su ovde)
- "Why did you reach out today? What's the goal? Why is that important?"
- "Why'd you book this call today? Why not 6 months from now? Why not 6 months ago?" — *"C" iz CLOSER-a* [mRlSb0O5QNU]
- "So I know you wanted some help with the sales thing. I know [we] provided some value to you... So why did you decide to take all this time? What's important to you about this? Why are you not getting what you want? Why'd you even take the time to show up on this call?" [okA9Yt2KZuk]
- "What made you come in today? / What made you reach out? / What's your goal right now? / Why is that important to you? / Why that number specifically?" [oi7bnS8uyJM, q32-l3Yoqg4]
- "Why are you here? / What are you struggling with? / Where are you at right now? / Where are you trying to go? / What have you tried so far? / How's that working for you? / Why is this important — does it matter within the bigger context of your life and what you're trying to do?" [CojS0DwflXc]
- "Where are you now? Where would you like to be? What's the obstacle?" — *jezgro dijagnostičke prodaje*
- **Otključavanje pravog dream outcome-a:** "Why is that? Why is ten thousand dollars the number? Why is that meaningful for you? Why not twenty-eight thousand, why not five thousand? Is there something as a result of having ten thousand dollars that is going to change in your life?" [neTSqOAMgao]
- **Pre-sale questionnaire:** "What brought you in today?" / "What's the current problem you're dealing with?" / "How long have you been dealing with it?" / "If you had to quantify how much this has cost you financially, what would it be?" / "If it continued for five years, how much worse would the situation be?" [1UhvBSQFy6A]
- **Samo-dijagnoza na skali:** "If I show this chart of faces and filler densities — where do you see yourself on here currently?" pa "And where would you like to be?"

### 3.2 Kad kaže "samo hoću informacije"
- "Oh, so you're finding out — do you do this all the time? Do you go to lots of different programs to find out information, or is there a problem you're trying to solve?" [NcD2t9qt-fM]
- "You just get information all day. You're trying to solve something with the information." [okA9Yt2KZuk]
- "I'm assuming you're not hopping on sales calls all day just for fun looking for information. Is there a problem you're trying to solve?" [okA9Yt2KZuk]
- "Cool — but why? What did you want the information to solve? I'm sure you don't just go around collecting information all day, right? There's something you're trying to get out of this. What's the problem?" [q32-l3Yoqg4]

### 3.3 L — Label (restatement / potvrda)
- "Just to make sure I'm clear, you are here, you want to get here, and this has been the issue that's been holding you back."
- "Okay, so I hear what you're saying. This is the reason you showed up for the call. So I just want to be clear that if we achieve this outcome for you, you would be happy, right? You would be like, 'You guys rock, I'd love you.' You can imagine that? Okay, great." [okA9Yt2KZuk]
- "What I'm hearing is you've done X, Y and Z, and the missing link has been ___. Is that right?" [bgmh7p1s7zs]
- "So what I'm hearing is: this is the primary issue that you've been trying to solve, this is what you've done so far, and this is why it hasn't worked. Does that sound about right?" [CojS0DwflXc]
- "So what I'm hearing is you're struggling with this — does that sound about right?" / "Just so I'm hearing you right — it sounds like this is your goal, correct?" [q32-l3Yoqg4]
- "So what I'm hearing is X, Y, Z — does that sound about right?" [oi7bnS8uyJM]

### 3.4 O — Overview past pain (pain cycle)
- "What have you tried so far? / How long did you do that for? / How long ago? / How did that work out for you? / What else have you tried?" [q32-l3Yoqg4]
- "So you've wanted this for however long. And what have you done so far to try? Because you seem like a pretty well-to-do person, you seem like you've got some work ethic, so I'm sure this isn't the first phone call you've hopped on." → "Got it. How'd that work for you? What was good? What was bad?" → "What else have you done?" (u petlji) → "God, I can totally understand why you'd be frustrated at this point, because it seems like you've done a lot." [okA9Yt2KZuk]
- "I'm assuming I'm not the first person you've come to to solve this. So what have you done in the past? ... Why did that not work for you?" [bgmh7p1s7zs, oi7bnS8uyJM]
- "I'm assuming I'm not the first guy you've ever dated, right? So is there anything else that's happened in the past that got you here that didn't work?" [oi7bnS8uyJM]
- "I can't be the first person to talk to you about this. Tell me a little bit about what you've done so far to try and get past this. Tell me more about that — how'd that work out for you?" [q32-l3Yoqg4]
- **Prelaz nazad na skriptu kad prospekt odluta:** "Thank you so much for sharing that, that must have been so hard for you. So what do you think that brought you...?" [oi7bnS8uyJM]
- **"Six inches away":** "Dude, you've got all these right things in place. You're so close. Gosh, I feel like you're this close to hitting your goal." [okA9Yt2KZuk]
- **Zatvaranje pain cycle-a:** "I can't imagine what that's felt like. It's not your fault — you're so close, you're six inches away. And I think you've had this one thing missing which I'm about to show you. Want to hear about the program?" [oi7bnS8uyJM]

### 3.5 Kvalifikacija (BANT, budžet, veličina, autoritet)
- **BANT, doslovno** [3yAiVjcImQ4]:
  - Budget: "Can you afford $5,800 in order to get your business incorporated, your IP protected?"
  - Authority: "Is there anyone else who's involved in this decision?"
  - Need: "What happens if you don't solve this?"
  - Timing: "When would you like to get this done / when would you like to start?" (drop-down: today / tomorrow / a few weeks from now)
- "Are you willing to spend at least $[pola cene] on this?" — *kvalifikaciono pitanje u aplikaciji* [Kk6Vdfdwmxw]
- "Do you host events, and how many per year?" — *kvalifikacija ugrađena u opt-in formu* [mRlSb0O5QNU]
- **Multiplikaciono pitanje (tiket $80k → $240k):** "How many events does your organization do every year?" → "Why don't we do all three? I'll commit to all three for you. We'll do the first one, and assuming the first one goes great, we'll book the other two at the same rate." [mRlSb0O5QNU]
- "Do you need more customers, or can you not handle the stuff you got?" — *supply vs. demand constraint* [jqo0lVveh98]
- "How much time can you dedicate to this implementation?" — *pitanje koje unapred pravi zamku za kasnije "nemam vremena"*
